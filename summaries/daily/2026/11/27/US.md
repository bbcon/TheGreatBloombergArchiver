**Equity Markets**
US equity futures rallied sharply on the Friday after Thanksgiving, led by AI-adjacent names — CoreWeave gained 7%, Micron 7.6%, and optical component maker Coherent 8.9%. The move appears technically driven: analysts note many sectors had reached oversold levels following the Nasdaq 100's 17% decline from early October highs, itself the product of a multi-week reassessment of AI earnings expectations. Whether this constitutes a durable inflection or a relief rally within a broader corrective phase remains genuinely uncertain.

**Monetary Policy and Dollar**
The dollar's sustained appreciation reflects the intersection of resilient US growth, still-elevated inflation, and elevated rate expectations. Market commentary suggests the Fed may need to tighten further — potentially at both the December and January meetings — reinforcing dollar strength into 2027 as AI-driven capital expenditure continues to attract global investment flows into US assets. The 10-year Treasury yield rose six basis points to approximately 4.91%, consistent with that rate outlook.
