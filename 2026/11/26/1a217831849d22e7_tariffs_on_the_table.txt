voters loudly voiced their cost-of-living worries. Not exactly the optics

Americans are closer to the beginning than the end of rising food costs. 
Prices are alsosurging

 for generators and toilet paper after a series of tech failures spooked the

US consumer spending stalled 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/us-consumer-spending-stalls-core-pce-rises-0-3-for-third-month?cmpid=BBD112626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261126&utm_content=11869>
 in October. The Federal Reserve’s preferred measure of underlying inflation 
was up 3.3% from a year earlier, the most since 2023.

On the positive side, economic growth showed a sharp upward revision in the 
third quarter and business investment remains broadly strong, with data centers 
and electricity generation the key drivers.

For Democrats, the data is backward-looking and lauding it while adding
