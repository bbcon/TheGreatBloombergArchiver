Little shows how much China’s relationship with global capital has changed — 
from both sides — than thefundraising efforts 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/alibaba-to-raise-10-billion-by-selling-shares-for-ai-expansion?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 of Alibaba and Shein.

In 2014, Alibaba chose New York when it raised $21.8 billion in its 
record-breaking IPO. For years Chinese tech firms had flocked to the US, drawn 
by a friendly regulatory environment and a vast pool of capital.

Not this time. The tech giant picked Hong Kong to raise HK$80 billion ($10 
billion) in what is the city’s biggest secondary share sale. Despite the 
massive amount raised, the company isa shadow of 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/china-misses-out-on-ai-boom-as-stocks-trail-by-most-since-2001?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 its former self in terms of value: its Hong Kong shares trade more than 60% 
below their 2020 peak after being caught up in Beijing’s regulatory crackdown 
that year.

Alibaba is shifting its focus 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/alibaba-to-sell-gaming-arm-for-1-5-billion-in-boost-to-ai-pivot?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 from e-commerce to AI — hence the need to raise so much capital. Some 
investors didn’t like the news: its shares tumbled as much as 10% on Monday. 
Michael Burry, ofThe Big Short fame, said he recently exited 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/burry-says-he-sold-alibaba-calling-it-pricey-before-share-sale?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 his position in Alibaba, adding he expects return on invested capital from the 
company to continue declining.

Shein, meanwhile, said it’s seeking to raise 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/shein-seeks-up-to-1-8-billion-in-long-awaited-hong-kong-ipo?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 as much as HK$13.9 billion in its Hong Kong IPO. The fast-fashion retailer had 
itsown odyssey 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-28/shein-is-said-to-weigh-hong-kong-as-ipo-venue-instead-of-london?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 getting to market, with regulatory scrutiny from both Washington and Beijing 
derailing Shein’s attempts to list in the US and then London. The debut will 
give it a market capitalization of up to $27 billion, vastly less than the 
almost $100 billion the firm commanded in 2022.

China’s champions are still able to raise serious funds, in a win for Hong Kong
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-05/hong-kong-draws-back-expats-seeking-low-taxes-finance-jobs-after-covid-exodus?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
. But it’s come at a cost to valuations.

Hedge funds are ramping up bearish dollar bets as they await more details on 
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s new fiscal plan to address the highest 
borrowing costs in years.

That pressure was evident 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/hedge-funds-ramp-up-dollar-shorts-ahead-of-bessent-s-fiscal-plan?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 on Friday, when the dollar extended a drop triggered by Bessent’s Aug. 19 
decision to increase the size of buybacks for longer-dated securities “by at 
least double.” The announcement sparked the dollar’s worst single-day decline 
in nearly three weeks and triggered a wave of selling across the cash market. 
The dollar was slightly higher on Monday.

Gold has been a beneficiary 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/gold-holds-near-three-month-high-as-debasement-trade-returns?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 of doubts over the dollar. It rose as much as 1.2% to above $4,650 an ounce, 
the highest intraday level since mid-May. Bessent’s drive to get borrowing 
costs down, especially with November’s midterm election looming, is running 
into forcesbeyond his control 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/bessent-has-no-easy-fix-for-what-s-really-driving-bond-yields-up?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 that arepushing them up 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-18/the-bond-market-is-sending-a-warning-with-rising-long-term-debt-costs?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
, such as record debt levels, a surge in corporate issuance and higher 
inflation.

Singapore announced a package of childcare benefits to support families as the 
city-state grapples with a record-low birth rate.

Each Singaporean child 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/singapore-unveils-sweeping-family-support-as-birth-rates-tumble?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 will receive almost S$70,000 ($55,150) in direct assistance through age 17, 
starting with a S$10,000 baby gift, Prime Minister Lawrence Wong said during 
his National Day Rally Speech on Sunday. The government will also significantly 
increase paid childcare leave and aims to cut monthly fees for subsidized 
full-day childcare to S$150.

Hong Kong too is struggling to encourage people to have children. Births 
plunged 15.6% to 29,700 in the 12 months through June, provisional data showed,
the lowest since

Canada Sees Long Trade War With US That May Last Beyond Midterms

Prime Minister Mark Carney is designing a domestic aid package to help 
Canadian businesses hurt by US tariffs that can ride out the balance of 
President Donald Trump’s term if necessary, people familiar with the matter 
said.
Read more 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/canada-sees-long-trade-war-with-us-that-may-last-beyond-midterms?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
China’s nationalist tabloid Global Times praised Canada’s retaliation against 
US tariffs, casting Ottawa’s move as validating Beijing’s opposition to 
Washington’s protectionist trade policies.

Calling Canada’s action a “Chinese-style counterattack 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/china-media-hails-canada-s-chinese-style-tariff-retaliation?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
,” the newspaper said in an editorial that the government’s decision shows that 
“more countries are realizing that concessions toward the US do not necessarily 
bring respect and stability.”

The Global Times cited US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer’s comparison of 
Canada’s measures to “the kind of things that China would do.” That remark 
amounted to an acknowledgment of “the failure of US policy,” the newspaper said.

Still, Carney’s move will come with a cost 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/canada-turned-down-a-us-tariff-deal-now-comes-the-economic-cost?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
. The US implemented a new 50% tax on imports of hundreds of Canadian items 
including furniture, plastics, plywood and electrical equipment on Saturday. 
University of Calgary economics professor Trevor Tombe estimated that 90,000 
jobs — about 0.4% of Canada’s labor force — may be lost if the new tariffs 
persist.

Iran’s oil shipments to Asia have all but dried up, driving the cost of those 
cargoes to thehighest levels in years 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/iran-oil-cargoes-dry-up-even-before-us-acts-on-tehran-threats?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 even before the US administration announcesfresh moves 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bessent-to-detail-us-plans-to-isolate-iran-s-economy-on-monday?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260824&utm_content=8104>
 to isolate Tehran and its trading partners.

China’s private refiners have long taken the lion’s share of Iran’s oil. But 
with little supply available in the region over recent weeks, prices on offer 
have flipped from a discount to global benchmarks to a premium of around $4 a 
barrel, according to traders involved in negotiations. Much of this scarcity is 
down to the success of a US blockade, which has left loaded Iranian vessels 
trapped inside the Persian Gulf and a fleet of empty ones stuck outside.
