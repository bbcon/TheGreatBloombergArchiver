**Japan Fiscal and Monetary Dynamics**
Japan presents a notable tension: Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi has explored expanding household subsidies to offset the effects of elevated inflation, even as the Bank of Japan faces mounting criticism for being slow to restore price stability. This week's large ministerial spending requests reinforce the view that fiscal consolidation is not a near-term political priority. The combination of accommodative fiscal policy and a central bank still navigating normalization keeps Japan among the developed economies where the gap between policy settings and inflation realities remains wide.

**Nepal Climate Disaster**
The scale of the Himalayan glacier collapse on August 26 was described by climate scientists as outside prior experience. A glacier section measuring between 1 and 1.3 kilometers wide detached near Langtang Lirung peak, triggering a debris flow that raised river levels by up to 9 meters within 30 minutes and swept through 100 kilometers of valley. Scientists note that the frequency of such events has increased markedly since 2021, suggesting the region faces structurally elevated disaster risk as permafrost thaw accelerates at altitude.
