**Central Bank Divergence**
Asian policy is tilting hawkish even as regional bond markets draw support from Washington's bond-buyback program, with sovereign debt in Japan and New Zealand advancing alongside Treasuries. Australia's inflation surprise adds to hawkish signals ahead of Japan's expected September hike. Thailand's central bank, by contrast, held its rate steady for a third meeting, while Indonesia's incoming governor pledged to preserve independence.

**China Corporate Pressure**
Beyond the tax clawback, investor unease over Alibaba's $10 billion Hong Kong share sale is persisting despite co-founder Jack Ma and senior executives buying stock; shares remain down almost 6% from before the deal. Existing Shein investors have agreed to a six-month lock-up on new IPO shares to signal confidence. Student protests over exam-system changes continued in Bihar, India, a month after similar unrest forced the education minister's resignation.
