**Monetary Policy**
The FOMC meeting on Wednesday has become genuinely uncertain. Brent crude briefly exceeded $100/bbl last week before this weekend's ceasefire pause sent it below $90 on Monday morning, temporarily reducing the urgency for a hike. Markets are pricing roughly a one-in-three chance of an increase — a dramatic repricing from earlier this month. The tension is real: several Fed officials had already set a low bar for additional tightening, and jobless claims at their lowest since 1969 suggests the labor market offers no cover for easing. The oil price whipsaw complicates rather than resolves the calculus, with the Fed likely to hold while it assesses whether the Middle East pause proves durable.

**AI and Equity Markets**
A notable pivot appears underway in the market's attitude toward AI capital expenditure. Despite overall earnings remaining strong, hyperscaler shares have gone essentially nowhere for 12 months, with gains having rotated into semiconductor names. Alphabet's beat last week was met with selling after it signaled further capex acceleration, dragging both hyperscalers and chip suppliers lower. This week's reports from Apple, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft will be closely watched for any signs of capex moderation. It is too early to declare the AI trade exhausted, but the market is increasingly asking whether deployment economics will justify the scale of investment.
