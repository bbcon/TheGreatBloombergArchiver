**Peru Political Transition**
Fujimori's inauguration marks a significant moment for Peru's political economy, though the structural fragility of the country's institutions remains unchanged. The sheer turnover rate of heads of state — nine in a decade — continues to deter long-term investment and complicates policy continuity, particularly in the mining and energy sectors where Peru holds substantial resource wealth. Investors will monitor her early cabinet and fiscal appointments closely.

**Argentina and the IMF**
Georgieva's visit signals cautious institutional confidence in Milei's stabilization program. Argentina has moved from chronic IMF tension to a more cooperative posture, but the program's real test — debt repayment — is now imminent. The IMF chief's public optimism provides political cover domestically but does not reduce the fundamental challenge of rebuilding reserves while maintaining fiscal adjustment. The trajectory of commodity prices, particularly given energy market disruption from the Iran conflict, will influence export revenues and the program's arithmetic.
