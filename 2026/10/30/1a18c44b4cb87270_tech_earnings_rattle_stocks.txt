BOJ’s Ueda to Mull Rate Hike at Each Meet Including December

Bank of Japan Governor Kazuo Ueda said he’ll keep judging the need for a rate 
hike at each meeting including the next session in December. Ueda took the 
first opportunity to sound hawkish about future moves at a press briefing, 
hours after the Japanese central bank kept its benchmark rate unchanged at 
1.25% as widely expected. The yen strengthened.

China’s biggest banks reported their best quarterly profit increases 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/china-s-mega-banks-report-profit-jumps-as-margins-recover?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261030&utm_content=10778>
 in recent years as they reined in bad loans and interest margins recovered 
from record lows. ICBC, the nation’s largest lender, reported a 10% jump in 
profit in the third quarter, while Agricultural Bank of China and Bank of 
Communications also reported double-digit gains. The results suggest the worst 
of the profitability crunch may be behind for the state banks, which have spent 
years absorbing the financial costs of supporting a slowing economy and a real 
estate downturn.

France’s economy unexpectedly contracted 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/french-economy-unexpectedly-shrinks-as-headwinds-gain-strength?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261030&utm_content=10778>
 in the third quarter, while inflation neared a three-year high, adding to 
headaches that already include surging borrowing costs, violent protests and 
political and budgetary uncertainty. GDP fell 0.1% from the previous three 
months as investment continued to decline and exports dropped. Analysts had 
estimated expansion of 0.1%. Energy costs drove inflation to 3.8% in October, 
the highest level since late 2023.
