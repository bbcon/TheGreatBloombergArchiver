**Brazil's Election Economy**
Lula's advantage owes as much to Trump's aggressive regional posture as to the domestic economy: unemployment is low and inflation contained, but voters remain unconvinced by his record on security, the top-ranked concern heading into the vote. A win would make him Brazil's longest-serving president of the democratic era.

**Venezuela and Trade**
Venezuela's push to re-attract US oil capital reflects both genuine investor appetite — shale veterans call the reserve base underexploited — and the country's poor historical record on contract enforcement, a tension that will determine whether the current wave of deals sustains momentum.
