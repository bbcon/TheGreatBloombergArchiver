A warning on deficits

The bond market’s message to Scott Bessent couldn’t be clearer: If you want 
financing for the growing US deficit,you’re going to have to pay up 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/us-braces-for-30-year-bond-auction-at-highest-yield-since-2001?cmpid=081426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7691>
.

The US sold $25 billion of 30-year bonds yesterday at 5.216%, the highest 
interest rate in a quarter century.The sale, which was met with decent demand, 
follows the 10-year auction a day earlier that drew the highest financing cost 
at that tenor since 2007.

It’s a headache for Donald Trump and Bessent, his Treasury secretary, before 
the midterm elections in November. Lofty government financing costs are already 
feeding through to the broader economy, after years of elevated inflation and 
government spending.

“Investors are being asked to absorb a growing supply of government debt 
globally,” said Michal Stanczyk at Allspring Global Investments. “If investors 
continue demanding greater compensation for inflation and fiscal risks, 
long-term yields could move higher and away from 5% even if Treasury auctions 
remain well covered.” —Greg Ritchie 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUJMWGi29pc/greg-ritchie?cmpid=081426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7691>

Crypto’s ‘bear market’

Crypto exchanges are discovering that it no longer pays to just provide a 
venue for trading crypto 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/crypto-s-wall-street-era-arrives-as-retail-buzz-liquidity-fade?cmpid=081426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7691>
.

Three crypto exchanges, including BitMEX, went out of business this summer. 
Gracy Chen of Bitget says liquidity in altcoins has fallen sharply and the 
downturn increasingly reminds her of 2022.

“For nearly a year now, liquidity is not coming back, and I don’t see it 
coming back,” she said. “That’s why we don’t want to be just a crypto exchange.”

While institutions are embracing crypto, they’re doing so via payments, 
collateral and real-world assets tied to blockchain rather than the broad 
speculation that powered previous booms.

So, platforms like Bitget — the sixth-biggest crypto exchange by trading 
volume — are pursuing the “everything exchange” strategy, offering everything 
from stocks to commodities. “That’s how we can survive the current bear 
market,” Chen said. —Olga Kharif 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/APyzq5YvgSE/olga-kharif?cmpid=081426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7691>
