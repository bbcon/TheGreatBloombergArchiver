**Mexico and Regional Growth**
Mexico's third-quarter GDP print came in above consensus, with the acceleration driven by both investment and export strength — the latter benefiting from nearshoring dynamics that have intensified since US tariff policy reshaped North American supply chains. Canada separately reported 1.3% annualized growth in Q3, slowing from a robust Q2 but remaining positive. Trump indicated a US-Canada trade deal is "very close" following talks with Prime Minister Carney, though Carney signaled Canada will not rush an agreement on unfavorable terms.

**El Niño and Credit Risk**
A developing super El Niño cycle is beginning to translate into measurable credit risk across Latin American loan books. BBVA, with significant exposure in Peru, Argentina, and Colombia, has initiated formal loan book reviews at the request of central risk management, with debt rescheduling identified as one potential tool for the most vulnerable borrowers. The World Meteorological Organization has flagged heightened drought and flood risks across Latin America. The phenomenon is compounding existing inflationary pressure from energy costs, with commodity supply disruptions carrying pass-through implications for food prices regionally.
