**Venezuela Oil Deal**

US Energy Secretary Chris Wright's visit to Caracas formalises an arrangement that the Trump administration is framing as a pre-midterm political win and a tool to suppress domestic gasoline prices. The strategic logic is that expectations of future Venezuelan supply will exert downward pressure on prices even before production ramps up materially. Venezuela's acting president Rodríguez has characterised the deal as sovereignty-preserving, though opposition figures question whether an interim government possesses the legitimacy to make long-term commitments over the country's primary resource. The deal may, paradoxically, reinforce Rodríguez's political position by giving Washington a stake in her continuity.

**Brazil**

The diesel crunch compounds seasonal cost pressures for Brazilian agriculture at a critical moment. Whether higher farmer input costs translate into sustained food-price inflation domestically and globally remains to be seen, but the timing — coinciding with a global bond selloff driven partly by food and energy price concerns — is consequential.
