**Argentina Sovereign Trajectory**
Three Moody's upgrades in under three months reflect growing external creditor conviction that Milei's fiscal consolidation and deregulation agenda has durability beyond initial skepticism. The pace of rehabilitation is notable: the upgrade sequence suggests markets are increasingly willing to price in policy adherence rather than treating reform as inherently fragile. Whether improved credit metrics translate into meaningful investment inflows and sustained disinflation — Argentina's central test — remains the key open question for the economic outlook.

**Brazil Political Risk and Regional Dynamics**
The Bolsonaro campaign's operational difficulties are accumulating at a sensitive point in the electoral cycle. New allegations linking the candidate's law firm to Banco Master funding, combined with a fresh police investigation and strained family political dynamics, increase execution risk for what had been a credible opposition candidacy ahead of October. Brazilian assets remain sensitive to further developments, as political uncertainty typically translates into spread widening in a market already attentive to fiscal dynamics. The broader regional backdrop is shaped by elevated global oil prices — a net positive for Colombia and Brazil as producers but a headwind for oil-importing economies — and by the stronger dollar environment, which is compressing export margins for coffee and banana producers concentrated in Colombia, Ecuador, and Central America. Mexico's USMCA review timeline, if confirmed by its top diplomat in Washington, would provide a meaningful near-term anchor for trade and investment sentiment, though the growing fleet of Chinese-made trucks in Mexico has complicated tariff-relief negotiations.
