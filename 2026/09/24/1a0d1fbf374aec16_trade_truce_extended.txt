Ninove, Belgium, September 9, 2026.Photographer: Cyril Marcilhacy/Bloomberg
From the outside, the dilapidated warehouse in the nondescript Belgian town of 
Ninove is as unremarkable as the discount retailer in front of it. Only 
brand-new surveillance cameras hint at something valuable inside:a clandestine 
factory 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-09-24/belgium-factory-raid-highlights-fake-cigarette-trade-plaguing-europe?cmpid=092426_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260924&utm_content=9261>
 making as many as a million counterfeit cigarettes a day.

 * As police and customs officers examined the contents of the warehouse 
following a dawn raid on Sept. 9, they found a slick operation from rolling 
machines to flat-packed boxes ready to contain fake Marlboros, Lambert & 
Butlers and Richmond King Size.
 * The counterfeit cigarette industry is proliferating across Europe as rising 
taxes on tobacco products create an increasingly lucrative market for organized 
crime. European Unionestimates suggest 
<https://www.eca.europa.eu/en/publications/SR-2026-23> almost one in 10 
cigarettes smoked in the EU are now produced illegally or smuggled.
 * Belgium, with its proximity to high-duty tobacco markets like the UK and 
France, is at the sharp end of efforts to tackle the growth in illicit 
production. Ninove was the 12th illegal cigarette factory the customs service 
has busted this year, already matching its previous annual record.
