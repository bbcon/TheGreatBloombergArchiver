**UK Growth and Policy**
Thursday's GDP data showed the UK economy outperforming expectations through the first half, with business investment up 1.7% in Q2 and strength in information and communications services seen as an early sign of AI-driven productivity gains. Bloomberg Economics cautioned that underlying momentum is nonetheless weakening, with a new prime minister, tighter household finances, and a loosening labor market pointing to slower growth ahead of the autumn budget.

**Weather and Supply Chains**
Extreme heat and record-low Rhine water levels are straining European industry simultaneously, forcing firms including Lanxess to shift shipments from barge to rail and road. Germany's gas storage sits at a seasonal record low after a sluggish summer refilling pace, raising concerns about supply ahead of winter.
