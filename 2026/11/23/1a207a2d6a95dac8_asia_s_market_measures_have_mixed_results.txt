MSCI Discussed Avoiding Indonesia Downgrade With Prabowo, Dalio

Here’s another example of “don’t give up,” from Indonesia. MSCI Chief 
Executive Henry Fernandez conveyed a willingness to consider alternatives to a 
two-step downgrade of Indonesia’s stock-market status in recent direct talks 
with President Prabowo Subianto, according to people with knowledge of the 
discussion. Fernandez and Prabowo spoke for more than an hour on a Nov. 10 
conference call brokered by Ray Dalio, the billionaire founder of hedge fund 
Bridgewater Associates.
