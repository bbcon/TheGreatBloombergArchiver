Nvidia weighs on stocks

Nvidia’s earnings arrive after the close today at perhaps the strangest time 
possible 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/nvidia-earnings-are-sideshow-as-huang-s-ouster-looms-over-market?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
 for the world’s most important AI chipmaker.

Investors already were questioning the durability of the AI boom. Then the 
Democrats swept to victory in the US midterm elections this month, raising the 
prospect of tighter regulation for the tech industry.

The scandal that led to the ouster of CEO Jensen Huang last week just added to 
theglobal upheaval 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/barclays-says-forced-retail-liquidation-behind-taiwan-stock-rout?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
 in the stock market’s hottest sector. Nvidia shares have plunged 35% from 
their Oct. 6 record high, wiping out $2 trillion in value. A survey by Bank of 
America shows fund managers last weekcut their allocations to tech stocks by 
the most in two decades 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/bofa-survey-shows-investors-cut-tech-exposure-by-most-since-2006?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
.

(One result of Huang’s affair with an alleged Chinese spy: The US yesterday 
imposedtighter restrictions 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/us-moves-to-limit-remote-access-to-advanced-ai-chips?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
 on exports of advanced chips,sending Chinese AI stocks tumbling 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/chip-stocks-slide-as-us-sets-new-curbs-on-china-s-compute-access?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
. Nvidia’s board said today it’s establishing a committee to overseethe 
company’s national security policies 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/nvidia-forms-export-control-board-committee-after-chip-curbs?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>
.)

Nvidia is expected to show strong earnings in its fiscal third quarter with 
healthy growth in revenue. In a normal year, that could serve as a stabilizing 
force for a stock that’s in freefall. But this isn’t an ordinary year.

“It is very important for the market as a whole that Nvidia is still doing 
well, but Nvidia’s stock will still have this weight on it,” said Zed Francis 
at investment advisory firm Convexitas. “It will be very difficult for the 
stock to break new highs until we get more clarity on this issue.” —Ryan

On the move

 * Intel falls 3.3% in premarket trading. Nvidia reduced its stake in the 
chipmaker by roughly 25% last quarter. Nvidia is also cut its holding ofNokia, 
which declines 4.8% in Helsinki.
 * Nvidia is falling 0.2% before its earnings report, in line with Nasdaq 100 
futures in premarket trading.
 * Health insurers are among the top premarket decliners, with Humana, CVS and 
UnitedHealth dropping more than 2% each. Senator Elizabeth Warren called for 
breaking up “big medicine”

Word from Wall Street

“The petrodollar is — rather quickly — being superseded by the chip-dollar. A 
dollar driven by chips, AI and private capital connections should be inherently 
riskier.”

Mallika Sachdeva

Strategist, Deutsche Bank

The dollar’s growing link to US equities 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/deutsche-sees-asia-chip-boom-supporting-dollar-through-equities?cmpid=111726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261117&utm_content=11507>

relationship with Treasuries and the oil business, creating the risk that the 
currency becomes more volatile, Sachdeva says.
