**Colombia's Earthquake Tests a New Government**
The quake, centered in Colombia's coffee region, raises the prospect of broader economic damage beyond the immediate humanitarian toll and struck just days into de la Espriella's term as the first Colombian president sworn in outside Bogotá. His response — including a roughly five-hour delay before addressing the country directly, during which local officials became the public face of the response — will be measured against Venezuela's earthquake response two months earlier, which was criticized as too slow and fed political backlash there. How the government funds reconstruction while pursuing its 40% spending-cut pledge will offer an early signal of whether de la Espriella's campaign commitments to fiscal discipline and smaller government can survive the realities of governing, and will also test whether his political alignment with Donald Trump translates into tangible US support for the recovery effort.
