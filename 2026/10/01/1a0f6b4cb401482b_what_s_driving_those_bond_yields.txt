Let’s talk about those surging bond yields. It’s the latest preoccupation for 
investors, but also worth everyone else paying attention to because ofwhat the 
trend signals about inflation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-30/30-year-yield-s-continuous-rise-fuels-inflation-worry-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
 and borrowing costs.

Bond yields are climbing almost daily around the world as investors sell 
fixed-income assets, likebenchmark US Treasuries 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/treasury-10-year-yield-hits-highest-since-2002-on-rate-outlook-mup7ryq2?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
. Global government bonds justposted their worst quarter since 2024 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-30/global-bonds-face-worst-quarter-since-2024-on-inflation-fears?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
 as $100 oil revives the threat of sticky inflation for the world economy.

That’s sparking a heated debate over the reasons why and how much further they 
have to run. The selloff is taking place against abackdrop of stubborn inflation
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/the-biggest-jump-in-global-crop-prices-since-2022-threatens-to-boost-inflation?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
, fiscal concerns and resilient growth. Which forces are really driving yields 
higher?Bloomberg’s markets reporters identify 10 reasons 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/ten-reasons-investors-are-driving-government-bond-yields-higher?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
.

Meanwhile, countries that moved fast to tackle this year’s inflation spike are 
finding favor with bond investors 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/global-bond-funds-are-rewarding-the-early-movers-on-inflation?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
. Those that delayed may end up paying the price in higher rates.

Indian oil refiners are hiring tankers to sail through the Strait of Hormuz to 
pick up crude from within the Persian Gulf, achange of tactics to help them cut 
costs and secure their supply chains 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/india-oil-refiners-change-tactics-and-hire-ships-to-cross-hormuz?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9569>
. Processors have issued tenders and are negotiating with shipping lines, 
according to people familiar with their efforts. The move marks a shift for 
Indian refiners, who have avoided sending their own tankers through Hormuz 
since early in the US-Iran war due to the risk of attacks. Instead, they relied 
on gulf producers and international traders to shoulder the risk of transiting 
the contested waterway and delivering to India.
