All the AI computing power in the world couldn’t prevent Nvidia chief Jensen 
Huang from making the basest of blunders.

Huang’s admission of an affair 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/nvidia-board-to-probe-spying-case-after-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-infidelity?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
 — when he’d previously said there was nothing inappropriate about the 
relationship in question — has prompted an internal investigation at the 
company which dominates the market for chips used in artificial intelligence.

The fact that the woman was identified in several reports as a suspected 
Chinese spy 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/what-to-know-about-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-s-affair-with-suspected-chinese-spy?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
 means the scandal is rippling through technology, politics and US-China 
relations. Beijing dismissed the allegations.

Huang is among AI’s biggest promoters, appearing frequently alongside 
President Donald Trump and acting as a forceful advocate of the US’s$500 
billion buildout 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-s-500-billion-plan-envelops-wall-street-in-its-ai-frenzy?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
 of infrastructure including data centers.

He supports Trump’s stance against implementing guardrails on a technology 
even leading AI developers have said carries potentially existential risks.
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-09/how-nvidia-ceo-s-affair-engulfed-ai-giant-in-spy-probe-video?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>

That kind of unrestrained cheerleading is now at odds with mainstream US 
opinion, as expressed in last week’s midterms.

Trump is hardly likely to disassociate himself from Huang over an infidelity 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/nvidia-ceo-says-ties-to-company-under-spying-probe-were-normal?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
. It does, however, raise questions over whether the Nvidia CEO can continue to 
helm what became the world most valuable company under his leadership. Nvidia
shares fell today 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/nvidia-shares-dip-on-robinhood-as-huang-affair-spurs-board-probe?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
.

Huang is also a vocal advocate of selling more Nvidia chips to China, acting 
as a bridge 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-newspaper-dismisses-jensen-huang-spy-claim-warns-on-ties?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
 between Washington and Beijing.

It’s a tack with parallels to Trump, who is due to meet President Xi Jinping 
in China next week for their third encounter of the year.

But a Democrat-controlled Congress will surely try to check burgeoning ties 
with the US’smain strategic rival 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/us-china-ai-race-five-key-flashpoints-explained?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
.

In September last year, Huang appeared with the president in the splendor of 
Windsor Castle during a state visit to the UK. Trump quipped then that Huang 
was “taking over the world.”

Huang doesn’t seem so all-powerful now, another indication that the wheels are 
coming off AI’s all-consuming charge. — Alan Crawford

North Korea is testing new weapons systems at twice the pace of Trump’s first 
term, ranging from missiles designed to strike the US and its allies to 
anti-aircraft systems. With the US president preparing for a trip to Asia, 
Pyongyang has carried out four tests in the past two weeks alone, underscoring
how much higher 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/north-korea-s-weapons-tests-surge-to-twice-trump-first-term-pace?cmpid=BBD110926_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11171>
 the bar has become for leader Kim Jong Un to make concessions in any 
negotiations with the US — particularly after the Republican Party’s midterm 
setbacks.
