**India's Caste Census**
Modi's BJP had long resisted a caste count to avoid straining its broad Hindu coalition, but reversed course after losing its parliamentary majority in 2024, seeking broader appeal among disadvantaged communities. A 2011 attempt to collect similar data produced over 4 million caste descriptions too difficult to classify and never released. The new count could reshape the federal bargain: it will inform redistricting frozen for decades, raising concern among wealthier southern states — which have brought fertility below replacement — that population-based reallocation would dilute their influence relative to faster-growing northern states. Opposition parties warn inconsistent enumeration could reduce the exercise to a symbolic one.
