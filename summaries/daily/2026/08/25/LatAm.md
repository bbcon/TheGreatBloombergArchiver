**Fiscal Strain in Mexico, Political Realignment in Colombia**
Mexico's Pemex bailout has pushed its bonds into junk-like trading territory, a credit-market signal that state-linked fiscal risk is being repriced even as Sheinbaum pursues a bilateral trade accommodation with the Trump administration. In Colombia, De la Espriella's immigration and security crackdown marks a rightward political shift following the Petro era, and coincides with a currency rally and yield compression that has rewarded early foreign buyers of local debt such as Pimco — though the durability of that rally amid a broader regional US-tariff backdrop remains to be seen.
