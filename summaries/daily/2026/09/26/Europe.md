**Rhine Disruption and Climate Adaptation**
Persistently low Rhine water levels are compelling shipping operators and industrial firms reliant on Europe's busiest inland waterway to adapt — an increasingly recurring operational constraint with structural implications for German and broader European manufacturing competitiveness. Development work on shallow-draft vessel designs is underway at the Duisburg ship technology center, suggesting industry is beginning to price in low-water conditions as a semi-permanent feature rather than an episodic disruption. The episode illustrates a broader challenge for European industrial policy: climate-related infrastructure constraints are adding a layer of structural cost that monetary or fiscal policy cannot directly address.
