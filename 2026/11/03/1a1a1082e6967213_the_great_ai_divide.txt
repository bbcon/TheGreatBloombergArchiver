Is there an AI bubble and is it going to burst any time soon? Those are the 
questions that have hung over investors since the AI-fueled supercycle started. 
Some of the world’s biggest asset managers and Wall Street’s top CEOswere 
divided 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/wall-street-ceos-big-global-money-managers-debate-ai-risks?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10936>
 on the subject when they spoke at the Global Financial Leaders’ Investment 
Summit in Hong Kong on Tuesday.

PIMCO’s Emmanuel Roman and Bain Capital’s John Connaughton are among a growing 
number of investment managers warning of bubble risks in AI and an eventual 
downturn after the sector’s breakneck expansion. AI-related financing could 
grow to about $10 trillion over the next five years with the amount eventually 
becoming excessive, Roman said. “At some point in time, there’s going to be a 
downturn, and thenthings are going to break 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/pimco-ceo-warns-ai-financing-is-on-path-to-become-too-much?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10936>
,” he added, pointing to anything from rising US interest rates or war in the 
Middle East to a euro-zone crisis as potential triggers.

Wall Street bank bosses however struck a more bullish tone 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/goldman-s-solomon-sees-very-robust-demand-for-ai-deals?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10936>
 as they look to benefit from hyperscalers desperate to raise capital. Goldman 
Sachs’ David Solomon acknowledged there will be bumps in the road but added 
that investors remain focused on the trajectory of AI demand and compute 
demand. Morgan Stanley’s Ted Pick compared it to the early stages of a baseball 
game. “We’re somewhere in the second inning of a nine-inning game. Ten to 15 
trillion — that’s a very large range, but that’s not an unreasonable sum to be 
thinking about to build that, which will push the product forward.”

Iran President Masoud Pezeshkian said his government has been granted more 
authority by the country’s supreme leader to pursue peace talks, as President

unidentified Iranian official. Brent crude rose, after plunging almost 8% in

Disney Plans Thousands of Layoffs, Targeting 20% Cost Savings

Walt Disney Co. is moving toward thousands of job cuts with the entertainment 
giant asking some managers to target 20% in annual cost savings, according to 
people familiar with the matter. Some of the company’s operations, which 
include theme parks, TV channels and the movie studio, will be affected 
disproportionately. CEO Josh D’Amaro aims to reinvest a portion of the savings 
into film and TV programming, technology and the theme parks and cruise ship 
businesses, the people said.
