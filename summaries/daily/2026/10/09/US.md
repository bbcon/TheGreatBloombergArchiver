**Equity Markets**
US equity futures recovered Friday after the S&P 500 fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq 100 posted its worst session in over seven weeks on Thursday, when initial OpenAI revenue reports disappointed. Subsequent clarification — annualized run-rate of roughly $50 billion at end-September, tracking to $70 billion or above by December — appeared sufficient to stabilize sentiment. Humana surged 13% in premarket after favorable Medicare plan ratings signaled a revenue uplift, while Apple fell 2.1% on reports of reduced iPhone 18 Pro component orders. Treasuries held gains from the prior session, with the 10-year yield at 5.25%.

**Immigration and Corporate Policy**
The Trump administration's suspension of Microsoft, Adobe, and six additional companies from the green card sponsorship initiative represents a meaningful escalation in tech-sector immigration enforcement. The indefinite suspensions — accompanied by active federal investigations — target a program that has channeled skilled engineering talent to US technology and financial firms for decades. The policy tension between restricting foreign worker pathways and maintaining US AI competitiveness is becoming an increasingly consequential structural risk for the sector.
