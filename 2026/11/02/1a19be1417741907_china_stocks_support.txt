Investors in mainland Chinese stocks finally got some good news after the 
worst month in more than a decade for the benchmark gauge amid reports that
regulators met with banks 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-01/china-to-convene-meeting-on-supporting-stock-market-reports-say?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261102&utm_content=10888>
 and brokerages to discuss market support measures.

Cailian reported that the central bank will conduct its swap operation on 
Tuesday, utilizing a key tool from the 2024 rescue plan to inject liquidity. 
The news outlet also reported earlier that financial regulators planned to meet 
with major banks on Monday to study optimizing lending programs, which were 
also used in 2024.

The CSI 300 rose 0.8%, clawing back some of last week’s 2.9% drop, while the 
tech-heavy Star 50 Index ended the day more than 3% higher. Chinese shares have 
been mired in a slump since the summer, weighed down by doubts over the 
near-term earnings potential of artificial intelligence companies and a 
sluggish economy. Data on Monday showed China’s factory activity expanded at a
slower pace 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/china-factory-activity-expands-but-ends-streak-of-faster-growth?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261102&utm_content=10888>
 for the first time since July.
