• Japanese 10-year JGB yields jumped 8.8 basis points to 3.04% at Thursday's open — a 30-year high — as the market reopened from holiday and absorbed the global bond selloff.
• The yen approached 160 per dollar, reviving intervention risk; the currency is down for two consecutive weeks.
• Alibaba unveiled its Zhenwu V900 AI accelerator chip at its annual conference, pledging to triple performance versus its predecessor and targeting 20 gigawatts of cloud data center capacity by 2032.
• Chinese ports logged a record 7.3 million container throughput in the seven days through September 20, up 9% year-on-year, as exporters rushed shipments ahead of the Xi-Trump summit.
• India's National Stock Exchange (NSE) shares rose on their stock market debut following a $2.4 billion IPO.

**Japan and Currencies**
JGB yields hitting levels last seen in the mid-1990s — in tandem with US 10-year yields at 5.12% and a strengthening dollar — is intensifying pressure on the Bank of Japan's policy framework. The yen's slide toward 160 per dollar closely tracks the level that prompted official intervention previously; the Ministry of Finance has not acted yet, but rhetoric is likely to escalate.

**China Technology and Trade**
Alibaba's Hangzhou conference signaled that China's AI buildout is accelerating despite US export controls that block access to leading-edge TSMC manufacturing and restrict Nvidia chips. The Zhenwu V900 relies on domestic capacity, likely SMIC, which remains constrained. China's record port throughput reflects exporters front-running tariff uncertainty ahead of the Trump-Xi summit, and suggests near-term trade data will flatter China's export figures regardless of the summit outcome.
