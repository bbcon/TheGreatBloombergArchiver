**Technology and Markets**
The negative read-through from Alphabet's revenue miss weighed on Asian technology equities broadly, with Nasdaq 100 futures declining approximately 1% during Asian hours. Samsung compounded the negative sentiment by leaving investors without a concrete enhanced shareholder return plan. Chinese tech hardware stocks fell despite some solid individual quarterly results, as weak global AI sentiment dominated. Australian institutional investors were also flagged as cautious ahead of Anthropic's anticipated IPO. The Nikkei 225 bucked the regional trend, rising 1.1%, partly reflecting yen dynamics and continued investor interest in Japan's corporate reform narrative.

**Regional Geopolitics and Commodities**
The Singapore-Indonesia diplomatic friction, while unlikely to escalate materially, illustrates the fragility of regional energy dependencies. Indonesia's gas export reduction was small in volume but symbolically significant, arriving as President Prabowo's domestic approval ratings slide. On copper, the Yangshan premium surge signals a meaningful tightening of the physical market that predates any formal tariff implementation — a pattern worth monitoring as a leading indicator of broader commodity trade dislocation.
