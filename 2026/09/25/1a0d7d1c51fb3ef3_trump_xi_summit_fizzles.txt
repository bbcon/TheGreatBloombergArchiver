Donald Trump’s fete for China’s Xi Jinping has been heavy on pageantry and 
platitudes but light onsubstantive announcements 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/trump-xi-summit-goes-big-on-pomp-but-small-on-substance?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9320>
, with the events shadowed by the US president’s preoccupation with 
construction projects and personal grievances.

Through Thursday evening in Washington, the most significant news tied to the 
gathering was announced before it even began, when Treasury Secretary Scott 
Bessent said just as Xi’s plane landed that the two sideshad extended 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/xi-gets-rare-tarmac-welcome-from-trump-after-landing-at-us-base?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9320>
 a trade truce for another two months.

Anticipated agreements on tariff cuts and a new line of communication on 
artificial intelligence have yet to be announced. The day was capped off by a 
state dinner featuring a three-course menu, attended by American tech and 
financial titans.

Trump gifted Xi a statue of a bald eagle — a national symbol of the US — and 
declared he and Xi have “never gotten along better.”
