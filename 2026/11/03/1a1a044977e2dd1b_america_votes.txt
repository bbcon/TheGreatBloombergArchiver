Deep Dive: Lumentum’s $115 Billion Rise
Michael Hurlston, CEO of Lumentum, in Tokyo on Oct. 9.Photographer: Shoko 
Takayasu/Bloomberg
In 2015, executives at the Silicon Valley hardware firm known then as JDSU 
spun off a division making optical components and commercial lasers, believing 
the unit would be better off chasing growth opportunities on its own.They were 
right 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/lumentum-goes-from-1-billion-spinoff-to-115-billion-takeover?cmpid=110326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10935>
.

 * When Lumentum Holdings began trading later that year, it had a roughly $1 
billion market value and was reporting quarterly adjusted net income of about 
$15.3 million.
 * A decade later, the company is generating more than $1 billion of sales in 
a single quarter, saw $461 million of adjusted profits last quarter and is now 
on track to become one of the largest tech takeovers of all time: On Monday, 
Marvell Technology agreed to anall-stock purchase 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/marvell-reaches-115-billion-deal-for-lumentum-holdings?cmpid=110326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10935>
 that values Lumentum at $115 billion. The deal has also beenblessed by 
Nvidia’s Jensen Huang 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/nvidia-s-huang-gave-blessing-to-marvell-lumentum-megadeal?cmpid=110326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261103&utm_content=10935>
.
 * The allure of Lumentum has always been its lasers. With the unprecedented 
expansion of data centers worldwide to power advanced AI systems, Lumentum’s 
technology has taken on a greater purpose: moving massive amounts of data 
across chips and servers at incredibly high speeds.
 * Such demand from AI data centers is now so strong that some of Lumentum’s 
components are “completely sold out” through early 2029, CEO Michael Hurlston 
said in a Bloomberg interview last month.
