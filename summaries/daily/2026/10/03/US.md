**Energy and Nuclear Policy**
The Trump administration's planned $4 billion loan to Vistra Corp. to upgrade existing nuclear capacity represents a meaningful fiscal commitment to baseload power, consistent with the administration's broader energy security posture. The package does not require new plant construction, instead targeting efficiency and output expansion at existing facilities — a faster path to incremental generation. Whether the loan advances through DOE processes without political friction remains to be seen, but the direction of policy is clear: nuclear is receiving active federal support alongside fossil fuels.

**Climate and Infrastructure**
San Diego Gas & Electric's preemptive power shutoffs, driven by extreme heat and high winds, underscore the physical risk exposure embedded in California's grid infrastructure. These events carry both near-term economic disruption and longer-term capital expenditure implications for utility operators navigating wildfire liability. Separately, Keurig Dr Pepper launched its AltaRounds compostable coffee puck system — a modest consumer goods development with limited macro significance but notable as an indicator of evolving ESG-driven product strategy in the consumer staples sector.
