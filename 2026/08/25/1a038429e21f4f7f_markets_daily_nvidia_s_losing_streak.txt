Nvidia’s losing streak

Nvidia shares have fallen in seven straight sessions, the longest run of 
losses since 2022. It’s a worrying stat for the company’sexecutives as they 
prepare to report earnings tomorrow. 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-24/nvidia-s-trillion-dollar-chip-market-has-friends-and-foes-closing-in?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>

Here’s what you should know:

 * It’s still a cash cow. Analysts estimate that revenue nearly doubled last 
quarter to $92 billion. That’s more than any of its rivals get in a full year.
 * The competition is heating up. A growing group of upstarts are vying for a 
bigger share of the market.
 * Nvidia’s star has dimmed on Wall Street. Though its shares have climbed 15% 
in 2026, the Philadelphia Stock Exchange Semiconductor Index has gained 66%. 
The stock ticked higher on Tuesday morning. 
 * The stock reflects more fear than hope, says MLIV strategist Sebastian 
Boyd. Its forward P/E ratio is merely in line with the S&P 500. In other words, 
traders don’t put much faith in earnings growth beating the broader market 
after next year.
 * Bank of America says buy. Last week, the bank’s analysts called it a 
“compelling opportunity,” 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/bofa-sees-nvidia-trading-at-up-to-50-discount-on-ai-risks?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
 saying the stock trades at a discount of as much as 50%. Their price target? 
$350. 
 * Price hikes are coming. Chipmaker stocks have been rattled in recent days 
by news that some of Nvidia’s biggest customers were told about AI-relatedprice 
increases above 15%. 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/nvidia-customers-notified-about-ai-related-price-hikes-above-15?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
 * Nvidia is an industry lynchpin. As the company helps arrange financing 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/nvidia-to-team-with-wall-street-on-500-billion-package-ft-says?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
 for AI infrastructure, some are calling it the “central bank of AI.”  
<https://x.com/biancoresearch/status/2091497365832114458>

Software’s comeback

Remember the SaaSpocalypse — the meltdown in software stocks triggered by 
fears that AI would upend the traditional Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business 
model? It’s over, or at least, themarket thinks so 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/software-stocks-get-an-earnings-gut-check-after-hot-summer-rally?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
.

Software stocks have soared recently, with the iShares Expanded Tech-Software 
Sector ETF (IGV) up close to 40% since the April lows.

Industry executives have meanwhile struck a newly confrontational tone 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/salesforce-servicenow-fight-ai-fears-with-buybacks-and-bluster?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
, and bellwether Salesforce will likely give an impassioned defense tomorrow, 
when the company reports. “You can give us back the market cap,” Bill 
McDermott, ServiceNow’s CEO, told analysts earlier this year.

A bullish sign: Profits have been strong so far. All 13 S&P 500 software 
companies that have reported results have beat estimates by an average of 10% 
and only one company missed on revenue. —Richard Abbey 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AV8Ll6yC_7Y/richard-abbey?cmpid=082526_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8151>
