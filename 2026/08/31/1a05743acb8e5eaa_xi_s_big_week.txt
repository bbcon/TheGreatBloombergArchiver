Xi Jinping will be clocking up air miles this week, zipping from Beijing to 
Bishkek to Cairo and back.

It’s part of a diplomatic push designed to increase the Chinese leader’s 
influence just as that of President Donald Trumpslides 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/where-the-us-canada-trade-war-stands-after-talks-fell-apart?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
, with his “America First” approach riling much of the globe.

On display will be an array of diplomatic approaches that help Xi boost his 
international standing, including his emphasis on the personal touch.

First stop is the capital of Kyrgyzstan for a state visit to meet with 
President Sadyr Japarov. It’s a classic example of “neighborhood diplomacy,” a 
top Xi priority aimed at securing peace and stability along China’s borderlands.

Japarov is also hosting the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, putting Xi in 
the same room with Russia’s Vladimir Putin, Narendra Modi of India and Iranian 
President Masoud Pezeshkian. The show of power signals to Trump that his 
unilateral actions in Iran and elsewherearen’t going unchallenged 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/trump-s-iran-war-sets-test-for-xi-and-putin-s-security-club?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
.
Japarov and Xi ahead of the China-Central Asia Summit in Xian in May 2023.
Photographer: Florence Lo/AFP/Getty Images
Still, while Iran will be top of the agenda, don’t anticipate much more than 
symbolic solidarity. It’s indicative of an inherent weakness of the body, 
stymied by internal rivalry and lacking a clear remit beyond the vague goal of 
building mutual trust.

In Egypt, Xi will be visiting a North African heavyweight that’s engaged in an 
increasingly precarious geopolitical balancing act between Washington and 
Beijing.

One example: Chinese tech champion Huawei has proposed supplying Cairo with AI 
chips 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/huawei-egypt-ai-ascend-chips-test-us-tech-diplomacy-nvidia-amd-microsoft?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
 for data centers, prompting Washington to assemble a possible counteroffer.

Meanwhile Egypt and Kyrgyzstan are both Global South countries, yet another 
prong of Xi’s statecraft aimed at fostering alignment with China’s position.

Xi’s diplomacy doesn’t stop there: It’s widely anticipated that he’ll attend 
the Modi-hosted BRICS summit in mid-Septemberbefore heading 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/bessent-to-push-g20-to-rethink-china-trade-terms-reuters-says?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
 to Washington.

<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/xi-signals-defiance-as-us-threatens-sanctions-for-iran-support?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
 to sit across the table from Trump.— Colum Murphy

have so far shrugged off the threat and analysts wereleft underwhelmed

 by the US actions.

in Venezuela right now, I would be asking myself if there’s going to be a

Iceland’s government pledged to honor the result of Saturday’s tight and 
divisive referendum 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/iceland-to-hold-off-from-eu-accession-talks-as-voters-say-no?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>

Modi has long sought to balance ties with Russia and the US as part of India’s 
broader policy of strategic autonomy, but that hasbecome more challenging 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/modi-putin-meeting-how-trump-tariffs-test-india-s-russia-ties?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>

warmer relations with Putin will be in the spotlight when the two leaders meet 
today.

Colombia’s government moved to officially end talks with some of the nation’s 
armed rebel groups, effectivelyhalting a peace process 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/colombia-ends-talks-with-rebels-in-blow-to-petro-peace-process?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>

Espriella’s Trump-friendly administration issued a series of resolutions to 
formally stop the negotiation process, El Tiempo reported, days after 
authorities bombed a rebel position and ordered alleged drug traffickers to be 
extradited to the US.

Chart of the Day

Modi’s ambition for India to reach developed nation status 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/modi-s-developed-nation-dream-strains-against-india-s-growth-gap?cmpid=BBD083126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260831&utm_content=8350>
 by 2047 is a centerpiece of his third term. However, assuming the current rate 
of growth, the economy would fall short of his vision, according to several 
economists. Getting there would require the world’s sixth-largest economy to 
grow at 9.25% annually for 21 years, estimates Ashok Lahiri, a senior official 
at a government-run think-tank — a rate reached just three times over the past 
50 years.
