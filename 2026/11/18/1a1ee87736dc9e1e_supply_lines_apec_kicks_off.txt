Xi Welcomes Trump in Show of US-China Warmth and Cooperation

After nearly 18 months of tough negotiations, tensions in the world’s most 
consequential and rocky trading relationship took a short break Wednesday as 
Chinese leader Xi Jinping and US President Donald Trump portrayed stability 
with a rare embrace.

Trump arrived in futuristic Shenzhen 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-11-15/futuristic-shenzhen-shows-trump-the-difficulty-of-stopping-xi-jinping-s-china?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
, where Xi greeted him at the airport with a cheek-to-cheek hug and much 
ceremony kicking off APEC leaders’ talks. Their more formal and substantive 
bilat is Thursday, and then both will meet with Russia’s Vladimir Putin later 
for an unprecedented trilateralsummit 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/xi-trump-putin-gets-world-worrying-about-a-great-power-stitch-up?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
. (Follow the day’s events on ourlive blog 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/live-blog/2026-11-18/trump-xi-apec-live?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 for news, commentary and analysis.)

The year-old US and China trade truce 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-extends-pause-on-24-us-tariffs-to-january-keeps-10-rate?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 expires in January amid fresh strains including an alleged spy scandal 
involving US high-end chip makerNvidia 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/interim-nvidia-ceo-says-it-s-no-longer-sending-ai-chips-to-china?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
.

Earlier this month Trump’s Republicans lost control of Congress to Democrats 
in midterm elections, a shift that may spark investigations and political 
pressure for Washington toget tougher 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/us-moves-to-limit-remote-access-to-advanced-ai-chips?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 on Beijing — particularly on access to advanced US semiconductors.

Related: China Issues Blunt Warning on Global Turmoil at APEC Meeting 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-20/china-issues-blunt-warning-on-global-turbulence-at-apec-meeting?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>

On Tuesday, China’s Ministry of Commerce said it will take countermeasures if 
the US proceeds with restrictions on remote access to advanced chips, warning 
that the measures threaten global supply chains.

In a speech setting the tone for the APEC talks he’s hosting, Xi warned 
against economic barriers and urged closer cooperation among Asia-Pacific 
nations as new US restrictions on China’s access to advanced AI chips test the 
fragile truce.

That message was reinforced by Putin 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/putin-trump-to-hold-full-fledged-talk-at-apec-kremlin-says?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
, who used the same forum to accuse formerly dominant powers of trying to hold 
back rising competitors, arguing that sanctions, tariffs and export 
restrictions couldn’t halt their development.

Read More: Xi, Putin Assail Trade Barriers Ahead of Summit With Trump 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/xi-warns-against-rampant-protectionism-ahead-of-trump-talks?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>

Some heads of state worried about getting caught in the crossfire. New US 
export restrictions on high-end semiconductors risk denting Thailand’s 
ambitions to be adata center hub 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/thailand-sees-new-us-chip-curbs-as-threat-to-ambitions-to-be-data-center-hub?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
, its top diplomat said. Meanwhile, Singapore’s prime minister expects some 
impact on thetransshipment volume 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/singapore-lawrence-wong-transshipment-impact-nvidia-us-chip-curbs-washing-megaspeed?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 of chips because of tighter US rules.

Others expressed optimism about regional cooperation. South Korea’s Lee Jae 
Myung called for greater supply-chain linkages, including early-warning systems 
and access tocritical minerals 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/rare-earth-strategic-metals-price-gap-flags-supply-strains-trump-xi-meet?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 and energy. Vietnam’s To Lam pitched the next stage of the country’s economic 
transformation, seeking more investment in semiconductors, AI and clean energy.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney said he was meeting with Trump on 
Wednesday on the sidelines of APEC. Carneytold reporters 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/carney-says-he-ll-meet-with-trump-later-today-in-shenzhen?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
 he’s been making progress with the Trump administration on a trade deal, but 
acknowledged it’s often been “two steps forward, one step back.”

During an earlier stop in Manila, Trump and his Philippine counterpart 
Ferdinand Marcos Jr. hailed their countries’ alliance and pledged stronger ties 
in areas includingtrade and investment 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/trump-reassures-philippines-of-us-support-ahead-of-xi-meeting?cmpid=BBD111826_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11458>
. Trump said the two sides had “consummated a very big trade deal,” though no 
details about the accord were immediately released.
