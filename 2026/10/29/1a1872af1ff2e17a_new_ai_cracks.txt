Chinese copper buyers rushing to secure supply in a tightening market have 
sent a key gauge of import demand shooting to a record high. The Yangshan 
premium, which measures what Chinese importers pay above London Metal Exchange 
prices,spiked to more than $200 a ton 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/copper-sold-out-everywhere-as-key-china-gauge-flashes-warning?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261029&utm_content=10723>
. That’s the highest in Fastmarkets data going back to 2017. The surge over the 
last couple of months reflects greater competition for the metal both inside 
and outside the country. The prospect of US import tariffs has seen massive 
volumes being sent there, while, in China, inventories have thinned and output 
has eased.
