**UK Macro and Monetary Policy**
The anticipated inflation print, if confirmed, would materially reinforce the case for a Bank of England rate hike at the December meeting. Energy-driven price acceleration — coinciding with a stabilizing labor market — presents the Bank with a classic stagflationary tension: domestic demand remains fragile, yet price pressures are re-accelerating. Markets are growing increasingly confident in a December move, and this week's data are likely to either cement or complicate that pricing.

**Deutsche Bank and European Credit**
Deutsche Bank's fixed-income unit is on course for record revenues of approximately €10 billion in 2026 — nearly double its 2019 level — positioning the firm just behind Bank of America and ahead of Morgan Stanley in European and Asian FICC rankings. The recovery, led by Ram Nayak, is notable given the bank's near-existential restructuring in 2019. However, with fixed income generating 84% of investment banking revenue, concentration risk is high, and the US expansion — where compensation costs alone are approaching €3 billion — introduces meaningful execution risk.
