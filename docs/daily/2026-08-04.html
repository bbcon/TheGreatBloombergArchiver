US

Economic Data

July ISM Manufacturing printed above consensus, marking the fastest expansion in over four years on strong production, new orders, and hiring. This is a notable positive surprise given the headwinds from elevated interest rates and oil-price volatility. The result suggests domestic demand has remained resilient despite the drag from the US-Iran conflict, lending credibility to the view that near-term recession risk remains contained.

Earnings and Equity Markets

The S&P 500 is approaching its June 2 record high, with the earnings cycle doing the heavy lifting. Q2 EPS growth of 47.4% year-on-year — partly reflecting tax benefits from the One Big Beautiful Bill — represents a genuine fundamental anchor for the rally. Hyperscalers rebounded sharply following reassuring results from Microsoft and Amazon, while Palantir's after-hours surge of 14.8% on raised guidance reinforced AI-driven commercial momentum. The Russell 2000 is on pace to outperform the S&P 500 for the first time since 2020, suggesting broadening market participation. Leverage metrics from prime brokers show meaningful positioning cleanup following last month's momentum unwind, reducing near-term technical overhang. ---

Asia

  • The US and Japan conducted their first coordinated yen-purchase intervention in decades; USD/JPY trades near ¥157.93, with 155 emerging as the critical technical threshold for the rally's durability.
  • Chinese AI models, including Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max, are rapidly closing the gap with US frontier models, intensifying geopolitical tensions ahead of a planned Trump-Xi summit in late September.

Energy Shock and Growth Implications

The protracted closure of the Strait of Hormuz is inflicting measurable economic damage across energy-importing Asia. Bloomberg Economics analysis of NASA satellite imagery shows nighttime radiance has declined across nearly 60% of the region's land area since the conflict began — an indicator corroborated by sector-level data showing curtailed manufacturing, transport, and tourism activity. Bangladesh, Pakistan, and Cambodia face the most acute stress. The ADB warns that scarring effects — household debt accumulation, job cuts — may persist well into 2027 even after oil prices ease.

Currency and Corporate Developments

The coordinated US-Japan yen intervention represents a meaningful shift in currency diplomacy. Treasury Secretary Bessent framed it as a gesture of alliance solidarity, though analysts note a range of US economic interests — including Japan's $550 billion investment commitment and ongoing trade negotiations — likely motivated Washington's participation. Toyota lifted its full-year profit outlook and announced a $6.3 billion buyback, supported by hybrid demand and yen weakness. Indonesia's central bank independence appears at risk as President Prabowo moves to install a more aligned governor, a development worth monitoring for regional monetary policy credibility. ---

Europe

  • European gas storage has fallen to its lowest seasonal level in nearly two decades, while the Rhine dropped to its lowest water level in approximately 150 years — compounding energy supply stress heading into winter.
  • HSBC reported Q2 pretax profit of $10.1 billion, beating consensus, and announced a $1 billion share buyback while raising its cost-cutting target to $2 billion in savings.
  • Lufthansa fell 11% after warning on full-year earnings and abandoning capacity growth plans, citing fuel price volatility.

Energy and Climate Stress

Europe's energy system is under simultaneous pressure from multiple directions. Low Rhine water levels are disrupting coal and fuel transport, while high river temperatures have forced French nuclear plants offline at a record pace. Hungary faced electricity prices above €500/MWh after low Danube levels forced the Paks nuclear facility to shut temporarily. Nordic hydro reserves are unusually depleted. Natural gas storage, already the lowest in nearly two decades for this time of year, reflects both the Hormuz-driven price surge and stalled stockpiling. Belgium became wholly dependent on Russian LNG imports in July, a politically sensitive development given the EU's planned Russian LNG ban in 2027. The compounding of heat-driven demand and supply-side constraints leaves Europe with limited buffer heading into the heating season.

Corporate News

HSBC's earnings beat and buyback announcement reflect continued strength in banking and wealth management revenues, with CEO Georges Elhedery accelerating restructuring. Zalando sank 17% on weaker-than-expected sales, signaling consumer discretionary pressure consistent with ECB research showing euro-area households have curtailed spending since the war began — driven primarily by uncertainty rather than realized income losses. ---

Argentina

Argentina's reserve position remains structurally vulnerable. The absence of sovereign bond issuance since Caputo's return to office contrasts with what analysts estimate is genuine market appetite, suggesting the administration is navigating a difficult trade-off between debt sustainability optics and hard-currency needs. Any deterioration in the external account — whether from a global shock or domestic policy slippage — could accelerate pressure on reserves before alternative financing is secured.

Mexico and Regional Dynamics

Mexico's investment climate is attracting high-level attention from global asset managers, with BlackRock's direct engagement with the Sheinbaum administration reflecting appetite for large-scale energy infrastructure deals. The economy's trajectory, however, remains sluggish, requiring private capital to complement public investment. Across the region, elevated oil prices from the Hormuz disruption present a mixed picture: net exporters benefit from higher revenues, while energy importers face additional inflationary and fiscal pressure. ---

Oil Markets and Hormuz

The relationship between ceasefire signals and oil prices is becoming increasingly reflexive and difficult to trade. Monday's crude decline on Trump's negotiation comments reversed a pattern that had reasserted itself since hostilities resumed, and the subsequent partial recovery on Qatar's deal-language confirmation illustrates the market's binary sensitivity to diplomatic headlines. The futures curve suggests Brent will remain elevated at six and twelve months, but at levels that do not represent a catastrophic supply scenario — an implicit market judgment that full Hormuz closure is temporary. That assumption is being tested: Houthi threats to attack Aramco tankers rerouted to the Red Sea, and Ukraine's drone strikes on Iranian Caspian shipping, introduce escalation pathways that complicate any stable resolution.

Global Monetary Policy and AI Geopolitics

The Federal Reserve and Bank of England appear content to allow elevated long-end yields to perform tightening work, with the US 10-year Treasury at 4.70%. A new front in US-China technology competition has opened around Anthropic's Mythos AI model, which Beijing views as a potential offensive cyber instrument — adding a volatile dimension to trade negotiations ahead of the Trump-Xi summit. Chinese AI models are simultaneously closing the capability gap with US rivals, raising questions about the long-run efficacy of semiconductor export controls.
