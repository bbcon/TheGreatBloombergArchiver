**Brazil-Argentina Diplomatic Rupture**
The recall of Brazil's ambassador represents the sharpest escalation yet in the Lula-Milei relationship, which has been strained since Milei's election. The immediate trigger was Milei's public criticism of both Lula and a sitting Brazilian Supreme Court justice, which Brasília characterized as interference in its domestic affairs. The diplomatic rupture carries economic as well as political consequences: Brazil and Argentina are Mercosur's two largest members, and sustained tension complicates the bloc's ongoing trade negotiations, including the long-delayed EU-Mercosur agreement. How quickly the dispute is contained — or whether it deepens — will depend partly on whether Milei moderates his rhetoric, which his track record suggests is uncertain.
