It’s a sign of how far the United Nations has become sidelined that on the eve 
of its annual gathering of world leaders, the news from inside Turtle Bay was 
whether theescalator that famously malfunctioned 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/un-scrambles-to-avoid-a-repeat-of-trump-s-2025-escalator-snafu?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 for Donald Trump last year was now working.

“It has been checked and rechecked,” UN spokesman Stephane Dujarric reassured 
ahead of the US president’s speech from the iconic green podium today.

From that same pulpit, Venezuela’s Hugo Chavez accused the US of being the 
devil.

It was here, too, that Libyan dictator Muammar Qaddafi rambled about the JFK 
assassination. Two years later, in 2011, he was dead. The last notable act by 
the UN Security Council had been to authorize the military intervention that 
toppled his regime at the height of the Arab uprisings.
Trump and Melania Trump during the escalator fail last year.Photographer: 
Alexi J. Rosenfeld/Getty Images
But to focus on the speeches — these days it’s Trump and Israel’s Benjamin 
Netanyahu whostill hold the attention 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-20/trump-s-envoy-works-with-the-un-while-his-boss-bashes-it?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 — is to miss the point of UN General Assembly. It’s really about the meetings 
that you don’t know are happening.

In 2025, as Brazil and the US ratcheted up the rhetoric on tariffs in public, 
all it took was one unscripted moment of “excellent chemistry” behind the 
scenes for Trump and Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva to dial it back.

This year, both men face fraught elections 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/brazil-election-lula-and-bolsonaro-fight-over-milei-s-economy-before-vote?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 within weeks of each other.

Meanwhile, the situation in the Middle East risks spiraling out of control 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/pakistan-iran-discuss-regional-peace-as-us-talks-remain-stalled?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 and Russia’s war in Ukraine is escalating precipitously ahead of what 
threatens to beanother hard winter 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/ukrainians-are-bracing-for-worst-wartime-winter-of-airstrikes?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
.

After intermittent threats to wipe out Iran, Trump suggested he’s open to 
meeting its president. Back in 2015, the year a nuclear deal was signed, Barack 
Obama and Iran’s top diplomat shook hands away from prying cameras.

Should something similar happen again, the weeklong jamboree will have just 
about been worth it.— Flavia Krause-Jackson 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ACWlz_feWqg/flavia-krausejackson?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>

Global Must Reads

The US has proposed a $5 billion fund to help rebuild critical infrastructure 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/us-plans-5-billion-middle-east-reconstruction-fund-for-iran-war?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 across the Middle East damaged in the war with Iran even though the 
seven-month conflict continues. The UK meanwhile hasagreed to support 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/uk-close-to-helping-saudi-arabia-counter-houthi-attacks?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 Saudi Arabia’s military in countering the Houthis as the kingdom comes under 
frequent attack from the Iran-backed militants, one of Andy Burnham’s first 
significant military decisions since becoming prime minister in July.

Chinese and US officials wrapped up a second day of talks in New York as they 
sought to advance negotiations 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/china-us-discuss-ai-investment-on-second-day-of-trade-talks?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 on issues such as AI, investment and trade ahead of President Xi Jinping’s 
visit to the US this week. Washington’s increased reliance on a Pentagon 
blacklist to target China’s biggest companiesthreatens to cast a pall 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/pentagon-blacklist-lingers-as-irritant-in-trump-xi-talks-on-ai?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 over Xi’s meeting with Trump, and so far the US hasn’t signaled a willingness 
to back down.
Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng arrives for talks at JPMorgan headquarters.
Photographer: John Lamparski/Bloomberg
Prime Minister Mark Carney’s government pledged to complete reviews of large 
projects within a year andcut down on labor disputes 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/carney-unveils-bill-to-shorten-project-reviews-reduce-strikes?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 in Canada’s transport sector as part of a legislative package unveiled 
yesterday. Trade Minister Maninder Sidhu meanwhile says talks with India are 
“moving along really well” and could conclude in the coming months as his 
countrylooks for new markets 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/canada-says-india-trade-talks-making-great-headway?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 for its goods amid a tariff war with the US.

China and the European Union should find a way to avoid a trade confrontation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/china-eu-should-avoid-trade-clash-beijing-s-top-diplomat-says?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
, Foreign Minister Wang Yi said in a call with German counterpart Johann 
Wadephul — comments that come before a deadline next month to find solutions to 
the two sides’ differences. Wang said he hoped Berlin would “maintain a 
rational and pragmatic attitude,” and encourage Brussels to “stay on the right 
track of open cooperation, dialogue and consultation.”

One of Australia’s largest states banned the construction of data centers 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/australian-state-bans-data-centers-from-residential-areas?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 in residential areas and mandated that they must “bring their own renewable 
energy supply,” part of a slew of rules designed to tackle growing community 
pushback. Victoria, home to Melbourne, says that while data centers are vital 
to the economy, they will need to ensure local water and electricity resources 
aren’t significantly impacted.

The UK reassured Mauritius of its determination to pursue a plan to cede 
sovereignty 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/uk-urges-patience-from-mauritius-with-chagos-deal-in-limbo?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 over the Chagos Islands to the Indian Ocean nation, even as officials concede 
the handover could remain on ice until after Trump leaves office.

President To Lam says in a Bloomberg interview that Vietnam is “very close” 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/vietnam-leader-says-us-deal-close-denies-re-routing-china-goods?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 to a trade deal with the US, as he pledged to buy more high-tech goods like 
aircraft to narrow the trade gap before talks with American officials in New 
York.

Russian leader Vladimir Putin is set to travel to China for the APEC summit 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/putin-plans-to-attend-xi-s-apec-summit-and-skip-g20-with-trump?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 hosted by Xi in November, sources say, but is likely to skip the ensuing Group 
of 20 gathering in the US with Trump.

Next year, the leaders of the world’s biggest economies will descend on 
Manchester 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/uk-confirms-burnham-s-former-fiefdom-of-manchester-to-host-g20?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 – where until recently Burnham was mayor — after the UK confirmed the English 
city will host the G20 summit.

For the richest of the rich in India, family control remains paramount. But 
the younger generation of its wealthy business scions, worth hundreds of 
millions of dollars, isincreasingly cashing out 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/rich-heirs-of-india-s-family-businesses-cash-out-inheritance-manage-wealth?cmpid=BBD092226_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260922&utm_content=9177>
 and investing their inheritance elsewhere, worrying some of the nation’s 
elders. In an economy dominated by first-generation fortunes, it’s become a 
broader discussion about society and the country’s future.
