If this was a scripted drama, then everything is building toward the US 
midterm elections in November as a season finale.

With concern growing over the size of US public debt, Donald Trump promised 
Americans 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/trump-urges-voters-to-let-him-finish-the-job-at-gop-convention?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260910&utm_content=8742>
 $5,000 each if they elect the Republicans. Vice President JD Vance suggested
tariff gains <https://www.foxnews.com/video/6370975230112> would fund the 
payments — in other words, countries such as Canada would unwittingly help 
grease the wheels for Republicans to retain their majorities in the House and 
Senate. The more than $1 trillion payout would be called the Trump dividend.

Regardless of the source of the funds, or whether the proposal becomes 
reality, the pledge to dole out huge sums of money to voters is likely to add 
to concern over the scale of spending by the Trump administration — and 
pressure on US Treasuries. Despite Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent 
dramatically claiming “I am the house now 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/newsletters/2026-09-10/bessent-dares-bond-traders-to-burn-down-the-house?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260910&utm_content=8742>
” with regards to betting against his market interventions, yields on the 10 
and 30-year yieldsremain elevated 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/treasuries-weaken-as-soaring-oil-prices-revive-rate-hike-worries?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260910&utm_content=8742>
.

In his speech, Trump also downplayed concerns about energy prices and said the 
war with Iran would be over soon. “The war is going to end immediately after 
the election because they can’t hold out any longer.” There is little sign of 
acquiescence from the Iranians. Iran is ready for a more intense war andwill 
escalate 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/iran-ready-for-more-intense-war-and-won-t-relent-official-says?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260910&utm_content=8742>
 counterstrikes if the US continues attacking its territory, a senior official 
from the Islamic Republic said.

With oil prices in London above $100 a barrel, traders are betting 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-sept-10?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260910&utm_content=8742>
 the conflict won’t be resolved soon. As for Trump, his pledge is the latest 
effort to ward off midterm losses that would hurtle him into lame duck status 
and launch a wave of congressional investigations. Plenty could yet happen 
between now and November.

extends beyond individual companies — in a historic first, risk premiums on 
emerging-market corporate debt indexes are converging with comparable US 
benchmarks.
