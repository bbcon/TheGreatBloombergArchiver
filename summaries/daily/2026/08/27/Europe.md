**Iceland's EU Question**
The referendum is a close-run test of sentiment for an EU enlargement process otherwise stalled since Croatia joined 13 years ago. Fishing and agriculture are seen as the hardest issues in any accession talks, and eventual euro adoption would require "deep reforms" of the labor market, per Iceland's central bank. Iceland already has single-market access via the European Economic Area, leaving voters to weigh incremental gains against sovereignty costs.

**UK Policy and French Politics**
A Barclays survey found that this year's extreme heat has measurably altered UK business investment plans and consumer behavior. In France, seven declared presidential candidates presented economic platforms to business leaders as Macron's decade in office winds down; polls show a fragmented centrist field is currently advantaging far-right leader Marine Le Pen and far-left figure Jean-Luc Mélenchon.
