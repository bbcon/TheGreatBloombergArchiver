**China Under Pressure on Two Fronts**
Beijing faces simultaneous trade and geopolitical pressure: alongside the overcapacity tariff, the EU's proposed measures could cover roughly 27% of China's annual exports to the bloc, per Goldman Sachs, while dozens of Chinese and Hong Kong firms were hit by Bessent's latest Iran sanctions. China has defended its cooperation with Iran and warned against disruption, and separately framed its uneven 2026 growth as resilience in a rare public defense of the economy.
