authorities summoned e-commerce operators and called for tighter 
product-quality standards, pressuring Hong Kong’s tech sector.

expected 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-22/philippines-delivers-jumbo-rate-hike-as-inflation-heats-up-anew?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261022&utm_content=10408>

third quarter, compared with analyst expectations for 1.55% growth. The region 
generates about 40% of Hermès’ total revenue.

Jio Leverage Play Pops Up at SBI as India Brokers Lure IPO Bets

In a rare move for an Indian initial public offering, SBI Securities is 
offering a leveraged bet on the Jio Platforms IPO — a sign of the intense 
competition as the excitement over the debut grows. The brokerage is offering 
to finance as much as 60% of the amount needed to apply for the IPO with 
investors able to seek funding for applications ranging from 500,000 rupees 
($5,300) to 1 million rupees, with shares allotted as collateral.

northern Taiwan late Friday into Saturday, according to the US Joint Typhoon

War-torn Europe Propels a Surge in Indian Defense Exports

Europe’s rearming efforts are fueling an explosive increase in Indian defense 
exports, boosting a key industry in a way that could disrupt New Delhi’s 
longstanding relationship with Moscow. Indian arms makers have sent at least 
$872 million worth of ammunition – including artillery shells, mortar shells, 
propellant and explosives – to European countries since Russia’s invasion of 
Ukraine four years ago, according to data sourced by Trade Data Monitor from 
the Ministry of Commerce and Industry of India.
