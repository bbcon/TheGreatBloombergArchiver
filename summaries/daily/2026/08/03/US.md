**Monetary Policy and Yields**
Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference last week left markets without clear guidance, though the projected rate path was barely changed. More consequentially, the 30-year TIPS yield has reached levels last seen only briefly during the 2008 financial crisis — a development that may signal deteriorating confidence in the Fed's inflation credibility rather than a routine repricing. Warsh is also reportedly exploring a structural reduction in scheduled policy meetings, an unconventional move that raises questions about communication strategy at a sensitive juncture.

**Equity Markets**
Microsoft's earnings-driven rally — adding more than $600 billion in market cap over two days to rejoin the $3 trillion club — temporarily reversed two months of equal-weight outperformance and re-concentrated market leadership in mega-cap technology. Meta and Alphabet were punished despite strong earnings on concerns about capital spending discipline. The hedge fund Situational Awareness suffered a significant unwind of AI positions and was rescued by Citadel at distressed valuations, a development that appears to have contributed to broader volatility, though its role as cause versus symptom remains unclear.
