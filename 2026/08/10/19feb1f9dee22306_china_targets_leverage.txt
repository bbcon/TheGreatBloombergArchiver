The dramatic boom and semi-bust in Korean shares this year highlights the 
risks of excessive leverage — a lesson that may have informed Chinese officials 
as the country’s brokerages moved totighten scrutiny 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-brokers-tighten-client-scrutiny-to-curb-excessive-risk?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
 on margin financing, securities lending, and options trading, as well as new 
accounts.

Chinese retail investors had flooded into local stocks amid a rally in 
AI-related shares — and were partly flushed out as gains reversed last month. 
The margin trading balance dropped to 2.6 trillion yuan ($362 billion) at the 
end of July after topping 3 trillion yuan in late June.

To encourage greater caution, some Chinese firms including Citic Securities 
and East Money Information have recently raised compliance requirements for 
clients seeking leverage or derivatives trading, according to people familiar 
with the matter. At several brokerages, investors who opened fresh accounts in 
the past six months or have received frequent margin calls will be restricted 
from further borrowing.

The moves reinforce the approach of officials seeking to maintain gains in 
AI-related companies withoutfueling a bubble. 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-21/china-seeks-to-rescue-ai-stocks-without-fueling-a-bubble?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
 When tech stocks tumbled in July, authorities intervened withunusual speed 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-09/china-bets-on-ai-stocks-as-it-races-against-us-for-chip-tech-dominance?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
 to restore confidence. The latest steps seem aimed at ensuring the risks of 
speculative leverage don’t flare up again, even as it’s clear officials are 
favoring the sector due to itsnational significance 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/deepseek-mulls-new-funding-weeks-after-7-billion-round-ft-says?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
.

“Excessive concentrations of leverage in certain segments have increased the 
risk of market instability,” said Wang Chen, a partner at XuFunds Investment 
Management. “While those risks appear to have eased, they have likely not been 
fully unwound and will remain a regulatory focus in the coming period.”

What You Need to Know Today

The risk of deflation returning is rising in China. Consumer prices grew in 
Julyat the slowest pace 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
 in six months, while factory-gate inflation eased for the first time since the 
US-Iran war began in February.

Both price gauges slowed more than expected in July, based on data published 
by the National Bureau of Statistics. The consumer price index rose 0.5% in 
July from a year earlier, decelerating from 1% in the previous month to the 
weakest reading since January. Producer inflation moderated to 3.5% from 4.1% 
in June.

Destry Damayanti has been nominated by President Prabowo Subianto as the sole 
candidate 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/destry-damayanti-sole-bank-indonesia-chief-nominee-markets-prabowo-subianto?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260810&utm_content=7545>
 to head Bank Indonesia, filing a hole left by Perry Warjiyo’s unexpected 
departure in late July.

It’s not clear whether Destry has been nominated to serve out the remainder of 
Warjiyo’s term or to start a new five-year term. The soonest parliament can 
meet to consider candidates is Aug. 14, when lawmakers return from recess. The 
president’s ruling coalition controls a significant majority in parliament. 
Destry had been considered a front-runner due to her strong market credibility 
and constructive relationship with Prabowo.

the aircraft dropped 300 feet mid-air. The incident, which occurred on Aug. 4, 
left several passengers and cabin crew members injured.
