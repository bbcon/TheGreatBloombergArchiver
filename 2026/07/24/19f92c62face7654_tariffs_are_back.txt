Deep Dive: Barclays Trading Boss Hunts Growth
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/barclays-trading-boss-seeks-to-deliver-growth-in-volatile-markets?cmpid=072426_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6924>
Adeel Khan, head of global markets at Barclays.Source: Barclays
Every few months the most senior traders and salespeople at Barclays head to a 
one-hour meeting with their boss Adeel Khan. He wants todissect how individuals 
are performing 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/barclays-trading-boss-seeks-to-deliver-growth-in-volatile-markets?cmpid=072426_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6924>
, if and why desks are lagging Wall Street peers and any rosy assumptions his 
team are making.

 * Khan is steering Barclays’ trading business through one of the trickiest 
balancing acts in the industry. That’s to deliver growth within the confines of 
capital limits imposed by Chief Executive Officer CS Venkatakrishnan, who in 
2024 laid out a strategy that increases the focus on other units, such as 
retail banking and wealth.
 * The global markets unit contributed over a third of the bank’s revenue in 
the first quarter, underlining how the business underpins Barclays’ global 
ambitions.
 * But its Wall Street rivals remain a tough comparison, as American 
investment banks trampled earnings records in a second quarter fueled by 
volatility. Barclays’ markets division has grown at a slower rate than some US 
and European peers.
