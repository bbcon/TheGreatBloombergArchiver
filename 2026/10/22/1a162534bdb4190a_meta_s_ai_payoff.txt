A Bulgarian Navy sailor assists the Mizar search-and-rescue tug, escorting 
four rescued sailors from the sunken merchant vessel HS Dignity following drone 
attacks, in the Black Sea in Varna, Bulgaria on Oct. 13, 2026.Photographer: 
Denislav Stoychev/NurPhoto/Getty Images
Bulgarian shipbroker Yordan Petrov no longer sends his vessels into the Black 
Sea, calling the brave few who still do “kamikazes.” Seven commercial ships have
been attacked by Russian drones 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-22/russian-drones-are-already-killing-people-within-eu-territory?cmpid=102226_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261022&utm_content=10402>
 off the coast of Bulgaria and Romania since the beginning of October, killing 
more than two dozen sailors, according to both countries’ authorities.

 * The strikes took place within or on the cusp of NATO members’ territorial 
waters, marking a dangerous new escalation. They now test the alliance’s 
ability to deter an increasingly aggressive Russia that is willing to move 
beyond its hybrid campaign and start attacking Europe.
 * Moscow has denied responsibility for the attacks. However, the Kremlin said 
ships entering and leaving Ukraine’s ports are potential targets in response to 
Ukrainian strikes on Russia’s “shadow fleet.”
 * The death toll has increased pressure on the alliance to respond to the 
attacks, but European nations are reluctant to do so as they seek to avoid a 
cycle of escalation with Russia at a time when they are unsure of US support 
for NATO.
