Bullish on Anthropic

A four-day losing streak for tech stocks? Apparently not a problem for the 
Anthropic IPO.

Yes, crypto futures tied to the AI lab’s stock market debut have fallen 
alongside AI stocks, but they still implya blockbuster valuation of about $1.7 
trillion 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/anthropic-crypto-bets-imply-1-7-trillion-value-despite-ai-rout?cmpid=111826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11521>
. That’s well above the $1.4 trillion the company would be worth at the top of 
its proposed IPO price range.

The perpetual futures contracts give investors an early read on speculative 
appetite, though those willing to place the bets aren’t necessarily the ones 
who will buy the shares.

SpaceX’s debut in June showed the limits of that signal. Its shares opened at 
$150, above the $135 offer price but well below the roughly $176 indicated by 
crypto futures.

Anthropic’s trading debut tomorrow will give investors another chance to see 
how close the crypto market comes to the opening price.

“The perps market is still positioned for a pop, just a smaller one than it 
was pricing a week ago,” said Kelly Ye of Talos.

The Bank of Nvidia

Nvidia, in the post-Jensen Huang era, is addressing one big criticism 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/the-bank-of-nvidia-is-becoming-more-disciplined-and-selective?cmpid=111826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11521>
 from skeptics of the AI rally. Investors seem to be reassured.

Under the now-ousted CEO, Nvidia provided multibillion-dollar investments and 
financial backstops for AI startups, computing providers and model developers. 
Those same companies were big buyers of Nvidia’s AI chips.

To skeptics, that was unhealthy “circular financing” that was artificially 
inflating the AI boom and sowing the seeds for its collapse.

In a strategy shift, interim CEO Colette Kress yesterday used the terms 
“disciplined” and “rigorous” to describe the greater level of scrutiny Nvidia 
will apply to future investments across the AI supply chain.

Those comments, along with Nvidia’s strong outlook, hit all the right notes 
for bulls: AI demand is humming along, but Nvidia is reassuring the market it 
will avoid financial excesses.

That helps explain why the neocloud stocks are rising this morning, along with 
futures on the Nasdaq-100 and shares of companies that provide data-center 
equipment. —Phil Serafino 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ABoP3GGeTtg/phil-serafino?cmpid=111826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261118&utm_content=11521>
