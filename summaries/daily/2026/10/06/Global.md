**Energy and Inflation Transmission**
The inflationary architecture of the current shock is more complex than 2022's energy crisis in one critical respect: refining capacity has been destroyed, not just crude supply interrupted. Middle Eastern and Russian refinery outages — collateral damage from active conflicts — have helped push diesel above $6.50 per gallon in the US, with American refineries already running at 97% capacity, leaving virtually no buffer against maintenance shutdowns. SocGen's analysis is explicit: if underlying supply does not normalize by year-end, the year-on-year inflationary impulse from diesel may not dissipate until Q3 2027.
