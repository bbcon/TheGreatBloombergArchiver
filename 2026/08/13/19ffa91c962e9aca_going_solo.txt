Ask a Canadian about the looming separatism referendum and the response might 
be: which one?

Prime Minister Mark Carney faces the possibility of governing through not one, 
but two such votes in the foreseeable future.

In the east, the French-speaking province of Quebec has elections this fall 
and the party leading in the polls promises to hold an independence referendum. 
Out west, the oil-rich province of Alberta is staging a non-binding vote in 
October on whether to begin steps towarda full-blown plebiscite 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-23/alberta-premier-smith-stakes-political-future-on-canada-secession-vote?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 on going it alone.

For Quebec, this is nothing new 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/quebec-separatists-are-positioning-themselves-for-another-referendum?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
. It’s held two independence votes since 1980 as it chafes at its place in a 
predominantly English-speaking confederation.

But Alberta’s effort is novel and stems largely from resentment at having its 
energy industry constrained by environmental policies enacted in distant Ottawa.
An Alberta independence town-hall event in Calgary on Jan. 26.Photographer: 
Leah Hennel/Bloomberg
For now, polling in both provinces shows a sizeable majority favors staying a 
part of Canada.

But Carney is taking the threat seriously, especially given his experience of 
governing the Bank of England through the Brexit era. He warned Alberta against
going down the same path 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-25/carney-warns-of-dangerous-bluff-in-alberta-vote-citing-brexit?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
, arguing that 10 years on, the UK is still trying to undo the fallout.

The twin separatist campaigns also complicate Carney’s ability to maneuver 
during thetrade war with Donald Trump 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/canada-races-for-deal-before-trump-s-50-tariff-inflames-feud?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
.

The US president frequently complains about Canada’s protectionism on dairy, 
but those farmers are a key constituency in Quebec.

While oil is Canada’s biggest source of leverage 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/canada-economy-tracking-3-4-second-quarter-annualized-growth?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 with the US, any attempt to use it as a weapon — such as through an export tax 
—risks major backlash 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/carney-rejects-idea-of-using-oil-as-weapon-against-us-tariffs?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 from Alberta.

The Trump factor also works in Carney’s favor.

Canadian national unity has long been challenging, given the country’s vast 
landmass. US insults have had a rally-around-the-flag aspect that hurts the 
separatist cause.

Trump’s threats make the case for sticking together that little bit more 
appealing.— Brian Platt 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVhsUwCy8eU/brian-platt?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>

Global Must Reads

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is increasingly unhappy 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/zelenskyy-is-losing-trust-in-his-central-banker-over-imf-ties?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 with central bank Governor Andrii Pyshnyi, viewing him as being too close to 
the International Monetary Fund and not doing enough to support the wartime 
economy, sources say. Zelenskyy doesn’t plan to remove Pyshnyi, at least for 
now, and doesn’t see strong candidates to replace a governor who’s led the bank 
for most of the war with Russia.

Facing a shortfall of necessary munitions and wary of continuing an unpopular 
war, Trump and his top officials are returning to afamiliar playbook 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/trump-reverts-to-economic-squeeze-of-entrenched-iranian-regime?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 with the Iranian regime: a steady increase in economic sanctions and a naval 
blockade to stifle oil exports. Iran has meanwhile reorganized its military to 
bemore aggressive abroad 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/iran-takes-more-aggressive-military-stance-with-us-talks-frozen?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 as talks on ending the war with the US remain mired in stalemate, a sign that 
Tehran is preparing for a protracted era of regional conflict.

Nigel Farage is about to fight the least challenging electoral test 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/reform-may-defeat-binface-this-week-but-farage-fatigue-is-real?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 of his political career, with pollsters expecting the Reform UK leader to win 
in Clacton, a faded seaside resort town where it’s easy to encounter the kind 
of anti-immigration rhetoric that’s catapulted the right-winger into the 
epicenter of British politics. His main competitor in today’s by-election is a 
satirical candidate dressed as a trash can who claims to be an independent 
space warrior from the planet Sigma IX.

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi issued a protest after Russian 
President Vladimir Putin made his first visit to a territory disputed with 
Japan,underscoring tensions 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/putin-makes-first-visit-to-island-disputed-with-japan-tass-says?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 between the countries. Putin’s visit to Iturup Island, known in Japan as 
Etorofu, came after Tokyo had repeatedly urged him not to go, Takaichi told 
reporters today, adding that his decision “hurts the feelings of the Japanese 
people and is absolutely unacceptable.”

Impressive headline economic numbers haven’t translated into better living 
standards for average Zambian citizens who vote today, leaving some unsure 
whether to give President Hakainde Hichilemaa second term 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/zambia-s-investor-darling-leader-asks-wary-voters-to-reelect-him?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
. Since being elected in 2021, he has shepherded a debt-restructuring plan 
lauded by investors, overseen record copper output in Africa’s second-biggest 
producer, and turned the kwacha into the continent’s best-performing currency 
this year.

Colombia’s new conservative government is partnering with Washington to fight 
drug gangs 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/hegseth-says-colombia-approves-joint-military-operations-with-us?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 in its territory, part of an aggressive US push to coordinate joint military 
strikes on so-called “narco-terrorism” across the Americas.

Opposition disruptions dominated India’s monsoon session of parliament as

widespread student protests,adding to pressure 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/india-student-protests-disrupt-parliament-put-modi-on-defensive?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 on Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s government.

Chart of the Day

Xi Jinping’s corruption crackdown has long fueled political gossip in China, 
and now an underground market is emerging for selling purported secrets on who 
might benext to fall 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/xi-s-secretive-graft-purges-spawn-underground-market-for-leaks?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
. Across the nation’s tightly controlled internet, authorities are playing 
whack-a-mole to stop shadowy users who peddle access to groups claiming to leak 
details of which officials are under scrutiny. The practice underscores the 
sense of anxiety among officials as the president’s anti-graft purges reach 
into new corners of the system.

And Finally

When Trump met with Takaichi in March, little attention was paid to their 
talks about a tiny Japanese island in the Pacific Ocean barely big enough for 
human visitors. Minamitorishima lies over 1,000 miles southeast of Tokyo, 
astride deep-sea rare-earth deposits that could “meet centuries’ worth of 
industrial demand,” according to a US-Japan statement. Mining the minerals will 
allow the allies to reduce reliance on China, yet at around six kilometers (3.7 
miles) beneath the ocean’s surface, they are alsosome of the deepest 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-12/us-japan-plan-world-s-deepest-undersea-mine-to-challenge-china?cmpid=BBD081326_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260813&utm_content=7679>
 considered for extraction.
