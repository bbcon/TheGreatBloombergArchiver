**Monetary Policy and Sovereign Risk**
The ECB's hold was fully anticipated, but the policy context has become more complex. French sovereign spreads have risen to levels last seen during the 2011 Greek debt crisis, though the underlying dynamics differ materially — this reflects fiscal credibility concerns around France's large and widely-held debt stock rather than an acute refinancing crisis. Officials have acknowledged the bond market selloff will tighten financial conditions, partially substituting for rate action. Lagarde's early departure removes an important anchor of continuity at a sensitive juncture.

**UK Fiscal Policy**
Prime Minister Andy Burnham's debut budget achieved its primary objective of avoiding an adverse gilt market reaction. The plan avoided broad tax increases while introducing a lower capital gains rate for entrepreneurs. However, analysts noted it leaves limited fiscal headroom given rising energy costs and the prospect of further rate hikes. The UK separately proposed measures to cushion households from higher energy bills while cutting £1 billion from home-efficiency programs — an internally inconsistent combination that may complicate the medium-term energy transition.
