Deep Dive: German Automakers’ China Problem

After decades of winning over Chinese buyers with its German engineering, 
Mercedes-Benztook the humbling step 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly?cmpid=081126_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260811&utm_content=7585>
 last fall of partnering with fast-food chain McDonald’s to hype its newest car.

 * In the “So Mc-Benz” campaign, Mercedes allowed a cheeseburger figurine to 
take the spot of its traditional three-pointed star on the all-electric CLA, a 
sedan it hoped would stop sales from sliding in the world’s largest car market. 
The ads were supposed to make the vehicle popular with China’s young and hip.
 * It didn’t work. Mercedes sold only 1,153 units in China in the first half, 
a fraction of the more than 80,000 similarly priced SU7 sedans Xiaomi delivered 
in the period.
 * The performance echoes the challenges BMW, Volkswagen and Porsche face in 
China. They have all reported second-quarter sales declines of at least 30%, 
worse than the overall market’s drop.
