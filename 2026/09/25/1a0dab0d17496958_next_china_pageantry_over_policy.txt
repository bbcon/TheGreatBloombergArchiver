Hi, this is Nectar Gan in Washington, where Chinese President Xi Jinping has 
just wrapped up his first state visit to the US in 11 years.

Throughout his trip, which lasted less than 48 hours, it’s been striking to 
watch President Donald Trumppull out all the stops 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-xi-trump-state-visits/?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
 for the leader of America’s chief geopolitical, economic, and technological 
rival.

When Xi arrived, Trump greeted him on the tarmac — an unprecedented 
presidential airport welcome for a Chinese leader.
Xi’s arrival at Joint Base Andrews on September 23.Photographer: Al 
Drago/Bloomberg
The next day, Trump welcomed Xi to the White House, giving him a tour and, at 
one point, making him burst into laughter by taking a jab at former US 
President Joe Biden over a framed autopen display. Trump also showed off his 
new helipad and even brought Xi aboard Marine One, the presidential helicopter. 
Earlier, Xi announced thattwo pandas will arrive 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/two-pandas-to-arrive-in-atlanta-in-a-few-days-xi-says?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
 in the US soon — a sign of stabilizing relations.
Trump giving Xi a tour of the White House’s new helipad and the presidential 
helicopter.Photographer: Annabelle Gordon/CNP/Bloomberg via Consolidated News 
Photos
That evening, over a candle-lit dinner, Trump presented Xi a statue of a bald 
eagle and invited him and other guests to inspect his still-unfinished 
ballroom. To cap it all off, the Republican leader toasted his guest with a 
drink steeped in historical symbolism — a sparkling wine from the same vineyard 
that supplied refreshment during President Richard Nixon’s historic visit to 
Beijing in 1972 that ended more than two decades of estrangement between the 
two countries.

It was a carefully chosen nod to history. But this summit was never going to 
produce the kind of fundamental shift in relations ushered in by Nixon’s visit 
more than half a century ago. Instead, what Xi sought was a commitment from his 
American counterpart to stay the course.
Trump during the state dinner for Xi.Photographer: Annabelle 
Gordon/CNP/Bloomberg via Consolidated News Photos
For Beijing, that means preserving what Xi calls “constructive strategic 
stability” — avoiding steps China regards as destabilizing, be it new tariffs, 
tighter chip export controls or strengthening US support for Taiwan, including 
arms sales.

The question is how much reassurance Xi has actually secured from a US leader 
facing the prospect of becoming a lame-duck president after the November 
midterm elections.

So far, the most concrete summit outcome was a two-month extension to their 
trade truce, announced by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent shortly after Xi 
landed. But even that fell short of the three- to six-month range US officials
previously 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/greer-says-us-china-to-continue-talks-on-trade-truce-extension?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
floated 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/greer-says-us-china-to-continue-talks-on-trade-truce-extension?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
. Xi suggested a “new joint agreement” had emerged from trade talks, but 
offered no details.

Notably, Xi used his meeting with Trump to push the US to outright “oppose” 
Taiwan independence 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/xi-presses-trump-to-oppose-taiwan-independence-during-summit?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
 — language that would mark a significant change from Washington’s longstanding 
position. US ambassador to ChinaDavid Perdue <bbg://people/profile/3844119> 
clarified that Trump hadn’t changed his position on Taiwan, but the US 
president himself hasn’t publicly addressed Xi’s request.
Trump, Xi and the first ladies chat over tea on the last day of the summit.
Photographer: Jacquelyn Martin/AP Photo
On the final morning of his visit, Xi did adopt the American addition to the 
language of strategic stability, saying the relationship should be “based on 
respect, fairness and reciprocity.” What those words mean in practice — and 
what each side believes the other must do to uphold them — will help determine 
how durable this fragile trade truce proves to be.

By the time Xi and Trump bid farewell on the steps of the National Archives, 
neither government had announced a detailed package of summit outcomes, 
including any agreement on how to manage the growing safety risks of artificial 
intelligence. Even before the summit started, Trump hadruled out any efforts <> 
to put guardrails around the technology.
Xi caps off his state visit on September 25 with a stop at the US National 
Archives.Photographer: Daniel Heuer/Bloomberg
Still, the two leaders will have another chance to pick up where they left off 
before the year ends. They’reexpecting to meet again 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/trump-hosts-xi-for-tea-with-final-day-of-summit-underway?cmpid=BBD092526_CN&utm_campaign=china&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260925&utm_content=9282>
 at the APEC summit in China in November and the G20 summit in Miami the 
following month.

The key question is whether those next meetings can produce more substance to 
match the pageantry.

— Nectar Gan
