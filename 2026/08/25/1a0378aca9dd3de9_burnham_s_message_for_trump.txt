Deep Dive: Europe’s Heat Reshapes Stock Bets

Europe’s devastating summer heat wave may have finally broken, but money 
managers say they’re still coming to terms with howclimate change will affect 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/europe-s-record-heat-is-splitting-stock-winners-from-losers?cmpid=082526_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8143>
 will affect their portfolios. The impact is becoming clearer among individual 
companies.

 * Recent filings by global companies contained more mentions of extreme heat 
than ever before, according to an analysis by BloombergNEF. Pictet Asset 
Management says the firm has been fielding calls from clients about the risks 
and opportunities posed by the extreme heat.
 * The challenge for investors now is to identify the firms most exposed, and 
those that stand to benefit.
 * So far, markets have treated the Rhine’s all-time low water levels as a 
weather story rather than a credit story, but “the scale of disruption suggests 
otherwise,” wrote Laura Cooper, global investment strategist at Nuveen.
 * Bloomberg Intelligence estimates extreme weather will drive more than $20 
trillion in global spending in the next decade.
