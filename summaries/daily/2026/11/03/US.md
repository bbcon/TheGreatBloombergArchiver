• US ISM Manufacturing PMI rose to 55.5 in October, beating consensus and reaching its highest level since late 2021, with new orders remaining firmly expansionary.
• US midterm elections underway; Democrats widely expected to take the House, with Senate control more uncertain — a split result would end unified Republican government.
• Apple shares fell 3.7% premarket after the company guided for thinner-than-projected profit margins; the Nasdaq 100 has entered correction territory, down 10.4% from its peak.
• Fiscal pressure looms: debt ceiling expected to be breached in early 2027, with a divided Congress making bipartisan agreement both necessary and uncertain.

**Manufacturing and Economic Data**
October's ISM Manufacturing index rose one point to 55.5, beating consensus and posting its best production reading since late 2021. New orders remained well within expansionary territory, suggesting the industrial economy retains momentum despite broader uncertainty around AI investment cycles and geopolitical risk. The print offers a constructive counterpoint to the equity market turbulence dominating near-term sentiment.

**Monetary Policy and Fiscal Outlook**
With the Fed's December rate decision approaching — markets continue to price a hike under Chair Kevin Warsh — the more consequential medium-term risk may be fiscal. Goldman Sachs and Citigroup analysts both assess it as unlikely that a lame-duck Republican Congress will resolve the debt ceiling before losing its majority. A divided government arriving in January faces two hard deadlines: a government funding expiration on December 11 and a Treasury borrowing limit breach projected in mid-2027. Analysts at Goldman note the most probable resolution involves a "win-win" spending deal that boosts both defense and non-defense outlays — an outcome that leans inflationary and should concern fixed income investors.

**Equity Markets**
The Nasdaq 100's 10.4% drawdown from its peak reflects a confluence of pressures: Midterm uncertainty, Apple's margin warning, Palantir's weaker sales outlook, and oil-driven inflation anxiety. S&P 500 futures were modestly lower as trading commenced.
