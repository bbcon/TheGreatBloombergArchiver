• China's economy weakened broadly in July: industrial output slowed to 4.5% YoY, retail sales growth eased to 0.6%, and fixed-asset investment fell 6.7% YoY over seven months — all missing forecasts.

**Growth Slowdown Complicates BOJ's Yen Defense**
Japan's growth miss underscores the bind facing policymakers: the yen, near a 40-year low, argues for higher rates, while weak consumption and capex argue against tightening. A former vice finance minister said the BOJ should hike "at every meeting" toward 2% to narrow the gap with US rates. China's slowdown compounds the concern, with ING's Lynn Song calling it "a slate of disappointing data across the board."

**Currency Pressures and Regional Ripples**
Japan is not alone: the Philippines and Indonesia face similar tradeoffs, even as Indonesia's Q2 growth beat estimates. Rising Japanese bond yields reflect building rate-hike and fiscal concerns. Trump's abrupt curtailment of US-South Korea drills, paired with praise for Kim Jong Un, adds fresh uncertainty for allies already unsettled by the shift of US military resources toward the Middle East.
