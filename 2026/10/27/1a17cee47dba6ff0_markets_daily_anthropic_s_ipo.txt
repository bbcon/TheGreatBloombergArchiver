The new AI barometer

Investors have spent the past 24 hours pulling apart Anthropic’s 335-page IPO 
prospectus, dissecting everything from itsconvoluted corporate governance 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/anthropic-s-seven-founders-are-worth-more-than-13-billion-each?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
 to itssobering list of risk factors 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/anthropic-warns-of-existential-risk-liability-and-slowing-sales?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
.

How the company fares when it prices its shares next month 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/anthropic-ipo-explained-s-1-filing-nasdaq-listing-and-key-risks?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
 will be closely watched by Wall Street.The company is in a class of one — a 
giant pure-play bet on the future of frontier AI.

On the one hand, its public market performance might cement or capsize 
confidence in AI at a moment when investors are anxious about the borrowing 
binge underpinning it. But equally, you can make the case that Anthropic will 
be a reflection of that confidence, that the fate of its listing hangs on 
investors’ fickle, ever-shifting view of what may be a bubble in the making.

More than any other companies, Anthropic and OpenAI have set the pace for 
global AI development. Anthropic has also put itself at the center ofa web of 
interconnected investments 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/anthropic-ipo-filing-cites-reliance-on-google-amazon-microsoft?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
 that are helping to prop up the entire sector and the US economy.

“This will really be investors’ first significant opportunity to invest 
directly in the company that is driving the AI economy,” said Gil Luria of DA 
Davidson. “It’s of monumental importance.” —Phil Serafino with Shirin Ghaffary 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWgQvzatnYQ/shirin-ghaffary?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>

Billions needed

Speaking of AI’s circular investments, here’s what’s been happening:

 * Meta sold $25 billion of common stock and $12.5 billion of preferred 
shares, and said it plans to raise as much as $60 billion more. The cash will 
be used for AI spending.
 * Broadcom arranged a $63 billion deal 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/broadcom-arranges-63-billion-deal-to-finance-anthropic-s-chips?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
 with investment firms to finance the purchase of chips for Anthropic.
 * SpaceX signed a roughly $40 billion equipment-finance lease 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/spacex-agrees-to-40-billion-chip-financing-deal-with-apollo?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
 with Apollo to pay for more chips.
Bulls are undeterred by the relentless demand for financing. The AI-driven 
debt boom is creatingsome of the most attractive fixed-income opportunities in 
years 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-27/blackrock-s-rieder-spent-all-weekend-doing-deals-in-ai-debt-rush?cmpid=102726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261027&utm_content=10595>
, eclipsing the appeal of long-dated US Treasuries, said Rick Rieder, 
BlackRock’s chief investment officer for global fixed income.

“I’ve never seen anything like it in terms of the size of the financing that’s 
coming in both debt markets, equity markets,” he said on Bloomberg Television. 
“This is one of the most exciting investment periods I’ve ever seen.”
