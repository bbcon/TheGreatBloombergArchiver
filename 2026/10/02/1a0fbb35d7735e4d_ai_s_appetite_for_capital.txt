The companies at the heart of the AI boom are testing the limits of investor 
demand to continue feeding their insatiable appetite for capital.

Broadcom is looking to raise $60 billion of fresh AI chip financing to benefit 
Anthropic and other companies, according to people with knowledge of the 
matter. Banks involved in themassive debt package 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/broadcom-starts-amassing-60-billion-to-fund-chips-for-anthropic?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261002&utm_content=9635>
 — led by Blackstone — are poised to send out syndication letters for a $42 
billion Class A senior-secured tranche, the people said. The deal comes as 
Anthropic closes in on an IPO that could overshadow the SpaceX listing in May.

Amazon is exploring a more asset-light model to mitigate the financial demands 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/amazon-seeks-to-move-8-billion-of-chips-off-its-books-ft-says?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261002&utm_content=9635>
 of the buildout. The company is exploring a deal to shift about $8 billion of 
Nvidia’s top-end chips off its balance sheet, according to the Financial Times. 
The move would see Amazon offload thousands of Grace Blackwell chips across 
more than a dozen US data centers into a special-purpose vehicle funded by 
outside investors, the paper said, citing people familiar with the matter.

One company joining the wave of massive fundraising 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/firmus-eyes-30-billion-valuation-in-australian-ipo-afr-reports?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261002&utm_content=9635>
 with a more traditional approach is Australian data center operator Firmus 
Grid. The Nvidia-backed company has priced its IPO at A$11 a share as it seeks 
to raise at least $5 billion this month. The price implies a valuation of about 
$30 billion, which would make it one of the largest listings in Australia.
