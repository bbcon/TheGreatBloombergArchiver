It all started so well. After initially surging on its hotly anticipated 
debut, Anthropic is nowheading for a drop 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/anthropic-shares-drop-post-market-on-apple-s-ai-agent-plans?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261120&utm_content=11659>
 on its second day of trading as the company gets swept up in the rush to sell 
everything.

The Claude chatbot maker’s shares were down about 6% on the Tradegate exchange 
and by a similar margin on Hyperliquid. Taking their cue from Anthropic’s slide,
chip stocks 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/asian-chip-stocks-slide-as-anthropic-ipo-fails-to-dispel-ai-woes?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261120&utm_content=11659>
 slumped across Asia, with key tech indexes hitting a string of bearish 
milestones. Taiwan’s Taiex capped itsworst week since 1991 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/taiwan-stocks-taiex-twse-1991-ai-chips-tsmc-hon-hai-foxconn?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261120&utm_content=11659>
, while South Korea’s benchmark posted an 8% weekly loss. And while AI shares 
had previously born the brunt of the selloff, the declines broadened across 
sectors as investors fled to the safety of bonds. Copper sank to itslowest 
level since March 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/copper-rout-deepens-as-ai-selloff-wreaks-havoc-in-metals-markets?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261120&utm_content=11659>
 fueled by growing doubts about the strength of the AI buildout.

AI Cloud CEO Hits ‘Rock Bottom’ in Race to Endure Chip Curbs

In little more than a month, Aolani Cloud CEO Nicholas Chia has watched his 
industry go from boom to dangerously close to bust. Just weeks ago, the 
neocloud firm was offered term sheets at a $3 billion valuation — up from $400 
million earlier this year — and signing letters of intent to build data centers 
across Asia. Now, Chia says valuations for Aolani and other neoclouds have 
likely halved after the US government targeted some of its key markets with 
chip export restrictions that have sent shockwaves through the global supply 
chain.
