China’s Economy Disappoints With New Stimulus Yet to Kick In

Beyond the bright summit lights, China’s growth weakened across the board in 
October as investment unexpectedly worsened while consumption and industrial 
output undershot estimates, in a sign Beijing’s latest stimulus measures have 
yet to feed through to the economy.
Read more 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/china-s-economy-weakens-more-than-forecast-despite-stimulus-push?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
A mixed bag of economic report cards was apparent elsewhere in Asia. 
Thailand’s growth accelerated 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/thai-gdp-growth-accelerates-to-2-9-on-ai-missing-estimates?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
 to 2.9% in the third quarter, with the AI boom powering exports while masking 
persistent weakness in household spending and tourism.Japan’s economy contracted
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/japan-s-economy-contracts-as-bad-weather-weighs-on-consumption?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
 for the first time in a year as a spate of inclement weather weighed on 
consumer spending, highlighting the fragility of domestic demand. The nation’s 
real GDP shrank 0.1% on an annualized basis in the third quarter compared to 
the previous three months.

Markets started the week pessimistic. Technology stocks plunged 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
 ahead of this week’s earnings from Nvidia and an expected listing by 
Anthropic, two events that are likely to influence the near-term course of an 
increasingly fragile AI trade.Asia’s two chip-heavy stock markets 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/nvidia-fallout-anthropic-listing-squeeze-asian-chip-stocks-anew?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
 came under renewed pressure, with Taiwan’s benchmark Taiex and South Korea’s 
Kospi gauge sliding.

But there was a contrarian view. Morgan Stanley strategists recommended a “max 
overweight” position in global stocks 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/morgan-stanley-max-overweight-global-stocks-on-intact-ai-cycle?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
, citing an intact AI capital spending cycle that will extend resilient 
economic and earnings growth into next year. “With risk assets having 
cheapened, we embrace risk,” Morgan Stanley’s strategists wrote in the report. 
“Within global equities, the story is broadening out beyond hyperscalers and 
2027 will increasingly be about returns for AI adopters.”

New World Shares Plunge After $3.2 Billion Rights Issue Plan

New World Development’s share price plunged after the Hong Kong property 
company said it plans to raise around HK$24.9 billion through a heavily 
discounted rights issue, fueling questions about its financial health. “To me, 
pricing new shares at an 82% discount is a sign of desperation,” said Anna Wu, 
an investment strategist and founder of Floodlines Research. The rights issue 
is New World’s latest attempt to address its financial problems, which have 
weighed heavily on its shares. But the announcement also offered investors 
proof that the company’s founding Cheng family will make good on its previous 
pledge to give support — albeit at a price.

Alibaba launched a new flagship AI model, claiming performance that rivals 
Anthropic and OpenAI inChina’s latest challenge to US leaders 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/alibaba-unveils-new-flagship-ai-model-in-riposte-to-us-rivals?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
. The e-commerce giant-turned-AI player said its Qwen4-Max model scored near 
the top of coding benchmarks, outperforming Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1 as 
well as homegrown rival Moonshot’s Kimi K3.1 Pro. Alibaba did not make the 
model open weight, which would allow users to download and customize it, but 
said it would make smaller versions of the series available soon.

The Indonesian currency fell to a fresh record low, pressured by a stronger 
dollar amid hawkish Federal Reserve expectations, lingering worries over 
domestic political stability and a potential market downgrade. The rupiah
dropped as much as 0.4% to 18,271 per dollar 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/idr-usd-indonesian-rupiah-prabowo-fed-bets-boost-dollar?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
 on Monday, having weakened about 9% this year. Indonesian authorities may
struggle to put a floor under Asia’s worst-performing currency 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/idr-usd-indonesian-rupiah-may-fall-further-past-record-low-says-analysts?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
, according to strategists.

Meanwhile, the Taiwan dollar’s slide toward its weakest level in a decade is 
confounding the normal rules of currency trading 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/new-taiwan-dollar-currency-paradox-ai-boom-twd-nt-tsmc-hon-hai?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11452>
. On paper, the case for a stronger currency is almost overwhelming. The island 
is riding its longest streak of double-digit quarterly economic growth in 
nearly 40 years, while running the fourth-largest current-account surplus in 
the world. Yet the currency is inching toward its weakest level since 2016 as 
mountains of export earnings pile up abroad.
