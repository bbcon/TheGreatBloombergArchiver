**Deficit Financing and the Consumer**
Elevated yields reflect investors demanding greater compensation for a growing fiscal deficit, a headache for Bessent ahead of November's midterms. Gavekal's Tan Kai Xian warned that once the saving rate hits zero, consumers must borrow or sell assets to sustain spending; with delinquencies low and no sign of re-leveraging, his base case is asset sales, pressuring consumption if valuations soften.

**Markets and Corporates**
Equities paused after closing at a record. Reddit jumped 12% ahead of its August 18 S&P 500 inclusion, while Applied Materials fell 5.3% on a tepid outlook reaction. BofA said Republican midterm gains would extend the rally, and Wells Fargo's Mike Mayo flagged banks as key AI beneficiaries. Thrive Capital's fund grew sevenfold to $3.7 billion on early OpenAI bets.
