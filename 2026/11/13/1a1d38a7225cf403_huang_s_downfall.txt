Vladimir Putin’s war in Ukraine, with businesses transferred to local managers 
spendingbillions of rubles 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/foreign-businesses-putin-seized-help-fund-russia-s-war-machine?cmpid=111326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261113&utm_content=11378>
 on equipment for Russian troops.

 * Everything for victory: Most of the money has gone to the People’s Front, 
an organization founded at Putin’s initiative. Donations have funded drones, 
vehicles, communications equipment and electronic-warfare systems, with the 
Russian units of Rockwool, AkzoNobel and DKC among those contributing.
 * Caught in the crossfire: Russia’s mechanism lets the state take operational 
control of businesses from so-called unfriendly countries while their foreign 
parents retain formal ownership. European and Japanese companies have been 
targeted, while major US businesses have largely been spared as Moscow seeks 
warmer ties with the Trump administration.
 * Seizures accelerate: The Kremlin has stepped up takeovers since September, 
including Nestlé and Japan Tobacco units. Putin has described the seizures as a 
deterrent against Western moves on Russian assets, while Russian businessmen 
are increasingly jockeying for control of foreign companies that could be 
targeted next.
