Nvidia crushes it

Nvidia’s stellar results are reinforcing investor confidence in the AI 
revolution 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/nvidia-estimate-topping-forecast-fails-to-wow-investors?cmpid=082726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260827&utm_content=8238>
, with CEO Jensen Huang saying the “golden age” of labs and startups is here.

Here’s the latest:

 * Sales surged. It sees revenue in the current period at $108 billion. 
Compared with a year ago, that’s close to double.
 * There’s no let up in demand. Nvidia said it would be growing even faster if 
it had access to more supplies.
 * Hyperscalers spend big. The data center division beat estimates, with firms 
like Alphabet and Amazon accounting for much of those sales.
 * Margins will narrow. Nvidia warned that margins would shrink slightly in 
the coming months as it copes with a surge in memory costs. 
At the current price, Nvidia is poised to add about $370 billion of market 
value when trading begins. The shares are up 12% this year.

“Death, taxes, and Nvidia beats on earnings are three things we can always 
count on in life,” says Ryan Detrick of Carson Group. “It is clear the AI wave 
is far from over, as demand continues to expand more and more.” —Lynn Thomasson

Gold + Bitcoin

Investors are no longer choosing between gold and Bitcoin as hedges against 
fiscal anxiety.They’re buying both. 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/bitcoin-and-gold-etfs-draw-7-billion-as-scarcity-trade-surges?cmpid=082726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260827&utm_content=8238>

ETFs tracking the assets attracted a record $7 billion over the past five 
trading days. The asset are moving together again, propelled by renewed anxiety 
over US borrowing, the dollar and efforts to contain long-term yields.

Precious metals are benefiting from their haven status, while cryptocurrencies 
are seen by some as a refuge from the impacts of government policy.

“Now we are back to the core story of Bitcoin and it’s fundamental case. This 
is what Bitcoin is born to do,” said Eric Balchunas of Bloomberg Intelligence.—
