Javier Milei is betting that Donald Trump’s new world order is here to stay — 
and that Argentina has picked the prevailing side.

As oil production nears off the British-controlled Falkland Islands, Milei is 
escalating claims to the archipelago 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/argentina-s-milei-revives-bitter-dispute-with-uk-over-falklands?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
 over which Argentina fought and lost a war with the UK in 1982.
Argentina’s players hold a banner reading “The Malvinas (Falkland Islands) 
belong to Argentina,” after beating England in 2026 World Cup football 
semi-final on July 15.Photographer: Paul Ellis/AFP/Getty Images
His assertions, made in a late night address last week, came amid low approval 
ratings for the Argentine leader seeking to flip the script. Theeconomy is 
losing momentum 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/argentina-economy-milei-magic-fades-on-slowing-recovery?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
 and Milei’s cabinet chief resigned in June amid corruption allegations.

The libertarian is now seizing the moment after Trump said the US is 
re-evaluating itshistorically neutral position 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/uk-rebuffs-milei-on-falklands-vows-to-uphold-british-control?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
 on the islands.

“Winds of change favorable to our claim are sweeping the world,” Milei told 
the nation.

Trump pointed to the UK’s limited support for Washington in its war with Iran. 
But his comments are in line with efforts to exert US controlacross the western 
hemisphere 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/trump-fails-to-curb-china-s-economic-influence-in-latin-america?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
.

Milei, a core Trump ally, is putting his money where his mouth is. He 
announced plans to develop an integrated naval base in Tierra del Fuego, 
Argentina’s southernmost province and a gateway to Antarctica.

He also said he’ll tighten sanctions against companies that drill for oil 
without Argentine permission.

Yet the order Milei envisions may be fleeting, with US midterm elections in 
November threatening to weaken Trump’s hand.

It may also test some of the Argentine president’s other bromances.

Milei recorded his TV speech hours after visiting neighboring Chile and fellow 
Trump-supporting leader, José Antonio Kast.

Just as Milei moves to isolate companies 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/milei-ally-eduardo-elsztain-defends-his-falkland-islands-investment?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
 doing business with the Falklands, a Chilean shipping company is preparing a 
new maritime route to the islands. The government in Santiago is signaling no 
contradiction in supporting Argentina’s claim while fostering private trade and 
transport links.

As with Trump, Milei may not get everything his way. — Manuela Tobias

heating up again. Both of Asia’s top financial hubs are mulling tax breaks for 
fund managers, the latest move in a decades-old game of one-upmanship to keep 
high-earning finance jobs. In this story we delve into big questions like how

And Finally

A tech shift in China that started in 2022 — known as dongshu xisuan, or “Data 
in the East, Computing in the West” — is already impacting the distribution of
data-center projects 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/china-races-to-build-ai-data-centers-far-away-from-its-biggest-cities?cmpid=BBD090826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260908&utm_content=8640>
. According to new research published by BloombergNEF, more than half of the 
country’s data project pipeline is now across the north and northwest, where 
both space and inexpensive green energy are abundant. DeepSeek and Z.AI are 
among the Chinese pioneers erecting giant centers of a gigawatt or more in 
Inner Mongolia, racing to put in place the infrastructure they need to power AI 
development.
