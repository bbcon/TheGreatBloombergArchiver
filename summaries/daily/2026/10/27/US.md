**Monetary Policy and Rates**
The Federal Reserve's Wednesday policy decision dominated trader attention, with the 10-year Treasury yield at 4.99%. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has previously highlighted inflation breadth as a concern; Fitch Ratings data show 46% of US CPI components running above 3% year-on-year in September, double the 2018–2019 average. This broad-based price pressure may reinforce the case for a higher-for-longer stance.

**AI Capital Markets**
The scale of AI-related financing activity on Monday was striking. Goldman Sachs co-led Meta's roughly $98 billion equity offering, sole-structured a $40 billion SpaceX chip financing facility, and was named lead bookrunner on Anthropic's IPO. Broadcom separately arranged a $63 billion deal to finance chip purchases for Anthropic. BlackRock CIO Rick Rieder described the AI debt opportunity as eclipsing long-dated Treasuries in attractiveness. Whether Anthropic's eventual pricing confirms or challenges broader AI valuations remains the key near-term signal for the sector.

**Fiscal Backdrop**
The US fiscal deficit is tracking above 6% of GDP for a fourth consecutive year — structurally elevated despite low unemployment. Rising debt-servicing costs have pushed interest payments above $1 trillion annually, and the current political configuration offers limited near-term prospects for consolidation.
