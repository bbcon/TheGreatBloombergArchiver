US Signals Frustration at Slow Pace of Rare Earth Exports From China

The top US diplomat in China indicated that Washington is frustrated 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/us-envoy-to-china-says-relations-are-bumpy-after-nvidia-affair?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 about the flow of rare earth shipments from China, saying companies are still 
waiting for them and criticizing Beijing for weaponizing its dominance in 
strategic industries.

“We’ve been waiting for months on some of these very important elements that 
go into data centers and aircraft engine manufacturing and so forth,” 
Ambassador David Perdue told Bloomberg Television inan interview 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-11/us-ambassador-to-china-on-us-china-relations-video?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 at the Citi China Conference in Shanghai on Wednesday.

He said the US is trying to regain 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/rare-earth-strategic-metals-price-gap-flags-supply-strains-trump-xi-meet?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 some economic independence “so we can’t be coerced.”

Perdue said he was optimistic that both sides would find a way to cooperate on 
areas of disagreement. Though ties are currently stable under a trade truce 
that expires in January, he said the controversy over Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s 
affair with an alleged spy wastesting relations 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/nvidia-board-members-tap-wachtell-lipton-to-probe-ceo-affair?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 between the world’s two largest economies.

Related: Nvidia’s Bull Case Hinges on CEO Huang Surviving China Spy Probe 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/nvidia-s-bull-case-hinges-on-ceo-huang-surviving-china-spy-probe?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>

“The situation with Nvidia is another reminder that we have to stay diligent,” 
Perdue said. “National security is part of this economic conversation because 
economic security is national security. And so, what we’re doing with China is 
bumpy right now.”
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-11/us-ambassador-to-china-on-us-china-relations-video?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
Separately and without mentioning the Nvidia situation, Treasury Secretary 
Scott Bessenthailed an improvement 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/bessent-hails-us-china-engagement-sees-more-talk-on-ai-models?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 in US-China engagement ahead of President Donald Trump’s visit to the Asian 
nation next week, and said he expects further dialogue on artificial 
intelligence models.

“Never before have our leaders engaged face-to-face so often in a single 
year,” Bessent said in remarks in New York Tuesday evening, where the Chinese 
ambassador also spoke. “More significant is the understanding they are helping 
to establish.”

Explainer: Jensen Huang, Nvidia and the China Spying Controversy 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/what-to-know-about-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-s-affair-with-suspected-chinese-spy?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>

Bessent highlighted that Trump and Xi have “established a new dialogue” on 
“super intelligence” — using Trump’s preferred term for AI. Theengagement 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/bessent-greer-raise-concerns-on-china-trade-deal-actions?cmpid=BBD111126_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11147>
 is “designed to guard against shared risks and prevent a misunderstanding 
between our two systems.”

Xie Feng, China’s ambassador to Washington, said in his remarks to the event 
that “joint efforts of China and the United States are indispensable” with 
regard to improving AI governance. That’s also the case for “advancing the 
political settlement of hotspot issues,” Xie said.
