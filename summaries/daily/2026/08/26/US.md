**Treasury Market**
The "Bessent put" shows tangible, if modest, signs of working. Traders say the knowledge of a large buyer has made them reluctant to press yields higher, and Citadel's Frank Flight reversed a prior bearish call to see conditions ripe for a bond rally. Skeptics remain: Stanley Druckenmiller, an early Bessent mentor, called the purchases a mistake, and the 30-year yield still sits just 15 basis points from its highest since 2007. Richmond Fed President Tom Barkin separately warned rising federal debt will eventually force a "reckoning."

**AI Trade and Earnings**
Ahead of results, SoftBank is weighing a bond sale of up to $20 billion to help repay a bridge loan tied to its OpenAI stake, underscoring how AI financing competes with sovereign issuance for capital. Barclays has flagged the AI trade's exposure to voter backlash over data-center buildout, and options markets show rising anxiety into the midterms.
