of the Houthis, dismissed by many as ragtag militia.

polite,” recalled Benomar, who said he was the only diplomat known to have met

East’s most consequential and enigmatic figures.
 * Al-Houthi presides over a militant group that taken over parts of Yemen, 
brutally suppressing the people under its control with their reach extending
