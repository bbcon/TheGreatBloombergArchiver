**Currency and Commodity Pressures**
The combination of a weaker dollar trend against commodity currencies and elevated global oil prices is creating divergent pressures across the region. Coffee and banana exporters — concentrated in Colombia, Ecuador, and Central America — are facing reduced dollar revenues as the greenback softens relative to local currencies, compressing export margins at a time when input costs remain elevated. The dynamic illustrates the asymmetric exposure Latin American commodity producers face to both energy price shocks and currency volatility simultaneously. Brazil, as both a major commodity exporter and an oil producer, faces a more nuanced trade-off, though political uncertainty ahead of the election cycle is an independent headwind to investor confidence.

**Brazil Political Risk**
The Bolsonaro campaign's operational difficulties are accumulating. New allegations linking the campaign to a bank scandal, combined with a fresh police investigation and the candidate's strained relationship with his broader political family, increase execution risk for what had been a credible opposition candidacy. How this develops over coming weeks matters for Brazilian asset pricing, as political uncertainty tends to translate into real spread widening in a market already sensitive to fiscal dynamics.
