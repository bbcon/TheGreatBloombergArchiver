**Eurozone Inflation and ECB Outlook**

The August CPI print for the euro area firmed the case for ECB tightening at the September meeting. Energy prices, driven by the Iran conflict's impact on oil markets, appear to be the primary accelerant. The ECB faces a difficult balancing act: hiking into slowing growth to address energy-driven inflation risks exacerbating fiscal stress in high-deficit member states.

**UK and France Political Risk**

Prime Minister Burnham returns to Parliament facing a crowded agenda — an October budget, a first Labour conference speech, and a meeting with President Trump — while polling against Reform UK remains uncomfortable. In France, political uncertainty is transmitting directly into sovereign spreads. With the fiscal deficit above 5% of GDP and the presidential field fragmented between Marine Le Pen and Jean-Luc Mélenchon, markets are demanding meaningful additional compensation to hold French paper, reflecting unresolved questions about fiscal consolidation under any plausible successor government.
