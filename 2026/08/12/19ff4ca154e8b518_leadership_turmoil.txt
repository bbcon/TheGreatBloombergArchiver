Deep Dive: Iran Factions Clash Over War Aims
President Masoud Pezeshkian on Aug. 8.Photographer: Iranian Presidential 
Office/AFP/Getty Images
When Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian told state TV last week that he found 
it “very difficult” to reach the country’s Supreme Leader, it sparked fresh 
speculation about the whereabouts of Mojtaba Khamenei who hasn’t been seen in 
public since taking over as the country’s ultimate authority in March.

 * The statement came after weeks of bitter public infighting 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/iranian-factions-squabble-over-war-aims-as-khamenei-looks-to-assert-control?cmpid=081226_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260812&utm_content=7628>
 in Tehran over the conduct of the war, with calls from hardliners for 
Pezeshkian to resign over his support for Iran’s beleaguered peace deal with 
the US, and the under-pressure president refusing to budge.
 * Khamenei’s absence from public life has left a vacuum in which disputes 
between political factions — a mainstay of Iranian life — have become more 
pronounced and seeped into the open.
 * “For now, Tehran believes that holding its ground and imposing costs has 
forced Washington to adjust its objectives,” according to Ali Vaez from the 
International Crisis Group. “The danger is that short-term vindication hardens 
into long-term overconfidence.”
