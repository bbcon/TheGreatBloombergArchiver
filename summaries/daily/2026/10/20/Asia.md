**Equity Markets and AI Sentiment**
The Elice IPO rout crystallized a broader valuation problem in Asian technology. Samsung and SK Hynix — which together account for nearly half of Kospi market capitalization — trade at price-to-earnings ratios of below five and four times respectively, roughly half their decade averages, despite record earnings. Samsung posted $80 billion in quarterly profit last quarter on AI-driven memory demand. The disconnect between fundamentals and multiples reflects investor skepticism about demand durability rather than current results. Catalysts for a re-rating likely require confirmation from US hyperscaler capex guidance — Meta, Microsoft, and Alphabet all report this week.

**Indonesia and Japan**
Indonesia's exchange moved swiftly to remove highly concentrated-ownership companies from benchmark index calculations following an emergency meeting with MSCI, which last week flagged a potential downgrade that would place Indonesia alongside Lebanon and Argentina in standalone classification. Whether these measures satisfy MSCI's concerns remains uncertain. Separately, Japan's 20-year bond auction demand signal is notable: elevated JGB yields appear to be drawing domestic institutional investors back into long-duration assets, a dynamic that could gradually reduce pressure on the Bank of Japan to intervene.
