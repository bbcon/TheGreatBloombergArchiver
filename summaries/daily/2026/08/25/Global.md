**Iran Pressure Shifts From Military to Tariff Leverage**
With munitions shortages constraining further military action, Washington has pivoted to tariffs as its primary lever against Iran, with the new pressure point being Chinese companies rather than the Strait of Hormuz. A campaign that excludes China — the top buyer of Iranian oil — would be largely ineffective, but targeting Beijing directly risks retaliation and broader damage to trade channels ahead of a planned Trump-Xi summit. The US naval blockade of Iranian ports is, separately, proving effective, with laden tankers stranded in the Persian Gulf and Iranian crude stockpiles in East Asia nearly depleted.

**Climate and Trade Frictions Widen**
Copernicus recorded the highest global ocean surface temperature ever measured, 21.1C, as climate change compounds a powerful El Niño, while BloombergNEF data show corporate filings referencing extreme heat at record levels. Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore reaffirmed commitments to keep the Strait of Malacca open amid rising trade tensions.
