Weekend woes

An $800 billion wipeout 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/magnificent-7-loses-767-billion-as-ai-skeptics-dump-tech-stocks?cmpid=072426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6931>
 in Big Tech stocks, Donald Trump’s latest threats against Iran, still-elevated 
oil prices and surging interest rate bets — there’s plenty to keep investors on 
edge going into the weekend, even if markets look like they’re stabilizing for 
now.

Here’s a closer look at some of the moves:

 * Alphabet and Tesla’s spending plans triggered the biggest drop since April 
2025 for a gauge of Magnificent Seven technology stocks. That wiped out almost 
$800 billion in market value on Thursday.
 * The yield on investment-grade government bonds globally has surged to 
3.68%, according to a Bloomberg index,the highest since the global financial 
crisis in 2008 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/global-bonds-are-reeling-as-oil-surge-renews-inflation-threat?cmpid=072426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6931>
. The benchmark is currently set for its biggest monthly loss since March. 
 * Bets on a rate increase at the Fed meeting next week have risen, with the 
market-implied probability now standing at a one-in-three chance.
 * And as investors look to reduce risks ahead of the weekend, Korean 
chipmakers are bearing the brunt 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/korean-stocks-sink-as-chipmakers-drop-on-middle-east-china-ipo?cmpid=072426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6931>
: The benchmark Kospi sank 5.7% today, led by steep losses in Samsung 
Electronics and SK Hynix, each down over 7%.

Chinese chip blockbuster

Another reason Korean stocks are falling: Asian hedge funds may be making room 
for China’s CXMT 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/cxmt-s-frenzy-ignites-hopes-of-futher-hardware-rally-in-china?cmpid=072426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6931>
, which debuts on Monday in the nation’s largest-ever chip offering.

China’s chip-heavy Star 50 Index fell into a bear market this month as funds 
repositioned for the IPO, which values CXMT at $86 billion.Investors hope gains 
in the sector will be revived once trading gets going.

It’s expected to perform well: the IPO was hundreds of times oversubscribed 
and listings on the STAR Board face no daily percentage limit for the first 
five trading sessions. Given CXMT’s heavy state backing as a key component of 
China’s drive for tech self-sufficiency, a big jump could provide a halo effect 
for other local chip stocks. —Jeanny Yu 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASf81kJXKek/jeanny-yu?cmpid=072426_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260724&utm_content=6931>
