**Inflation and Fiscal Trajectory**
The forthcoming September 11 CPI print, expected at 3.4% YoY, reinforces that the Federal Reserve remains well short of its 2% inflation target. The persistence of above-target inflation — now in its fifth year — is occurring against a backdrop of renewed energy price pressures, with US gasoline averaging $4.15 per gallon as of Thursday, the highest level on record for September. The energy impulse is directly linked to the ongoing US-Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz disruption, complicating the Fed's path toward normalization.

**Fiscal Policy**
The fiscal picture remains a source of market concern. Bessent's plan for fiscal consolidation, initially promised imminently, has been delayed by months, and congressional dynamics make any significant budget tightening structurally unlikely. The IMF noted in April that rising Treasury supply is eroding the traditional safety premium embedded in US sovereign debt — a dynamic now visible in 10-year yields trading above 4.75%, the highest in decades. Bessent conducted an out-of-cycle bond buyback program last month, an unusual step that markets interpreted as a signal of stress in the Treasury market's functioning.
