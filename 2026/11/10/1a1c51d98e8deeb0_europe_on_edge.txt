Europe has reason to be nervous about new US-led talks

 beginning in Abu Dhabi today aimed at ending Russia’s war in Ukraine.

Russia wants any deal to include broader security arrangements for Europe, 
something President Donald Trump’s administration is signaling it’s willing to 
consider.

European leaders say they already have a security framework — NATO — and 
there’snothing to discuss 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/europe-insists-putin-end-war-before-discussing-russia-s-concerns?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>

 on the continent.

Trump indicated after meeting Putin envoy Kirill Dmitriev last week that a “
big agreement 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/trump-meets-putin-envoy-kirill-dmitriev-at-the-white-house?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
” that “might involve all of Europe” is under consideration.

His son-in-law Jared Kushner, who’s leading US mediation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/russia-ukraine-envoys-to-talk-ceasefire-monitoring-territory?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 alongside envoy Steve Witkoff, said a lasting settlement will “require direct 
engagement between Russia and our European partners on the broader security 
arrangements that affect them.”

To be sure, Europe and Ukraine want the war that’s in its fifth year to end. 
Russia is bombarding Ukrainian energy, logistics and civilian infrastructure, 
aiming to make life unbearable for people in Kyiv and other cities as winter 
approaches.

Putin shows no sign of abandoning territorial demands in Ukraine, despite 
mounting costs. Confidential data show about 400,000 Russians fighting in 
Ukrainehave died 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/hacked-records-show-400-000-russians-killed-in-war-on-ukraine?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
.

Ukrainian drone and missile attacks are also having a growing impact 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/putin-s-war-in-ukraine-comes-home-to-russia-as-death-toll-mounts?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 on life in Russia.

Ahead of the full-scale invasion, Russia sought to push the US to withdraw 
NATO forces 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2021-12-17/russia-demands-nato-return-to-1997-in-security-treaty-proposals?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 to their positions before 1997, when eastern European states joined the 
alliance. President Joe Biden rejected the demand.

Europeans fear Putin may entice 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/trump-s-weakness-and-overtures-to-russia-put-ukraine-on-edge?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 Trump into weakening US commitment to NATO — an organization the US president 
hasopenly derided 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-15/trump-questions-us-spending-on-nato-criticizes-italy-s-meloni?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 — in return for a peace deal in Ukraine, leaving them to face aresurgent Russia
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/finland-considers-ukraine-style-tank-barriers-as-russia-rearms?cmpid=BBD111026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261110&utm_content=11220>
 alone.

That would let the Kremlin snatch a strategic victory from a war that’s been a
