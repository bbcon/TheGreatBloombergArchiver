When it comes to Chinese AI, every development brings angst along with 
excitement. A flurry of model launches from the country’s AI sector israpidly 
narrowing the gap with Silicon Valley 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
 and making companies — read: US rivals — without frontier-pushing technology 
or market-breaking pricing.

Alibaba became the latest in a parade of Chinese debuts hitting the top of 
global benchmarks with its Qwen3.8-Max this week, its most advanced model to 
date that appeared to match or exceed Anthropic’s flagship Fable 5. Two weeks 
earlier, Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 showed performance comparable to the priciest US 
options built on a much humbler budget, sparking freshquestions over the 
effectiveness of US chip sanctions 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/what-is-moonshot-ai-why-china-s-new-model-is-roiling-markets?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
 intended to slow China’s tech ascent.

And they are not the only ones. What was once an isolated shockwave is now a 
cascade of unexpected depth — delivering not just low-cost alternatives, but 
high-end capabilities in reasoning, coding and complex tasks that give OpenAI 
and its US peers real competition.

On the flip side, China is growing anxious that Anthropic’s Mythos could be 
wielded 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/china-is-getting-more-anxious-about-mythos-before-trump-meets-xi?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
 against the world’s second-biggest economy, adding a volatile new issue to 
already heightened tensions before a planned summit between Xi Jinping and 
Donald Trump. Officials in Beijing areconcerned about the cyber capabilities of 
Mythos 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-04/china-anxious-about-mythos-ahead-of-trump-xi-meeting-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
 and other AI frontier models developed by US companies, seeing the potential 
for them to be used as an offensive weapon, people familiar with the matter 
said.

The race is heating up.

Apple exceeded $10 billion in annual sales in India for the first time last 
fiscal year, underscoring surging demand for its pricey devices in a market 
where it’s steadily ramping up its retail network. Revenue grew at double-digit

iPhones accounted for a significant majority of the sales

Hormuz Traffic at a Trickle as Ship Attacks Heighten Concerns

Observable traffic transiting the Strait of Hormuz remained at a trickle as 
attacks on vessels and Iranian threats heightened safety concerns for 
shipowners and crews considering a trip through the waterway.
Read more 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/hormuz-traffic-at-a-trickle-as-ship-attacks-heighten-concerns?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
Saudi Aramco reported a 33% increase in second-quarter profit as it benefited 
from a war-driven surge in oil prices, while keeping exports flowing through a 
pipeline that bypasses the Strait of Hormuz. Adjusted net income rose to $33.4 
billion, from $25.2 billion a year earlier, beating analysts’ profit estimates 
of $31.1 billion compiled by Bloomberg. Benchmark Brent crude averaged almost 
$97 a barrel during the quarter, as the closure of Hormuz caused the biggest 
oil supply disruption in history. That generated rising profits for Aramco as it
redirected most exports to the Red Sea 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/aramco-profit-jumps-33-as-us-war-with-iran-boosts-oil-prices?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260804&utm_content=7325>
, but now it faces further risks as Yemen’s Houthi group threatens to attack 
tankers using that route.
