**Colombia Earthquake Response**
The earthquake's rising death toll — approaching 170 — has prompted Colombia's government to declare an economic emergency, freeing up fiscal and administrative tools to accelerate relief and reconstruction. The president is using the moment to press forward with a broader reform agenda aimed at strengthening the autonomy and investment of remote regions, framing disaster recovery as an opportunity to address longstanding regional disparities. It remains too early to assess the fiscal cost of reconstruction or the durability of the reform push once the immediate crisis response subsides.
