Deep Dive: Wildfires Rage in Europe

France and Spain are studying ways to pump money into the regions hit by 
massive wildfires 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/france-and-spain-plan-economic-support-as-major-fires-still-burn?cmpid=072826_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7059>
 over the past week even before the conflagrations are fully contained.

 * Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez said the country’s cabinet will 
declare the affected areas as “emergency zones” on Tuesday, opening the door to 
financial aid. In France, Finance Minister Roland Lescure said on Monday that 
insurers would cover relocation costs for the more than 200,000 people ordered 
to evacuate, even if there is no damage to their homes.
 * While the fires have yet to be contained, there were no major advances by 
the flames on Monday, either near Bordeaux or in the areas south and west of 
Madrid, according to government authorities in both countries.
 * Climate change is intensifying periods of extreme heat in Europe, the 
fastest-warming continent. Record-breaking heat and drought have fueled the 
worst start ever to the global wildfire season, with climate scientists warning 
that a developing El Niño could bring even harsher conditions later this summer.
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-27/wildfires-force-evacuations-in-france-and-spain-before-next-heat-wave?cmpid=072826_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7059>
