**Sovereign Debt Stress**
The global bond selloff is landing hardest on eurozone periphery and France. Wednesday's yield moves — France +15.4 basis points, Italy and Greece each near +15 basis points — are the most severe tests of government finances since the European sovereign debt crisis. Whether this reflects genuine fiscal concern or US-driven spillover remains to be seen, but the magnitude warrants close monitoring given the limited fiscal space in Paris and Rome.

**Energy Transition**
Europe's wind turbine manufacturers appear to be benefiting from the political backlash to energy insecurity. Chinese turbine makers have found negligible commercial traction, with Ming Yang acknowledging no offshore orders expected in 2026 or commitments for 2027. The sharp acceleration in EV adoption — itself a consequence of elevated fossil fuel prices — suggests the Middle East conflict may paradoxically be pulling forward Europe's energy transition.
