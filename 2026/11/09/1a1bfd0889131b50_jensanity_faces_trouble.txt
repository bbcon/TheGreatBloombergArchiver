Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s future as the face of the AI infrastructure boom and 
head of one of the world’s most valuable companies may be hanging in the 
balance.

Huang is being investigated by his own company over whether he’d been “the 
target of a foreign intelligence effort,” after he acknowledged aromantic 
relationship 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/nvidia-board-to-probe-spying-case-after-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-infidelity?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11167>
 with a business executive whom US intelligence determined was a Chinese spy. 
Key House Republicans had also requested a briefing with Huang to discuss his 
dealings with the woman, who worked for a Singapore-based customer. He had
minimized 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/nvidia-ceo-s-affair-admission-counters-past-statements-on-ties?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11167>
 those ties for over a year.

Beijing has dismissed allegations that it spied on the Nvidia CEO. The scandal 
could complicate the APEC summit meeting between President Donald Trump and 
Chinese leader Xi Jinping set for next week, thoughTrump has defended 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/trump-defends-nvidia-s-huang-after-he-admits-affair-with-spy?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11167>

Wall Street is bracing for the worst for the stock 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/nvidia-shares-dip-on-robinhood-as-huang-affair-spurs-board-probe?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11167>
, which was down about 6% on Tradegate versus Friday’s US close. There is key 
man risk for the man dubbed “Jensanity” for his legion of fans. He has no heir 
apparent and has created an informal organizational structure whereby his 
direct reports are tasked with rapidly implementing his decisions. Huang’s
future will depend 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-11-09/jensen-huang-is-bigger-than-nvidia-and-it-s-a-problem?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261109&utm_content=11167>
 on what emerges from the board’s fact-finding process, according to Bloomberg 
Opinion columnist Shuli Ren.
