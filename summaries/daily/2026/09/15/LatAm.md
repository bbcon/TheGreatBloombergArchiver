**Colombia Fiscal Concerns**
The congressional approval of a 16% increase in Colombian government spending represents the most consequential regional development of the period. The expansion raises immediate questions about debt sustainability in an environment of rising global yields and tightening financial conditions. Colombia enters this fiscal expansion with investors already on alert regarding its debt trajectory, and the move arrives precisely as emerging market borrowing costs are moving higher in sympathy with the global bond selloff. The interaction between domestic fiscal loosening and external rate pressures could put further pressure on Colombian sovereign spreads and the peso in coming weeks. No other significant macro data releases for the broader Latin American region were reported during this period.
