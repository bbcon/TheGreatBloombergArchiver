**Brazil Election and Environment**
The record deforestation decline marks a sharp reversal from the Bolsonaro era, when deforestation reached a 15-year high, and directly validates a central pillar of Lula's platform. The timing — one week before the runoff — is politically significant, though whether environmental performance translates into decisive electoral advantage in a closely contested race remains to be seen. Former anti-corruption prosecutor Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa was shot dead in Guatemala City, a reminder of the political risks facing institutional figures across the broader region. No major macro data releases from Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, or Argentina were reported for this period.
