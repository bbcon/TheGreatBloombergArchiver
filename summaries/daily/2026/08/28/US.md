• Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivers his first Jackson Hole keynote at 10 a.m. ET; investors want a hawkish signal after his July 29 press conference, where he declined to hike and offered no reaction-function guidance, was blamed for a Treasury selloff that spread into equities.
• Nvidia shares surged almost 9% Thursday, adding $442 billion in value, the second-biggest single-day market-cap gain on record, even as fixed-income investors say AI has so far been "a bust" for credit returns.
• Advent and Stripe abandoned their roughly $50 billion pursuit of PayPal, sending PayPal shares down 15%.
• The 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.68% ahead of the speech; markets price only a 34% chance the Fed hikes at its September 15-16 meeting.

**Fed Communication**
Warsh's address is his first as chairman speaking solely in his own voice rather than for the full FOMC, and officials remain divided on inflation. Bloomberg Economics' David Wilcox warned under-correcting again could damage his chairmanship. No forward guidance on September is expected, but investors want "backward" guidance explaining why he resisted hiking even as inflation ran hot.

**Markets and Corporates**
Earnings were mixed: Gap gained 15% on a profit beat and an Old Navy CEO change, while Marvell fell 7.9% and Autodesk fell 3.9% on weak results; Ulta Beauty dropped 3.4% on margin pressure. Citadel Securities posted record quarterly trading revenue of $7.3 billion. Gold-mining stocks extended a historic rally, with the MSCI gold miners index up 43% this month — its biggest monthly gain on record — as the Treasury's expanded bond buyback plan revived the "debasement trade."
