**Labor Market Watch**
With the August nonfarm payrolls report due Friday, the primary focus among US economists is the recent deterioration in labor force participation rather than headline job creation. A further decline would complicate the Federal Reserve's already difficult policy calculus: tighter labor supply could sustain wage pressure even as broader economic momentum shows signs of moderating. No major domestic macro prints were released Thursday, leaving markets in a holding pattern ahead of the employment data.

**Energy and Corporate**
US retail diesel prices rose to their highest since mid-2022, reflecting the ongoing pass-through from elevated crude and refined product markets amid Middle East disruptions. The data point carries meaningful inflation implications, as diesel feeds directly into freight and logistics costs across the economy. In corporate news, Broadcom guided for a surge in AI chip sales over the next two years but failed to clear elevated investor expectations, while Snowflake's raised revenue forecast drove a 25% single-session gain. Meta rose 1.1% after releasing its most powerful AI model to date. S&P 500 futures were effectively flat on the day.
