<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-13/bofa-poll-shows-investors-most-exposed-to-tech-stocks-since-2020?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261013&utm_content=10026>
. The proportion of fund managers who are overweight the tech sector has risen 
to 37% from 30% last month, the highest since August 2020, according to a poll 
by Bank of America. About 45% of respondents agreed that there will be an AI 
bubble in 2027, with 42% saying no.

With cracks growing in the AI funding boom, the question now is whether this 
is just a shaking off of excess or a bigger turning point.

The BOJ officials see little need

looked to keep on top of upside inflation risks.

Growth will accelerate

buoyed by an exuberant tech industry — have taken a “broadly benign view of the 
challenges,” the IMF said.

 limiting imports of Chinese hybrid vehicles.

China will require the EU to implement the deal, the China Central 
Television’s Yuyuantantian account said in a post. “China reserves all rights 
to resort to dispute settlement and demand trade compensation within the WTO 
framework,” it said. “If the EU introduces new tricks while negotiating, 
China’s reserved measures will not be merely for show.”

The EU will cap Chinese hybrid imports at 350,000 a year from December, with 
the quota increasing 6% annually, a senior EU official said Monday.
