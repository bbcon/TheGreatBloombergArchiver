**Argentina's Growth-Employment Divergence**
Milei has eliminated Argentina's budget deficit while lowering inflation and poverty, but formal employment — jobs carrying salary, benefits and health coverage — continues to contract even as the broader economy expands. That divergence between headline growth and labor-market health now represents the clearest political vulnerability heading into next year's election.

**Mexico and Trade Policy**
Mexico is considering tougher restrictions on selected Chinese products and higher import taxes on others as it navigates ongoing negotiations with the US over broader trade terms, according to people familiar with the discussions, though no final decisions have been announced.
