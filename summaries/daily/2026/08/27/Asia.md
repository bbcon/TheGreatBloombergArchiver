• India's central bank shifted to intervening routinely to defend the rupee against oil-driven pressure, drawing on $73 billion of fresh dollar inflows since June.

**Central Bank Divergence**
The region's tightening bias contrasts with China's soft patch: BOK and Philippine hikes, alongside signals of an imminent BOJ move, point to inflation still dominating in Korea, the Philippines, Japan and Australia. New Zealand's November 7 election adds uncertainty, with Labour vowing to restore the RBNZ's dual mandate if it wins.

**China and Currency Defense**
China's decelerating profits underscore persistent demand softness even as authorities avoid aggressive stimulus. The RBI's more active rupee defense appears to be buying calm, with volatility near a 10-month low despite a widening current-account deficit. Japan's Kioxia plans a new NAND fab with Sandisk for AI memory demand, joining Samsung and SK Hynix in a capex race, while US authorities probe a Singapore logistics firm over suspected Nvidia chip smuggling to China.
