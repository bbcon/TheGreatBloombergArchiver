**Bank of Japan and Yen**
The yen's sharp appreciation — extending a 0.9% advance from Wednesday — reflects a significant repricing of BOJ policy expectations following comments from a board member raising the possibility of outsized or back-to-back rate hikes. Japan spent a record $96.4 billion over the past month defending the currency after it fell to a four-decade low, according to Finance Ministry data. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent described recent yen moves as "pretty well contained" while expressing confidence that BOJ Governor Ueda would "do the right thing." The dual pressure of intervention risk and a hawkish policy pivot appears to have materially shifted near-term positioning, though the durability of the move without fundamental rate support remains uncertain.

**India Capital Inflows**
The RBI's special deposit program for overseas citizens attracted approximately $127 billion, with two additional programs contributing $9.15 billion, bringing total inflows to $136 billion — a result that materially exceeded official expectations. The surge provided the RBI a substantially larger foreign exchange buffer than anticipated, enabling the rupee's recovery from successive record lows. Managing the resulting liquidity influx now presents the central bank's next challenge.
