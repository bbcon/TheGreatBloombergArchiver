**Energy Shock and Macro Pressure**
Oil above $100 poses a differentiated but broadly negative shock across Asia. Japan, South Korea, and India face steeper import bills and renewed inflationary pressure at a moment when domestic price dynamics are already sticky and policy space is constrained. One economist estimates every $10 increase in Brent adds meaningfully to Indian CPI while shaving approximately 15 basis points from growth. Governments in Indonesia and Thailand may face renewed pressure to subsidize fuel costs, straining already-stretched fiscal positions.

**China: AI Infrastructure and Trade**
China's "dongshu xisuan" strategy — routing coastal AI workloads to data centers in energy-abundant western regions such as Inner Mongolia's Ulanqab — is accelerating. BloombergNEF projects these hinterland hubs will surpass coastal megacity computing capacity around 2028, effectively doubling national data center capacity within five years. The contrast with the US, where data center development faces political backlash and state-level freezes, is drawing explicit attention in Washington. Meanwhile, China's expanding trade surplus and the approaching Trump-Xi summit — provisionally anchored around a September 24 state dinner — are converging into a high-stakes negotiation over tariff structures. Barclays estimates China faces an effective US tariff rate of 23%, against a 7% average for other major partners.
