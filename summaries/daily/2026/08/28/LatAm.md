• Venezuela is weighing an exit from OPEC, according to people familiar with the matter, a move that would deal a fresh blow to the cartel it helped found more than six decades ago.
• The US is separately in talks with Venezuelan leaders to take a large stake in the nation's oil fields — a development Bloomberg describes as unthinkable as recently as two years ago.

**Political Realignment**
A potential OPEC exit would underline the scale of Venezuela's political and economic reorientation since Trump ousted longtime leader Nicolás Maduro and Washington took control of the country's crude sales. Deepening US-Venezuela ties, including the prospective equity stake in oil fields, suggest Caracas is being drawn more tightly into the US economic orbit, with implications for regional oil supply and for OPEC's cohesion as a price-setting bloc. No other Latin American economy — Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia or Chile — featured in today's coverage.
