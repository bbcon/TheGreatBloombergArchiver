**UK Heat Adaptation Policy**
The UK government is set to streamline planning requirements it acknowledged were "unnecessarily restrictive," easing the path for heat-protection upgrades on historically listed buildings. The change follows extended lobbying from Grosvenor and coincides with a string of heat waves that has exposed gaps in UK housing resilience; the Met Office has warned that 40C (104F) summer days are becoming more likely. Grosvenor's chief sustainability officer also flagged that energy bills are set to rise again this autumn, tying the heat-adaptation push to broader affordability pressures facing UK households.

**Water Infrastructure Strain**
Separately, a sudden "flash drought" has pushed England's water network toward breaking point. The episode adds to evidence that UK infrastructure remains exposed to increasingly volatile weather patterns even as policy attention and investment focus on longer-term climate adaptation measures such as building-level heat retrofits.
