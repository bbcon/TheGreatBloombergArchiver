**Energy and the Iran Conflict**
Oil markets remain hostage to the unresolved war. Fund managers on average pencil in $76 Brent by year-end, even as futures trade nearer $85 — a gap suggesting professional investors may be more complacent about tail risk than participants trading energy markets directly, with confusion still surrounding US strategy toward Iran.

**AI Capex and Global Bond Markets**
Hyperscalers are proving "almost price insensitive" to funding costs as they race to finance AI infrastructure, per Robeco, with Alphabet paying a record near-7% yield on a longer-dated Australian dollar bond. That issuance is compounding pressure on long-end government debt already strained by deficit concerns, with Japan's bond market correcting sharply enough that authorities are intervening to support the yen even as its yield gap with China narrows.
