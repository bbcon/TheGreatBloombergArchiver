Poland has spent years building its brand as one of Europe’s economic success 
stories and a geopolitical player.

But with a fragile security situation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-14/poland-mulls-compulsory-military-service-to-bolster-defenses?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 adding to an increasingly bitter political divide, the mood in Warsaw is
anything but triumphant 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-14/poland-s-political-dysfunction-threatens-1-trillion-economy?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 heading into next year’s elections.

A key transit country for Western supplies to Ukraine, Poland has been 
grappling withRussian sabotage 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/tusk-says-germany-detains-two-for-arson-at-polish-starlink-base?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 and cyberattacks.

Then last weekend, Polish security services arrested a man suspected of 
plotting an attack 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-11/poland-probes-possible-attack-planned-on-premier-defense-chief?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 against Prime Minister Donald Tusk and his defense minister. That followed a 
series of stabbings at schools that left one person dead and several injured.

The incidents aren’t necessarily connected. But they’ve created a febrile 
atmosphere as Poland faces one of its most consequential elections since the 
fall of communism.

For Tusk, the stakes are particularly high 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/poland-s-rating-downgrade-deals-a-pre-election-blow-to-tusk?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
.
Tusk in Paris on July 13.Photographer: Nathan Laine/Bloomberg
A former top European Union official, Tusk returned to power in 2023 promising 
to restore democratic institutions eroded under eight years of nationalist 
rule. He pledged to repair EU relations and firmly anchor Poland in the 
European mainstream.

Next year’s vote will determine the fate of his ambitious project, whether 
it’s a lasting political realignment or a brief interruption in Poland’s 
nationalist turn.

The opposition’s hardly in fighting shape, divided by leadership struggles and 
competing factions on the right.

Yet it remains a potent threat, particularly if it can translate growing 
anxiety over security 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/russia-s-attacks-on-europe-are-getting-more-brazen-and-more-dangerous?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
, migration and political violence into a sense the government has lost control.

Poland’s international ambitions are growing along with its $1 trillion 
economy. One of NATO’s biggest defense spenders in GDP terms, it’s demanding a 
greater say in global affairs.

President Karol Nawrocki, a nationalist rival of Tusk, has an invitation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/if-poland-gets-a-us-base-it-ll-be-called-fort-trump-nawrocki-says?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 from Donald Trump to attend December’sGroup of 20 summit 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/poland-seeks-voice-in-global-regulations-with-g-20-presence?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
 in Miami.

The question is whether Poland turns its newfound weight into lasting 
political influence or looks inward to fight domestic battles. — Zosia Wanat 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXKPYetNRcg/zosia-wanat?cmpid=BBD101526_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261015&utm_content=10133>
