**Monetary Policy**
The October FOMC minutes confirmed the committee's hawkish disposition. Almost all participants judged further rate hikes likely appropriate, with the majority seeing upside risks to inflation and several specifically citing the AI buildout as a material inflationary force. Governor Chris Waller moved further, publicly floating a 50bp move in December on CNBC; market pricing assigns only an 8.6% probability to a jumbo hike, but rate-sensitive assets — particularly precious metals, now down 37% from January peaks — are reflecting the tightening path.

**Equity and Credit Markets**
Anthropic's record offering is providing near-term relief to equity bulls. Nasdaq 100 futures rose 2.1%, with the Magnificent Seven broadly advancing on hopes the blockbuster IPO signals continued investor appetite for AI. Palo Alto Networks gained 9% premarket after raising guidance and acquiring Applied Compute for $4.5bn. Nevertheless, the Nasdaq 100 has shed more than 10% since Jensen Huang's departure and the Democratic sweep of the midterms, and the dollar's sharp, rate-independent rally warrants monitoring as a possible early signal of financial stress.
