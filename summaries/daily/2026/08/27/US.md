• Nvidia guided to roughly 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028, well above the ~45% consensus, forecasting $108 billion of current-quarter sales; shares jumped as much as 12%, adding an estimated $370 billion in market value.
• S&P 500 futures rose 0.5% and Nasdaq 100 futures gained 1.1%, with Salesforce (+11%) and CrowdStrike (+8.8%) extending a broad AI-software rally; the 10-year Treasury yield edged up to 4.67%.
• Gold and Bitcoin ETFs drew a record $7 billion of inflows over five trading days as the "debasement trade" gathered pace ahead of Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Friday Jackson Hole address.
• Fed Governor Lisa Cook again denied mortgage-fraud allegations as President Trump weighs renewing his push to remove her.

**AI Capital Spending**
Nvidia's guidance reinforced the view that AI infrastructure demand remains undersupplied: the company said it could grow faster still with more capacity, and its data-center division beat estimates on continued spending from Alphabet and Amazon. Margins are expected to narrow modestly as memory costs rise, a pressure also visible in HP's 8.9% decline on PC-demand concerns.

**Fiscal Anxiety and Gold**
The record gold-and-Bitcoin inflows reflect renewed investor unease about US government borrowing, the dollar, and efforts to contain long-term yields, rather than a rotation away from equities. Warsh's Jackson Hole remarks are seen as a potential catalyst: any signal that fiscal pressure is bleeding into monetary policy could reinforce the debasement narrative, a concern sharpened by the dispute over Cook's tenure at the Fed.
