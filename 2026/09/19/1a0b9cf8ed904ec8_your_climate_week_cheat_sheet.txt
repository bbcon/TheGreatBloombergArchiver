Turkey’s reality check

By Selcan Hacaoglu 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQ6hs25pQys/selcan-hacaoglu?cmpid=BBD091926_GREENDAILY&utm_campaign=greendaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260919&utm_content=8994>
 andLaura Millan 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASzdyi2b6bE/laura-millan?cmpid=BBD091926_GREENDAILY&utm_campaign=greendaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260919&utm_content=8994>

Turkey’s progress on the energy transition is coming under scrutiny as the 
host of the next UN climate summit tries to rally international support for a 
new global electrification goal that would require significant policy changes 
for the country itself to fulfill it.
Electricity pylons above a residential neighborhood in Istanbul.Photographer: 
Nicole Tung/Bloomberg
Ahead of this year’s COP31 climate gathering in Antalya, Turkey is seeking 
support from policymakers attending Climate Week in New York for a global 
target of 35% electrification by 2035. The country’s official projections 
forecast it will only have achieved 25% electrification by that date.

“Very significant policy changes to accelerate renewable electrification would 
be needed to bring it to 35% by 2035,” said Climate Action Tracker, a 
non-profit that analyzes countries’ climate goals and their progress in 
achieving them. “Power generation from wind and solar is increasing, but not 
enough to displace fossil fuels which are also expanding.”

A Climate Week NYC guide for the overwhelmed

By Bloomberg News
Photographer: Michael Nagle/Bloomberg
Climate Week NYC, an annual fixture held alongside the United Nations General 
Assembly, is back for 2026. The sprawlingcalendar 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search> includes hundreds of events 
around the city through Sept. 27 — everything from a morningbirdwalk 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/climate-week-birdwalk-at-marine-park>
 to asustainable fashion showcase 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/fashioning-impact-deploy-showcase-event-celebrating-20-years-x-20-icons>
 to acompost tour 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/climate-week-nyc-compost-tour>.

Below, our journalists offer a selection of what struck us as intriguing, 
timely or can’t-miss. One event we’ll give special mention to:Bloomberg Green’s 
own 
<https://events.bloomberglive.com/event/bloomberg-green-new-york-2026/agenda>. 
Join us on Tuesday and Wednesday for lively conversations with speakers from 
the World Bank, Amazon, Microsoft, Ben & Jerry’s and more.

Monday, Sept. 21

Buildings for People, Cities for Life: A New Agenda for Urban Resilience 
<https://mfa-events.dk/partnershipsforthefuture26/urban-resilience-summit>

Instead of treating urban policy and climate policy as separate domains, how 
can they be fused into a single cohesive agenda? Scheduled speakers at this 
event, organized by the Danish consulate and partners, include former Paris 
Mayor Anne Hidalgo.

BloombergNEF at Climate Week 
<https://events.bloombergevents.com/event/BNEF-briefing/agenda>

For the best-informed insights into what lies ahead, drop by this event 
featuring a passel of clean economy experts. They’ll talk about global impacts 
from the Iran war, the outlook for renewables and data centers in the US, the 
future of advanced transportation and more.

Climate Town & Amy Westervelt Live 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/past-events/climate-town-amy-westervelt-live>

Rollie Williams, host of the popular YouTube channel Climate Town, and 
journalist Amy Westervelt of Drilled Media are teaming up for a ticketed comedy 
night. Climate disinformation is likely to be one of their targets.

Tuesday, Sept. 22

Perspectives Shaping Climate Action Through Sports 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/perspectives-shaping-climate-action-through-sports>

Lace up your sneakers and get ready to “plog.” New York Road Runners brings 
together leaders from the realms of sustainability, science and sports to share 
their thoughts on meeting the climate challenge. After the panel, participants 
are invited to head outdoors to collect litter while running or walking. Bags 
and gloves will be provided.

Climate, Security and America’s Place in a Shifting World 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/climate-security-and-americas-place-in-a-shifting-world>

The convergence of energy security and national security demands a rethink of 
US climate policy. This panel, hosted by the Climate Group, will explore what 
that means, from scaling technologies to developing leaders and rebuilding 
coalitions. Scheduled speakers include Senator Sheldon Whitehouse of Rhode 
Island and Phil Duffy, chief scientist of Spark Climate Solutions.

Sustainable Investment Forum 2026 
<https://events.climateaction.org/sustainable-investment-forum-north-america/>

This annual, all-day gathering of asset owners and institutional investors 
will focus on everything from how to shield portfolios from physical risk to 
governance approaches for artificial intelligence.

Dynamo Energy Transition Summit
 
<https://www.dynamoenergyhub.com/events/dynamo-energy-transition-summit-climate-week-nyc-2026-registration/>

An afternoon gathering focused on the future of energy will bring together the 
tech, banking and energy industries, with representatives of Google, Meta and 
Bank of America due to speak.

2nd Annual Climate Jam
 
<https://www.brooklynbowl.com/brooklyn/events/detail/climate-jam-k7vgf_8kxoymg?utm_source=broadscalegroup.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=you-re-invited-climate-jam-2026&_bhlid=1de34b2a64e4c3b511fbb96c3b90ec426e9808f2>

This benefit concert will raise money for the nonprofit Protect Our Winters, 
which aims to inspire snowboarders, rock climbers and cross-country runners to 
take up the climate fight. The evening will feature live music from Karl 
Denson, saxophonist for the Rolling Stones, and Adam Met of AJR.

Wednesday, Sept. 23

Brazil at a Crossroads: From COP30 to the 2026 Elections 
<https://eot.cri.org/f/a/gjsUXyVPzeNYI-HWPI3sJA~~/AAAHURA~/XeqIvuUHZJ2bnPE1WTGEgR8SKP66GF9CRACiXXP12iA1OO4SuFIq8RF74JTKwFpxT46h9JXTmQ6MBMYoGp7W7VdUZitkfymg8URyZeQB_iIu2iLlGt6stLnlyVz-Ttl6SeElncpTc436QyFZbJ0_p-oTf3uFm6NmoAtnNcTMfKIFtRojNnXnHxxm0J_mZ5THMwK2crXZDvKpOI_pxPHFGZNh9_ebkNUZVykBnmKiwe3d-kjGX462B5YlOEtM0ibIWQe-MqG8A3tJS2nPZzga3Q~~>

Brazil’s elections next month will shape the country’s climate and 
environmental policies for years to come — with far-reaching consequences for 
the Amazon and other threatened ecosystems. This event, hosted by Climate 
Rights International, weighs what’s at stake.

The Future of Data Centers: Technologies to Change the Paradigm 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/the-future-of-data-centers-technologies-to-change-the-paradigm>

Data centers are at an inflection point, says the MIT Climate Project, and 
that demands innovation across disciplines and sectors. MIT researchers will 
present technologies that could reshape the data center in a session that will 
also convene leaders from technology, infrastructure, capital and policy.

Superpollutant Action Summit 
<https://beyond-alliance.org/our-work/superpollutants/superpollutant-action-summit/#>

Hosted by Beyond and the We Mean Business Coalition, this daylong event aims 
to catalyze concrete action on near-term warming agents: methane, black carbon, 
HFCs and nitrous oxide.

Rethinking Enterprise AI: Where Compute Efficiency Meets Climate Strategy 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/rethinking-enterprise-ai-where-compute-efficiency-meets-climate-strategy>

Representatives of GitHub, Yale and LinkedIn will discuss findings from a new 
survey of software developers’ climate opinions. Then they’ll discuss how to 
mobilize IT and sustainability leaders around prioritizing efficiency.

Habitat for Humanity Solar Barnraising 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/past-events/habitat-for-humanitys-solar-barn-raising-at-historic-mascot-flats>

At a co-op on the Lower East Side — the first Habitat for Humanity project 
that the late President Jimmy Carter worked on — the group’s Solar 
Collaborative will host a “barnraising” to prepare for the installation of 
rooftop panels. Be ready to haul some up the stairs.

Building Postcarbon Futures 
<https://www.bklynlibrary.org/calendar/cbh-talk-building-center-for-brooklyn-20260923-0630pm>

How do we forge a sustainable future that is both just and resilient? Author 
Billy Fleming, drawing on his new book that surveys climate justice projects 
around the world, joins philosopher Olúfẹ́mi O. Táíwò, sociologist Daniel 
Aldana Cohen and designer Keller Easterling for a wide-ranging discussion.

Thursday, Sept. 24

The Resilience Test: An Interactive Catastrophic Disaster Simulation 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/the-resilience-test-an-interactive-catastrophic-disaster-simulation>

DLA Piper hosts a workshop “that will challenge participants to navigate a 
realistic, evolving climate scenario,” an exercise intended to yield practical 
insights into disaster response and risk management.

New Climate Futures
 <https://www.newlab.com/new-climate-futures-2026>

Newlab’s annual gathering returns to the Brooklyn Navy Yard, where more than 
1,000 climate tech founders, investors and policymakers will meet and greet. 
This year’s themes include decarbonizing hard-to-abate industries and 
strengthening the power grid. The event will also highlight startups working on 
climate adaptation.

Climate Capital Summit
 <https://www.climatecapitalsummit.com/>

Some of the biggest firms on Wall Street, from BlackRock to Goldman Sachs and 
Apollo, will gather with energy and technology executives to discuss scaling 
finance for climate solutions.

Firebreaks: From Satellites to the Streets 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/firebreaks-from-satellites-to-the-streets>

As part of the main Swiss stage at Climate Week, this event hosted by Swissnex 
and partners considers how advances in remote sensing and Earth observation can 
help communities adapt to a world in which fire season is longer and more 
destructive. Following an expert panel, several guests will be invited to pitch 
their own climate-ready city projects at an open mic.

Friday, Sept. 25

The Future of the Grid: From Utility Infrastructure to End Users 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/the-future-of-the-grid-from-utility-infrastructure-to-end-users>

Organized by HearstLab, the program will feature live demos from its portfolio 
companies Buzz Solutions, Symbium and Scope Zero, as well as leaders from 
government, tech and the utility sector discussing how to build a resilient 
energy system.

Free Central Park Bike Tour 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/free-central-park-bike-tour-for-climate-week-nyc-925-200pm>

Unwind after a week of frenetic networking by exploring Central Park on two 
wheels, courtesy of Holland Bikes.

Nuclear Rooftop Party 
<https://www.climateweeknyc.org/event-search/nuclear-rooftop-party>

Join Nuclear New York and the Nuclear is Clean Energy Club for atomic DJ sets 
under the stars. 21+.
