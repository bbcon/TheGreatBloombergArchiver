guidance amid rising interest rates. Earnings were helped by elevated rates, 
inflows into its wealth business and lower-than-expected credit losses. Kaur 
also said the bank is conducting a “robust and rigorous process” for her 
replacement and that artificial intelligence will impact some jobs and 
transform others.
