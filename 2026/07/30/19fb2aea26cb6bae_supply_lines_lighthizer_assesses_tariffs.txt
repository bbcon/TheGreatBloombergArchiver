Tariff pressure and taxes on Chinese capital are among the ways rivals like 
the US can address Beijing’s efforts to keep its currency undervalued, 
according to the architect of President Donald Trump’strade agenda 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/trump-s-tariffs-are-likely-to-stick-around-despite-unpopularity?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 during his first term.

Addressing a weak yuan 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/eur-cny-why-china-s-yuan-is-drawing-scrutiny-in-europe-what-it-means-for-euro?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 and other US challenges stemming from China’s economic rise are among the 
issues Robert Lighthizer covers in an interview withThe Foreign Affairs 
Interview podcast <https://www.foreignaffairs.com/podcasts/end-free-trade> 
posted Thursday.

Here are some of other topics Lighthizer discusses on the episode, titled “The 
End of Free Trade”:

 * A few times he talks about green shoots in US manufacturing 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/trump-touts-tariffs-in-midterm-pitch-to-gm-workers-in-michigan?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 over the past 18 months, while acknowledging that the country isn’t going to 
re-industrialize in a couple of years.
 * Mexico’s trade surplus with the US needs to be addressed in USMCA talks 
with moreAmerican content 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/us-presses-mexico-to-mirror-its-steel-tariff-wall-aimed-at-china?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 in their production and more demand for US imports, he says.
 * For investment to take hold, the uncertainty he worries about isn’t about 
the introduction ofnew tariffs 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/us-sets-new-forced-labor-duties-as-trump-resurrects-tariff-wall?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 but of the unknowns of whether they’ll stay in place.
 * The US has to protect its technology but needs to be careful 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/us-risks-broadening-china-tech-clash-with-robot-inverter-curbs?cmpid=BBD073026_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260730&utm_content=7001>
 because China has demonstrated a willingness to weaponize its control of 
critical minerals, Lighthizer says.
