**Political Economy**
Brazil's fiscal credibility remains the dominant regional macro theme. The sounding-out of Bradesco's Trabuco Cappi for a government role is best read as a market-management signal from Lula — an acknowledgment that investor concern over fiscal deterioration is reaching a level that requires a credible institutional response. Whether an appointment materializes, and in what capacity, will determine how markets interpret the gesture. Separately, Trump's new tariff structure covers multiple Latin American economies, with Brazil's public pushback the most pointed. The tariffs add an external headwind to a region where domestic demand dynamics are already uneven, and where commodity export revenues remain sensitive to Chinese growth conditions.
