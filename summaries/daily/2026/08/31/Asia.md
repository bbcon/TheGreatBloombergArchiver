**Currencies and Rates**
The yen's slide has proven expensive to fight: it has surrendered more than half its July gains despite record intervention, as a stronger dollar and higher US rate expectations keep pushing the other way. Strategists see 161 and then 162-163 as levels where the risk of action rises. Bessent said he expects the BOJ to "do the right thing," fueling bets Tokyo moves faster on rates.

**Markets and Politics**
Korea's benchmark remains up 61% year-to-date but 25% below the record reached two months ago, with retail traders citing regulatory friction and a fading AI-trade narrative. South Korea named a career technocrat as finance minister in a surprise reshuffle, while Modi's meeting with Putin at the SCO summit underscores the strain Trump's tariffs place on India's strategic-autonomy posture.
