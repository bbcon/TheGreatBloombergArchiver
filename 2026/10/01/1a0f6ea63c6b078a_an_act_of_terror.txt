Two hours into a flight bound for Tel Aviv, an altercation was heard inside 
the cockpit. The co-pilot had stabbed his fellow pilot and the aircraft 
nose-dived 14,000 feet in 30 seconds. Storming in totake down the assailant

and the UAE are close allies, and those ties have deepened during the US-led 
war on Iran.

And Finally

Glaciers all over Earth are shrinking as the climate changes, but the rate of 
loss isunexpectedly high in the Alps 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-30/swiss-glaciers-have-shrunk-20-over-five-years-after-summer-melt?cmpid=BBD100126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261001&utm_content=9577>
. Lack of snowfall in winter and abnormal heat over the summer saw Swiss 
glaciers lose over 5% of their volume and at least two disappeared altogether, 
bringing the loss on Switzerland’s peaks to nearly 20% over the past five 
years. Scientists have long forecast glaciers shrinking, a direct consequence 
of the warming of the planet caused by human-emitted greenhouse gases, yet the 
pace of the melting leaves Alpine ice caps no chance of surviving over the long 
term.
