• US public debt surpassed $40 trillion for the first time this week, reinforcing concerns that the buyback plan amounts to debt monetization rather than fiscal repair.

**Fiscal Policy and the Dollar**
Bessent's buyback announcement and promised fiscal initiative appear aimed at capping long-term yields (the 10-year traded at 4.68%), but strategists including former Goldman Sachs FX chief Robin Brooks argue the move violates the Treasury's "regular and predictable" issuance principle and could ultimately demand a higher risk premium from investors rather than lower one. The dollar weakened further on the news, and commentary increasingly draws parallels to Japan's currency debasement, where the yen hit a four-decade low last month amid a debt-to-GDP ratio above 200%.

**Markets and Positioning**
Equity investors remain broadly risk-on: fund managers' equity allocation is the highest in nearly five years, according to a Bank of America survey, even as respondents flag a disorderly rise in bond yields as their top concern. Nasdaq 100 futures were little changed, up roughly 0.2%-0.4% on the day, while Anthropic's mega-IPO and continued AI-linked capital-markets activity underscored still-strong risk appetite despite the fiscal and currency crosscurrents.
