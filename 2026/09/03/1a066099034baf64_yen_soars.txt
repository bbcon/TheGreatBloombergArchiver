 * Power demand was rising as London crossed 34C on June 24. Energy prices 
spiked as the sun set and solar panels across the UK stopped generating 
electricity. That’s when Austin knew he could take advantage of his 200 
kilowatt-hour home battery, which he had charged up using cheap power from the 
grid during the daytime.
 * He sold the power back to the grid for £60 from 4 p.m. to 9 p.m. In just a 
few hours, Austin had earned about as much as an apartment dweller would 
typically pay for electricity every month. All this happened without Austin 
having to press a single button.
 * Many parts of the world, especially Europe, are struggling with high
