 * Losing ground: United hasn’t made a profit since 2018-19, while revenue 
growth has lagged its biggest rivals. Liverpool is now valued ahead of Man 
United for the first time, and some members of the Glazer family believe they 
missed an opportunity to cash out.
 * Ownership rift: The Glazers and Jim Ratcliffe are at odds over the club’s 
future, people familiar said, even though Ratcliffe was handed control of 
football decisions in 2024. Some Glazer siblings are pushing to sell more of 
United, which they value at about $6 billion.
 * No quick fix: Mauricio Pochettino becomes United’s eighth permanent manager 
in 13 years when he takes charge against Liverpool on Sunday. But the 
Manchester United Supporters Trust says another coaching change can’t repair 
problems stemming from the club’s ownership.
