**Technology and Trade Exposure**
Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix fell sharply in initial trading on fears that a Democratic Congress would pursue more aggressive AI regulation, curtailing demand for advanced memory chips. The concern is not unfounded: Democratic governors elected in Texas, Georgia, Ohio, and Pennsylvania each campaigned on restricting data center expansion, which is currently the primary driver of high-bandwidth memory demand. Chinese AI developers, separately, are set to receive only a fraction of the 70 billion Nvidia RTX chips ordered, as production constraints bite. Chinese authorities responded by directing the "national team" and corporate buybacks to support domestic equities, with the CSI 300 rising 2.3%.

**Indonesia Political Risk**
The wiretapping probe targeting President Prabowo has escalated to the point where senior officials are characterizing the alleged conduct as potential treason. The mobilization of troops to Jakarta introduces a degree of political uncertainty that markets will need to price alongside an already-difficult economic backdrop of rising prices and weak consumer sentiment. Singapore, meanwhile, proposed tougher cross-border haze legislation in response to Indonesia's devastating wildfire season, adding a bilateral dimension to Jakarta's governance challenges.
