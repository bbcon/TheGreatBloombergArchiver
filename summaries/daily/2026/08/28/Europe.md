**Inflation Pressure Building**
The energy-driven inflation surprises in Spain and France point to a euro area where price pressures are re-accelerating even as growth stays fragile — France's near-miss on recession sits uneasily against a backdrop that argues for tighter policy. That leaves the ECB, and a Christine Lagarde succession process lacking a formal early-exit procedure, navigating a narrowing path between growth and prices.

Germany's AfD is positioned for a historic win in the Saxony-Anhalt state election, which would be the far-right's first hold on regional power since World War II, while a senior Meloni-party official floated an alliance with Italy's far-right National Future.
