**ECB Leadership Transition**
The ECB faces its most significant leadership overhaul in more than a decade, with candidate nominations for Christine Lagarde's successor due Friday. Two names are currently in circulation — Dutchman Klaas Knot and Spain's Pablo Hernandez de Cos — though the broader context is notable: Lagarde is departing nearly nine months early, and four of six Executive Board seats will turn over within a year. Lagarde has cited a desire to speak freely on political matters ahead of France's presidential election. The concentration of appointments raises coordination risks at a moment when euro-area inflation remains well above target and policy decisions are consequential.

**UK and European Markets**
The Stoxx Europe 600 fell 0.6% in early trading before recovering to gain 1.4% as investors digested the US election outcome and its implications for regulatory policy. Research suggests UK rates are proving less effective at curbing inflation than the Bank of England anticipated, strengthening the case for a Thursday rate hike. Deutsche Bank's integration of UK broker Numis has lost roughly a third of its client base since the £410 million acquisition in 2023, with IPO activity remaining sparse despite early signs of improving conditions.
