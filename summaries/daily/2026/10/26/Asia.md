**India Equity Markets**
Jio's IPO, the largest share sale in Indian history, priced successfully despite the overhang of a weeklong domestic equity decline. However, the deal illustrated the structural compression in Indian investment banking fees: the 19 underwriters shared approximately $20.7 million, equal to roughly 0.65% of deal size, trailing fee pools on several prior offerings. Fierce competition among banks for marquee mandates has eroded economics even on landmark transactions, with firms accepting thin fees in exchange for league-table positioning.

**Korea and AI Sentiment**
South Korean equities reflected broader AI skepticism. The Kospi fell 1.5% on Monday, and Goldman Sachs's revised price target — still approximately 60% above current levels — underscores how dramatically valuations have compressed since June. Two leveraged ETFs tracking SK Hynix, launched just months ago, will be liquidated as demand for amplified AI exposure moderates. Volatile swings in Korean semiconductor stocks had been a meaningful driver of prime brokerage revenues at banks including Barclays.

**Singapore Energy Security**
Indonesia's unannounced reduction in gas exports, framed around domestic supply needs, prompted Singapore's energy minister to outline contingency measures at the Singapore International Energy Week. The government emphasized the cuts represent less than 2% of daily demand, but the episode highlights broader resource-nationalism risks across Southeast Asia.
