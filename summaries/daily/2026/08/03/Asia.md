**China AI and Manufacturing**
Alibaba released its largest-ever AI model, Qwen3.8-Max, built on 2.4 trillion parameters and ranking above Moonshot's Kimi K3 on several benchmarks — the latest in a series of Chinese breakthroughs challenging US rivals at significantly lower computational cost. Against this, China's factory activity contracted unexpectedly in July, underscoring that domestic demand softness and export headwinds from US tariffs continue to weigh on the manufacturing base.

**Japan and the Yen**
The $52.8 billion single-day intervention on July 31 was the largest on record, and the first coordinated US-Japan yen-support operation since 2011. The Bank of Japan held rates at 1% last week despite Governor Ueda projecting above-2% inflation in the second half of the fiscal year — a contradiction that has undermined forward guidance credibility. Prime Minister Takaichi's growth-oriented fiscal stance constrains the BOJ's room to tighten, while Japan's high public debt caps how far yields can rise, leaving the yen structurally vulnerable. The September BOJ meeting is now the critical decision point.
