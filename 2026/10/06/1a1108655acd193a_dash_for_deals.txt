From China’s hottest artificial intelligence startups to the hardware that 
keeps the technology running in Japan, Asia’s dealmakers are chasing the AI 
boom.

DeepSeek has blown past 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-06/deepseek-to-raise-at-least-12-billion-in-tencent-backed-funding?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261006&utm_content=9749>
 its capital-raising target ahead of a landmark IPO early next year. The 
company is close to securing at least $12 billion in its latest round of 
funding with Tencent and battery maker CATL among the largest contributors to 
the round, according to people familiar with the matter.

Beijing-based rival Moonshot AI has closed the final round 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-06/moonshot-said-to-eye-early-2027-ipo-after-value-hits-50-billion?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261006&utm_content=9749>
 of its latest private fundraising at a valuation of about $50 billion as it 
also targets a Hong Kong listing in the first quarter of next year, according 
to people familiar with the matter. Both Moonshot and Deepseek have drawn 
enormous interest from investors as two of just a clutch of companies at the 
heart of China’s effort to compete globally on AI.

Meanwhile in Japan, two hard drive makers are locked in a battle 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-06/seagate-is-said-to-battle-toshiba-for-tdk-s-hard-drive-head-unit?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261006&utm_content=9749>
 over TDK’s magnetic-heads business. Toshiba initiated talks with TDK in the 
spring, followed by a higher offer in the summer from Seagate Technology, 
people familiar with the matter said. The acquisition is expected to be worth 
as much as several billion dollars. The winning bidder will corner a part of 
the supply chain controlled by just three companies. TDK is the only 
independent maker of HDD magnetic-recording heads — the part of a hard drive 
that actually records and retrieves data — and supplies both its suitors as 
well as Western Digital.
