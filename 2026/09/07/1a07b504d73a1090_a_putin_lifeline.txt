The far right in Europe is giving Vladimir Putin a lifeline just when he needs 
it most.

European security is looking shakier 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/russia-s-hybrid-war-is-europe-s-top-threat-eu-commander-warns?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 as a result.

The Alternative for Germany’s record election victory 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/german-far-right-afd-set-to-win-state-vote-short-of-majority?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt yesterday shows its growing confidence 
in challenging the political establishment in Berlin.

The AfD won days after Chancellor Friedrich Merz blamed Russia 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/germany-to-blame-russia-for-failed-leipzig-airport-drone-attack?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 for a drone attack at Leipzig airport, a sign the region’s voters weren’t 
listening and were in any case undeterred by — and probably cheered — the 
nationalist party’s pro-Moscow stance.

Russia’s dialing up 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/lavrov-warns-that-germany-is-moving-toward-war-with-russia?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 the rhetoric. Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov accused Merz of “essentially 
moving to declare war on us” over the drone incident.

Marine Le Pen’s National Rally party is also threatening to tilt France away 
from unconditional support of Ukraine in its defense against Russia if she 
succeeds Emmanuel Macron in next year’s presidential election.
AfD co-chairwoman Alice Weidel at a party congress in Erfurt, Germany, on July 
4,Photographer: Jens Schlueter/Getty Images,
“Everything needs to be reviewed,” party President Jordan Bardella, who’s 
likely to be her prime minister,told us 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/france-s-far-right-chief-bardella-wants-ukraine-to-pay-for-europe-s-help?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
, praising President Donald Trump for demanding Ukraine give something in 
return for US support.

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner made little apparent progress in 
negotiations to end the war duringweekend meetings 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/ukraine-is-bracing-for-another-winter-of-war-zelenskyy-says?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 in Moscow and Kyiv with Presidents Putin and Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Putin isn’t budging 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/us-envoys-to-make-new-ukraine-peace-push-with-russia-pessimistic?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 from his demands that Ukraine surrender more territory. Russiahas intensified 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-05/kyiv-adapts-to-constant-attack-by-russian-jet-powered-drones?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 missile attacks on Kyiv, which isdesperately short 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/europe-leans-on-spain-and-greece-to-give-ukraine-air-defenses?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 of air-defenses.

European leaders nervous about Trump’s review of American military deployments 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/us-presses-allies-on-ideological-loyalty-ahead-of-troop-cuts?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 in NATO are watching November’s US midterm elections closely. They know a 
Republican defeat could make him a lame-duck president, while worrying he may 
become even more unpredictable abroad.

Le Pen and the AfD have expressed skepticism toward NATO.

The risk is far-right victories in Germany and France splinter support for 
Ukraine and Europe’s determination to rebuild its own defenses against the 
challenge from Russia,amid doubts 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/us-nato-allies-prepare-to-defend-themselves-after-trump-threats?cmpid=BBD090726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260907&utm_content=8611>
 over US protections for the continent.

That would deliver Putin the geopolitical victory of his dreams. — Anthony
