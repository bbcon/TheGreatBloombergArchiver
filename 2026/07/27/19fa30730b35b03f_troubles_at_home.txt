The summer is producing unexpected difficulties for both the presidents of 
Ukraine and Russia.

Volodymyr Zelenskyy overhauled Ukraine’s top military leadership, a move 
forced on him bypublic protests 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/zelenskyy-bows-to-pressure-for-new-model-army-to-confront-russia?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 over his dismissal of Mykhailo Fedorov, the popular defense minister who’s 
been a key architect of its successful drone strategy.

New army chief 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/ukraine-s-new-military-leader-is-forged-by-war-and-technology?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 Mykhailo Drapatyi is an advocate of the same tech-driven reforms as Fedorov, 
who’sopenly lobbying 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/ukraine-s-fedorov-snubs-zelenskyy-job-offers-seeking-comeback?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 to get his job back.

That’s posing an awkard challenge to Zelenskyy’s authority as he visits the UK 
today to meet new Prime Minister Andy Burnham. Britain says it’ll share 
innovative “Stone Cloak 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/burnham-to-host-zelenskyy-in-signal-of-continued-uk-support?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
” technology with Ukraine, capable of blocking Russian airdefense systems from 
tracking and targeting drones.

That spells more trouble 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/kyiv-s-strikes-battering-russian-economy-hang-over-rate-decision?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 for President Vladimir Putin, who appears helpless to stop Ukrainian drone 
attacks across Russia.
A demonstration in support of Fedorov in Kyiv on Friday.Photographer: Andrew

Strikes on warehouses 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/ukraine-hammers-putin-s-economy-with-strikes-on-giant-warehouses?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 of Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries are rippling through the economy as 
thousands of small businesses calculate losses from inventory destroyed in the 
fires. It’s adding to anger among ordinary Russians over fuel shortages caused 
by Ukrainian attacks onoil refineries 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/russia-says-it-will-end-diesel-export-ban-when-market-recovers?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
.

Putin suddenly looks far from all-powerful at home. Kazakhstan’s president 
even told himto his face 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/kazakhstan-urges-putin-to-freeze-ukraine-war-in-first-such-call?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 that he should stop the war during a visit to Russia on Saturday, which would 
have been unthinkable a few years ago.

Zelenskyy will be in Washington tomorrow for talks with US President Donald 
Trump, seekingto capitalize 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/us-to-produce-ukraine-s-magura-sea-drones-for-the-first-time?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 on therecent improvement 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/trump-says-he-will-give-ukraine-license-to-make-patriot-missiles?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
 in their relationship.

The Kremlin, meanwhile, accuses the US of abandoning what it sees as 
understandings reached at last August’s Alaska summit between Putin and Trump 
on a deal to end the war.

With no sign of progress in peace talks, Zelenskyy and Putin are on the back 
foot domestically 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/five-ways-putin-is-in-trouble-over-russia-s-war-in-ukraine?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>

<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/ebola-cases-in-congo-reach-3-200-as-outbreak-nears-second-largest-on-record?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>

mining and cross-border trade with Uganda and South Sudan continue to fuel 
transmission.

<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/french-fire-heads-toward-bordeaux-as-spain-battles-record-blaze?cmpid=BBD072726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260727&utm_content=7013>
