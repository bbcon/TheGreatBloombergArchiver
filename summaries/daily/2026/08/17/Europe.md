**Inflation Set to Reaccelerate**
UK price pressures are expected to pick up for the first time in four months, with a Bloomberg survey pointing to a 2.9% year-on-year July CPI print, reflecting higher airfares and energy costs tied to the Iran war and an AI-driven component squeeze. BOE rate-setters may grow uneasy as inflation reasserts itself against a resilient economy.

**Deregulation Push and Bank M&A**
European lenders hope Washington's rollback — which reversed a threatened 16% capital-requirement increase estimated to cost US banks $160 billion — will cross the Atlantic, after the EU pledged to address complaints. German officials are separately open to discussing a Commerzbank stake sale to UniCredit, though this is not yet formal policy. Greece plans to repay roughly €13 billion of debt early to bring its stock below Italy's.
