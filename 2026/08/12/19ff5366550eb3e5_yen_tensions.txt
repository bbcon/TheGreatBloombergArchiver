the PM has resisted aggressive tightening to protect growth.

The split sharpened after the BOJ held rates steady last month, and the 
currency has since slipped back toward ¥160 per dollar. Markets are looking at 
a possible BOJ rate hike in September or October.

Meanwhile, Bank of New York Mellon has created the Katsu Curry Index
