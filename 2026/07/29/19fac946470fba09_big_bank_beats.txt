<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/spain-s-warren-buffett-followers-score-a-rare-win-for-value-investing?cmpid=072926_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260729&utm_content=7112>
Francisco García Paramés.Source: Cobas Asset Management
Value investors are a rare breed these days. That’s what makes the story of 
Francisco García Paramés and hisever-expanding band of acolytes 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/spain-s-warren-buffett-followers-score-a-rare-win-for-value-investing?cmpid=072926_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260729&utm_content=7112>
 in Spain so surprising.

 * Over a four-decade career, Paramés has reached legendary status in value 
circles by churning out top-tier returns year after year. His flagship €1.2 
billion Cobas Internacional fund achieved an average annual return of 20% in 
the past five years, after a run at asset manager Bestinver that earned him 
comparisons to Warren Buffett.
 * Alongside his rise, other investors adopted his same “buy cheap and be 
patient” ethos, turning Madrid into something of a value investing hub in 
Europe.
 * Fund managers at Azvalor Asset Management, A&G Global Investors and 
Magallanes Value Investors have all worked alongside Paramés or been influenced 
by his philosophy, and at least seven value shops have sprung up in the city in 
the past decade.
