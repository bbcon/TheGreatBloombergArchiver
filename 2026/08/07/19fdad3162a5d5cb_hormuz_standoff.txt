Deep Dive: OpenAI’s Doughnut Device
Sam Altman, chief executive officer and co-founder of OpenAI.Photographer: 
David Paul Morris/Bloomberg
A highly anticipated new device from OpenAI will have a unique look, complete 
with moving parts that help give it personality, and likely cost more than 
$300, according to people familiar with the matter.

 * The product — essentially a smart speaker without a display 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/what-is-openai-s-device-a-doughnut-shaped-speaker-that-costs-over-300?cmpid=080726_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260807&utm_content=7482>
 — will be shaped like a doughnut that’s roughly the size of a hockey puck.
 * OpenAI is looking to break new ground with the product, which is slated for 
release in 2027. The device will be positioned as an AI-first computer that can 
help users get things done, the people said.
 * The new hardware will also give OpenAI a chance to show that it’s not 
cribbing from Apple Inc.’s designs. The two companies are locked in a legal 
battle over whether the AI firm stole Apple’s intellectual property, something 
OpenAI denies.
