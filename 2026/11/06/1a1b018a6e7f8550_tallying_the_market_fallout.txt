political trophy. Now, a reversal that’s wiped out more than a trillion dollars 
in value is stirring a retail investor backlash and testing public faith in its 
leaders.

The wild swings have already inflicted widespread losses in the hedge fund 
industry, including for giant firms such as ExodusPoint Capital Management and 
Millennium Management, though some benefited from the volatility. For an 
industry premised on making money no matter the direction of the market, the
breadth of the damage is stunning

SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son and other tycoons across Asia saw billions 
erased from their fortunes as a global tech selloff rippled through their 
companies on Friday. Twenty-two billionaires from China, Japan, Taiwan, and 
South Korea who rank in the world’s 500 richestlost more than $21 billion in 
combined wealth 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/ai-elite-lose-18-billion-as-chip-stock-rout-spreads-to-asia?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261106&utm_content=11100>
 as of 10 a.m. Hong Kong time, before paring that to more than $18 billion, 
according to calculations from the Bloomberg Billionaires Index. Son was the 
biggest loser, with his fortune declining by $3.9 billion to about $29 billion.

‘Anti-AI’ Trade Makes Indian Stocks Asia’s Lone Bright Spot

For months, investors touted Indian equities as a place to hide if the global 
artificial-intelligence trade unraveled. This week, that thesis is playing out. 
The NSE Nifty 50 Index rose Friday, defying a selloff that left every other 
major Asian market in the red.
Read more 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/indian-stocks-gain-haven-appeal-as-investors-flee-ai-trade?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261106&utm_content=11100>
India’s Diwali sweet box comes with a bitter dose of inflation this year. 
Sugar, the foundation of almost every Indian sweet, costs almost 30% more in 
September than it did a year earlier. Refined cooking oil used for frying rose 
about 15%, raisins 10% and nuts 9% — all of them running far ahead of the 
country’s overall inflation rate. Thecost pressures are filtering through to 
the sweet shops 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/india-diwali-deepavali-hindu-festival-sweet-box-comes-bitter-dose-inflation-sugar?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261106&utm_content=11100>
 of central New Delhi as they prepare for a rush of business before Sunday, 
when most people will celebrate the festival.

Tokyo Is Being Reshaped by a Historic Real Estate Boom

For decades, property prices in Japan barely budged and renters could expect 
their costs to never rise. That era is now over. Rents in Katsushika, one of 
Tokyo’s 23 wards, have surged 23.9% in five years, the most of anywhere in the 
city, according to a Bloomberg analysis of exclusive data from property portal 
estie.
