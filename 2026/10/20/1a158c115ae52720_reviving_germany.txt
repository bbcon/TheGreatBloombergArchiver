Friedrich Merz is hosting the German government’s first investor conference in 
Berlin today, a fresh push bythe embattled chancellor 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-20/merz-s-cdu-posts-worst-state-result-in-postwar-german-history?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>
 to revive growth in Europe’s biggest economy.

Thanks to expansive fiscal spending to modernize Germany’s crumbling 
infrastructure and boost defense capabilities, some early signs that the 
economy isslowly picking up speed 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/german-economy-to-gain-momentum-toward-year-end-bundesbank-says?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>
 are appearing. But it will need vast amounts of private capital to really turn 
things around.

In an interview with Bloomberg TV, Merz stressed that Europe had the ability to
harness its strength 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/merz-vows-to-tap-german-economic-strength-to-revive-investments?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>
 and overcome economic malaise by implementing artificial intelligence for 
industrial operations faster than rivals.

He also indicated that Germany would cut back on its sprawling social welfare 
system.
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-19/merz-russia-s-push-to-destabilize-europe-underestimated-video?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>

: Merz says Russia’s push to destabilize Europe is underestimated.
The German leader, who suffered some political setbacks in recent weeks, says 
it’s crucial for the European Union to assert itself and wield the leverage of 
its 450 million consumers to catch up with the US and China in tech.

“We have something to offer,” he said, adding: “We can catch up and we will do 
that.”

Merz spoke as France and other EU member states are getting swept by 
cost-of-living protests 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/french-violence-raises-alert-as-protests-flare-across-europe?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>
 that have sometimes descended into violence. The German leader says anxiety 
over high prices and an uncertain future are catalysts for extremist forces.

He was also notably outspoken when he warned against complacency over the 
dangers posed by Russia: President Vladimir Putin is seeking to not only defeat 
Ukraine, but to alsoundermine democracy in Europe 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/merz-warns-europe-that-it-s-underestimating-the-russian-threat?cmpid=BBD102026_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261020&utm_content=10307>
.

“My biggest concern is the underestimated Russian impact on our societies,” 
Merz cautioned. “Russia is on the way to destabilize the entire political order
