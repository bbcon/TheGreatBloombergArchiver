Deep Dive: Deutsche Bank’s London Bet

Deutsche Bank’s timing seemed auspicious when it bought the UK corporate 
broker Numis in the spring of 2023, just as the stock market was starting to 
pick up. Three years on,it’s still waiting for the recovery 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/deutsche-bank-numis-deal-yet-to-transform-uk-investment-bank-amid-ipo-drought?cmpid=110426_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261104&utm_content=10985>
.

 * The German lender had hoped for a flurry of lucrative deals from Numis’ 
more than 160 clients at the time of the takeover as well as new business from 
companies looking to list on the London Stock Exchange. Alas, initial public 
offerings remained few and far between. So Deutsche Bank was seen to focus more 
on doing business for bigger British corporates while trying to sell some of 
its other banking services to existing clients.
 * The combined broking business — now called Deutsche Numis — has lost 
roughly a third of its clients since the £410 million takeover was completed in 
2023, according to data provider Adviser Rankings. A number of key staff, 
including top executives and dealmakers, have departed.
 * “The last couple of years have been a challenging period for UK equity 
capital markets, but we have remained very active in M&A,” Ross Mitchinson, CEO 
of Deutsche Numis, told Bloomberg. “While some of that M&A activity reflected a 
continued trend towards UK companies being taken over and delisted, we are now 
seeing early signs of improved market conditions and a gradual rebuilding of 
confidence in UK equities.”
