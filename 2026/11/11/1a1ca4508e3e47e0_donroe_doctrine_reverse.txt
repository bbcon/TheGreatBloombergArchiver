Donald Trump’s “Donroe Doctrine” is supposed to revive an era of US dominance 
in Latin America. So long as countries hitch their tails to the president’s 
kite, the US-led Shield of the Americas promises to usher in security and 
prosperity across the hemisphere.

US voters have sent that kite careening, and some of Trump’s allies in the 
region may be going down with it.

That’s already happened in Brazil with the unexpected triumph of leftist icon 
Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva over his Trump-backed rival Flávio Bolsonaro, an 
upset that foreshadowed last week’s midterm cataclysm for US Republicans.
Lula after casting his vote on Oct. 25.Photographer: Rodrigo Paiva/Getty Images
In Paraguay, Banco Ueno’s meltdown 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/paraguay-s-new-president-taps-ex-regulator-to-lead-central-bank?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>
 just brought down another Trump ally, Santiago Peña.

A diminished US president now risks losing more friends. That’s as his 
Spanish-speaking Secretary of State Marco Rubioflirts with departure 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/rubio-considers-leaving-administration-after-gop-s-midterm-loss?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>
 to pursue the top job himself.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth — architect of deadly strikes on 
“narco-terrorists” in the region —dodged impeachment 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/house-republicans-thwart-massie-s-effort-to-impeach-hegseth?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>
 yesterday but remains under bipartisan fire.

In Argentina, libertarian darling Javier Milei hasn’t been able to vanquish 
inflation despite a $20 billion US currency swap deal. Hefaces voter wrath 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/pope-in-argentina-leo-urges-unity-as-milei-ignores-lula-win-in-brazil?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>
 next year.
President José Antonio Kast welcomes Milei to La Moneda Palace in Santiago on

recession

<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/colombia-seeks-15-billion-imf-deal-as-fiscal-pressure-mounts?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>

Like fellow Trump allies in Ecuador, El Salvador and Peru, ADLE’s predicament 
is aggravated by El Niño’s extreme weather, turbocharged by climate change that 
Trump calls a hoax.

The Donroe trophy belongs to Venezuela. But even as a neocolonial construct, 
this oil-rich countrycan’t keep the lights on 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/trump-s-venezuela-oil-deal-with-betancourt-hinges-on-aging-and-distant-fields?cmpid=BBD111126_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261111&utm_content=11241>
, spurring protests and dialing up pressure to hold elections.

Trump’s most unlikely ally, former socialist firebrand Delcy Rodríguez, faces 
a reckoning at the hands of María Corina Machado as early as next year.

Still flying, at least for now, is the tattered kite of Trump’s Cuba policy
