To European governments, the past two months have been a watershed for 
Russia’s malign activities 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/russia-s-attacks-on-europe-are-getting-more-brazen-and-more-dangerous?cmpid=BBD091726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260917&utm_content=9013>
 on their territory.

An explosives-laden UAV 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/german-airport-resumes-flights-after-explosive-drone-sighting?cmpid=BBD091726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260917&utm_content=9013>
 found at Leipzig-Halle airport near a Ukrainian cargo jet. An armed maritime 
drone discovered off a Romanian gas project under construction in the Black Sea.

Multiple unexplained suspected arson attacks on weapons makers. Fighter jets 
scrambled to down drones over NATO airspace.

All these so-called hybrid attacks take place in a gray zone between war and 
peace 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/us-indictment-exposes-russian-global-assassination-network?cmpid=BBD091726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260917&utm_content=9013>
, making them easily deniable — and harder for governments at the sharp end to 
develop a deterrent. To date, a common European or NATO response has been 
conspicuously absent.

Leipzig may have been a turning point, with the German government publicly 
calling out Moscow for the incident on Sept. 1. Others go unattributed, 
however, both to avert escalation and to avoid revealing intelligence methods.

Government officials see Russia undertaking a more aggressive campaign with a 
higher risk of fatalities as it targets European support for Ukraine in a bid 
to raise the cost of aiding Kyiv. President Donald Trump’s ambivalence toward 
NATO onlyemboldens the Kremlin 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/russia-is-escalating-hybrid-attacks-and-europe-has-no-answer?cmpid=BBD091726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260917&utm_content=9013>
.

Yet even as tensions ratchet up, there is little sign that Russia is achieving 
its aims.

Germany’s public response to the Leipzig attack was to reach for soft targets, 
closing a consulate in Bonn and a Russian cultural center in Berlin. More 
quietly, it made additional weaponry available to Ukraine and remains Kyiv’s 
largest foreign backer.

Russia has long been accused of carrying out covert activities on European 
soil, from disinformation campaigns to targeted assassinations. It regards 
itself as retaliating against Europe for aiding Ukrainian attacks on Russia.

But what’s being witnessed now is of a different scale, with incidents not 
just increasing in number but inthe scope of their recklessness 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-13/ukraine-says-passenger-train-headed-to-warsaw-is-hit-by-drone?cmpid=BBD091726_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260917&utm_content=9013>
.

The fear heard across Europe is that it’s only a matter of time until a hybrid
