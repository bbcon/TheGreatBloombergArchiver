• Europe's fifth heat wave of the summer threatens to erase almost all of the euro area's expected 1.1% GDP growth this year, according to analysis by Triodos Bank.
• Sustained heat and drought are driving deadly wildfires and disrupting energy supply across the bloc.
• Crop harvests have been damaged and reservoirs and rivers depleted, compounding the growth drag.

**Heat Wave Growth Risk**
Triodos Bank's analysis suggests the cumulative economic toll of this summer's heat waves — the fifth to hit Europe this season — could offset nearly all of the region's projected 1.1% GDP expansion for the year. The transmission channels are broad: wildfires have proven deadly and destructive, energy supply has been disrupted amid peak cooling demand, crop harvests have suffered, and reservoirs and rivers have been depleted. The estimate remains a single analytical projection rather than a realized outcome, but it points to extreme heat becoming a material, recurring drag on European output rather than an isolated weather event, with implications for the region's already-fragile growth trajectory heading into the second half of the year.
