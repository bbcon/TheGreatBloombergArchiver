**Brazil: Fiscal and Market Stress**
Brazilian markets continue to price fiscal risk following Lula's reelection, with traders assigning low probability to the structural adjustment needed to ease monetary conditions. The real's depreciation to April 2025 lows reflects capital outflows and elevated rate expectations rather than any near-term macro catalyst. The dynamic illustrates a broader tension in Brazilian policy: electoral outcomes that signal continued spending ambitions are incompatible with the lower borrowing costs required for sustained growth.

**Chile and Andean Markets**
Copper's decline — down more than 10% from recent all-time highs near $15,000 per ton — has collided with Chile-specific production concerns. Codelco output is in a marked downward trend, and an emerging misreporting scandal has further weighed on sentiment. Despite GDP per capita of approximately $35,000, Chile represents only 0.6% of the MSCI Emerging Markets index. Active Latin American fund managers' performance remains overwhelmingly determined by Brazil-Mexico allocation decisions, leaving Andean markets structurally underweighted relative to their economic fundamentals.
