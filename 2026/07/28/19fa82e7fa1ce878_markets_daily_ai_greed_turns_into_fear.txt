Tech wreck

With Asian tech stocks tumbling again 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/korean-stocks-sink-as-chipmakers-plung-on-deepening-ai-fatigue?cmpid=072826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7066>
 this morning, one takeaway is that the “buy the dip” mentality doesn’t seem to 
hold sway anymore.

Instead, traders are all too eager to ditch the hottest AI stocks as jitters 
resurface about frothy valuations and circular, over-the-top spending.

“Greed has turned into fear for AI-related semiconductor stocks,” said 
Vey-Sern Ling, managing director at Union Bancaire Privee. “Investors are now 
interpreting every piece of news negatively and using it as an excuse to sell, 
rather than critically analyzing the true fundamental impact.”

Here’s the latest:

 * South Korea’s Kospi plunged 11% on Tuesday, with Samsung and SK Hynix each 
sliding more than 13%.
 * Japan’s Nikkei 225 and Taiwan’s Taiex both dropped around 4%.
 * The cost of protecting Nvidia debt against default 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/nvidia-credit-risk-jumps-in-swaps-market-on-ai-deal-talk-reports?cmpid=072826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7066>
 surged by the most on record Monday. The jump shows “the credit market 
noticing something the equity market hasn’t fully priced,” says Dilin Wu, a 
strategist at Pepperstone. 
 * Among the triggers for the recent drop 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/asml-slides-after-report-of-china-beginning-duv-tool-production?cmpid=072826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7066>
 is a report from theInformation  
<https://www.theinformation.com/articles/china-starts-mass-producing-homegrown-duv-chipmaking-tools-advance-local-chip-industry>
that a Chinese state-backed company is mass producing certain chipmaking 
machines. While the report didn’t name the company, it has raised some concerns 
about tougher competition in the industry.
 * The downdraft in the US is milder. Nasdaq 100 futures are down less than 1%.
 * Still, for the high flyers of the AI trade, it’s been a painful unwind. Take
SK Hynix, for example 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/sk-hynix-s-rebound-from-470-billion-rout-hinges-on-ai-spending?cmpid=072826_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260728&utm_content=7066>
. The stock has crashed almost 50% from an all-time high in June. Even though 
it’s expected to post record earnings tomorrow, investors are worried that 
rising memory costs will force customers to reduce usage and turn to cheaper 
alternatives.
