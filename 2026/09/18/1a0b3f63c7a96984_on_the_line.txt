It’s always tempting, but usually wrong, to draw national lessons from 
regional elections in any of Germany’s 16 federal states.

This Sunday is different.

The outcome of voting in the city-state of Berlin and in Mecklenburg-Western 
Pomerania in the northeast will go a long way towarddetermining Chancellor 
Friedrich Merz’s fate 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/merz-faces-down-party-revolt-with-far-right-advancing-in-germany?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
.
Merz.Photographer: John Thys/AFP/Getty Images
His future as the head of Europe’s heavyweight nation is on the line 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/merz-cancels-trip-to-un-in-new-york-amidst-party-rebellion?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
, with all the political and economic ramifications that implies.

Merz is a deeply unloved leader, by some measures the least popular chancellor 
on record.

His dilemma isn’t helped by a set of challenges that would make any politician 
wilt.

Intense economic competition with China 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/merz-calls-for-trade-tools-to-fight-unfair-china-competition?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
. The collapse of the US-led world order and Donald Trump’sscorn for allies 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/merz-says-us-friendship-built-since-war-probably-over-for-now?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
. Russia’s war in Ukraine, andsurging energy prices 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/merz-to-respond-to-surging-prices-as-breaking-point-reached?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
 due to hostilities in the Gulf.

All are particularly acute for Germany — still the world’s No. 3 economy — 
with the scars seen in itsbeleaguered auto industry 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/vw-ceo-s-streamlining-push-faces-german-state-s-veto-threat?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
, humbled industrial sector and an export engine buffeted by rising 
protectionism.

Politically, Merz’s misfortune was to ascend to the chancellery last year just 
as the far-right AfD party capitalized on evaporating support for center forces 
like his.

But Merz also lacks the common touch of predecessors like Angela Merkel, a 
fellow Christian Democrat, or Gerhard Schroeder, a Social Democrat.

His hectoring approach to politics, like a supermarket manager attempting to 
bully voters into purchases they don’t want, unsurprisingly fails to resonate.

His CDU party is polling at record lows nationally, well behind the AfD.

Two weeks back, the CDU suffered a calamitous defeat 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-06/german-far-right-afd-set-to-win-state-vote-short-of-majority?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
 to an aggressively anti—migrant, nationalist AfD in Saxony-Anhalt.

Polls suggest further humiliations on Sunday.

Merz wants to hold on, yet party officials staring at political oblivion 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-05/-we-can-only-win-the-rise-of-the-far-right-in-eastern-germany?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
 may see it as the tipping point for the chancellor.

Just 16 months in office, Merz already looks out of time. — Alan Crawford 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AOTqYzZDKuY/alan-crawford?sref=LlL4jr17&cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>

<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/intense-heat-strains-power-grids-for-100-million-north-americans?cmpid=BBD091826_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260918&utm_content=9061>
