Both Russia and the US have far more nuclear weapons than China, but Xi 
Jinping will be the most powerful leader in the room when he sits down with 
Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin on Thursday.

Trump is fresh off a shellacking in the midterms, while Putin — still firmly 
in charge — facesgrowing economic strain 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/russian-economy-slows-as-ukrainian-strikes-weigh-on-output?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
 as his war with Ukraine trudges through its fifth year.

During that span, his dependence on Beijing has increased dramatically 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/russian-push-to-escape-dollar-for-yuan-collapses-under-sanctions?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
: China now accounts for about half of Russia’s imports, nearly double the 
level before the full-scale invasion.

Xi, meanwhile, is all but guaranteed to secure a fourth term in office next 
year, despite his ownmounting economic challenges 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/china-s-economy-weakens-more-than-forecast-despite-stimulus-push?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
. With Trump looking more like a lame duck and Putin a junior partner, the 
Chinese president will seek to further press his long-term goals at their 
unprecedented meetingin Shenzhen 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-11-15/futuristic-shenzhen-shows-trump-the-difficulty-of-stopping-xi-jinping-s-china?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
, where Xi is hosting Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation leaders.
 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-15/trump-heads-to-asia-with-trade-security-at-stake-video?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>

While the trilateral summit is unlikely to yield much substance, each wants 
something out of it.

Putin gets a seat at the table with the heads of the world’s two biggest 
economies, a diplomatic coup that will enrage Europe andexpand his leverage 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/putin-tests-europe-s-unity-with-new-pressure-to-talk-security?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
. Trump gets to show he can still makewaves on the world stage 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/weakened-at-home-trump-looks-to-flex-power-at-putin-xi-summit?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
 and perhaps press for help ending his war in Iran.

Xi will look to burnish his credentials as a global statesman and advance 
China’s broader strategic interests: avoiding limits on its nuclear program, 
asserting Beijing’s claims over Taiwan and weakening US alliances in Europe and 
Asia,particularly Japan 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/hello-kitty-owner-sanrio-axes-japan-show-ties-after-china-furor?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
.

Much has been made of whether this meeting represents a new Yalta, when 
Franklin Roosevelt, Winston Churchill and Joseph Stalin met to carve up the 
post-World War II globe.

While this meeting won’t produce a grand bargain, it allows Xi to project a 
powerful reminder that China will play a central role in shaping the global 
order for decades to come.— Daniel Ten Kate 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/APUt5nEgYZQ/daniel-ten-kate?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>

And Finally

The failure of a United Launch Alliance rocket carrying 40 Amazon satellites 
is a majorsetback for a space industry 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/pentagon-rocket-provider-hit-by-failure-while-spacex-soars?cmpid=BBD111626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261116&utm_content=11454>
 struggling to meet soaring demand — and provide alternatives to Elon Musk’s 
SpaceX. Amazon’s Leo satellite network is designed to compete with Musk’s 
Starlink, and yesterday’s failed mission threatens to delay plans by ULA, a 
venture between Boeing and Lockheed Martin, to accelerate launches. It also 
stands in stark contrast to SpaceX, which returned its Starship from orbit at 
the weekend, catching it midair using giant mechanical arms at a Texas launch 
site in a first-of-its-kind engineering feat.
