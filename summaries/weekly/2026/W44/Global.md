**Oil Markets and the Hormuz Risk Premium**
The structure of the oil futures curve has shifted materially since Gulf hostilities began. Brent's 12-month forward contract is at levels last seen immediately after Russia's 2022 Ukraine invasion, and the November 2027 contract has reached an all-time high — implying markets price a sustained disruption rather than a temporary one. The contradiction in the US position — Vice President Vance conducting outreach to Tehran while President Trump simultaneously signals renewed post-midterm strikes — sustains a high-uncertainty environment. A third scenario, a prolonged stalemate that becomes a structural feature of the market, appears underpriced.
