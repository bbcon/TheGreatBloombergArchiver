Rio de Janeiro has drawn countless visitors on the fame of its golden 
coastline and intoxicating samba.

Its dirty politics are just as legendary 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-09-16/rio-s-crime-and-corruption-are-shaping-brazil-s-presidential-election?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
 — and helped propel Brazil’s premier conservative political family onto the 
national stage.

Now, Flávio Bolsonaro is attempting to carry his family’s Rio-rooted brand of 
politics to Brazil’s top job. In his bid to unseat President Luiz Inácio Lula 
da Silva, he’s leaning on a message forged in a city consumed by crime, 
corruption and broken promises to voters.

It might just be enough 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/brazil-election-bolsonaro-surges-in-poll-as-supreme-court-crisis-weighs-on-lula?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
 to win.

Polls show Bolsonaro, the son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, neck and 
neck with Lula going into the Oct. 4 first round.

To prevail, he’s deploying a battle-tested strategy, casting his opponents as 
soft on crime and tainted by graft. It’s the same playbook his father rode to 
the presidency a decade ago.
Flávio Bolsonaro speaks in São Paulo on Sept. 7.Photographer: Maira 
Erlich/Bloomberg
His attacks in recent days have centered on tying Lula to a Supreme Court 
judge under scrutiny 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/alexandre-de-moraes-says-probe-against-him-driven-by-politics?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
 over his relationship with Daniel Vorcaro, the former owner of Banco Master, 
the now-defunct lender at the center of a massive banking fraud.

The president has not been directly implicated in the scandal. But the 
Bolsonaros have long cast Justice Alexandre de Moraes as aligned with the 
leftist incumbent; the judge was among those who convicted Jair Bolsonaro of
attempting a coup 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-11/brazil-s-bolsonaro-found-guilty-of-coup-attempt-by-majority-of-top-court-panel?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
 after his 2022 reelection loss to Lula.

That line of attack has been complicated by Flávio Bolsonaro’s own ties to 
Vorcaro 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-14/bolsonaro-ties-to-banco-master-ceo-rock-brazil-presidential-race?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
. Police are now investigating those dealings for potential crimes including 
corruption and money laundering.

But allegations of misconduct have followed the challenger throughout his time 
in Rio without halting his ascent.

Flávio Bolsonaro denies any wrongdoing. Yet with much of Brazil’s political 
elite smeared by the scandal, his larger challenge may be convincing voters 
that he can match his father’s appeal. — Andrew Rosati 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASFv3NN53es/andrew-rosati?cmpid=BBD091626_politics&utm_campaign=bop&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260916&utm_content=8957>
