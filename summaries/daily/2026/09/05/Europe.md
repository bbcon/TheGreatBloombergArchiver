**Inflation and Monetary Policy**
Eurozone inflation remains above target, and the ECB faces a policy environment complicated by renewed energy price pressures stemming from the US-Iran conflict. The dynamic mirrors the US experience: persistent above-target inflation despite years of tightening, with fiscal policy providing limited consolidation. France is specifically highlighted among developed economies facing structural fiscal credibility challenges, a concern that has contributed to the broader repricing of developed-market sovereign debt. Ten-year yields across core eurozone markets have risen alongside Treasuries, reflecting both the energy shock and the fiscal risk premium being demanded by investors.

**Food Prices and Environmental Policy**
Global food prices reaching their highest since late 2022 in August — driven by geopolitical tensions and extreme weather — represent an upside risk to European inflation prints in the months ahead. The climate dimension is relevant to European agricultural conditions as well, with the Nepal disaster illustrating the broader pattern of climate-related supply disruptions affecting global commodity markets. The interplay between energy costs, food prices, and still-elevated core inflation presents a challenging set of conditions for the ECB as it calibrates the pace of any further policy adjustment.
