**Housing**
Redfin data show 51.3% more home sellers than buyers in July, a deep buyer's market with few actual buyers. The median existing-home price held near June's record $434,000, the 30-year mortgage rate remained elevated at 6.67%, and homebuilder sentiment has stayed below the breakeven level of 50 for 16 months, the longest such stretch since 2012. KPMG's Yelena Maleyev pointed to an emerging "K-shaped" market splitting well-capitalized, high-end builders from the rest, and expects residential construction — already a drag in seven of the last nine quarters — to weigh further on growth in the back half of the year.

**Fiscal Policy and Markets**
Federal debt is nearing $40 trillion against a roughly $41 trillion statutory ceiling; Evercore ISI expects the limit to bind in the first half of 2027, potentially forcing a standoff with a Congress Democrats may partly control. Equities have so far absorbed rising long-end yields, with resilient corporate profits cited as the reason the S&P 500 hasn't followed AI-linked chip names lower.
