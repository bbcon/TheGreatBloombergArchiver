**China: Deflation Risk and Leverage Controls**
China's July inflation data present a deteriorating picture. CPI decelerated to 0.5% YoY — the weakest since January — from 1.0% in June, while PPI eased to 3.5% from 4.1%, its first moderation since the Iran war began in February. Both prints missed expectations, raising renewed deflation concerns even as the trade account remains robust. In response to an AI-driven margin debt frenzy that pushed the balance above 3 trillion yuan in late June, Chinese brokerages including Citic Securities have tightened compliance requirements. The margin balance has since retreated to 2.6 trillion yuan. Beijing appears to be threading a narrow path: supporting AI-related equities for strategic reasons while containing speculative leverage that contributed to July's market turbulence.

**Regional Central Banks**
The Bank of Japan's July meeting summary flagged rising upside price risks, with one board member raising the possibility of an accelerated tightening pace, sustaining pressure on the yen. Meanwhile, Indonesia's President Prabowo nominated Destry Damayanti as Bank Indonesia governor following Perry Warjiyo's unexpected departure, with parliament expected to consider the appointment from August 14. In the Philippines, Governor Eli Remolona signaled readiness to tighten "as much as necessary" despite Q2 GDP growth slumping unexpectedly to 2.3%, as inflation at 6.2% runs more than double the government's target.
