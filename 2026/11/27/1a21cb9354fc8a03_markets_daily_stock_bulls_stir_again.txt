Post-turkey rally

Americans are emerging from their tryptophan-induced 
<https://inside.charlotte.edu/2024/11/22/the-truth-about-tryptophan-and-thanksgiving-turkey-tiredness/>
 Thanksgiving haze to find ... stocks are up? Yes indeed, futures are rising 
sharply and there are signs that weeks of unease over AI are giving way to a 
cautious rebound.

“The market seems to be stabilizing,” said Gerald Gan of Reed Capital. 
“Although it could be a dead-cat bounce, our base case still suggests that we 
will have a final melt-up till the end of the year.”

In case you need reminding: The Nasdaq 100 isn’t far from entering a bear 
market.While the index is up 4% this week, that’s little consolation for 
investors who have endured the 17% plunge since early October.

On a monthly basis, the index has fallen about 8%, which would be the biggest 
drop since 2022.

Government action may be helping to improve sentiment. President Trump 
intervened to bring Jensen Huang back to Nvidia, and while that bump proved 
short-lived, Taiwan, South Korea and now China have acted to support stocks.

As Bloomberg’s Garfield Reynolds writes from Sydney, investors may decide that 
the upshot of all this is that global leaders are drawing a line in the sand 
for stock markets. If that’s true, equity traders may start buying the dip, he 
said.

Frederic Rozier at Mirabaud Asset Management agrees there could be a 
short-term bounce ahead.

“There’s clearly some signs of a technical rebound unfolding. Many sectors 
were oversold and valuations were quashed, so there’s some upside potential in 
the short term,” he said. “That said, we remain prudent in the mid-longer term 
on the direction of travel.” —Charles Riley 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXaNtvarAGI/charles-riley?cmpid=112726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261127&utm_content=11942>

 * The AI trade is roaring back. CoreWeave +7%, Micron +7.6% and optical 
module maker Coherent +8.9%.
 * Miners also rebound. Freeport-McMoRan +6.2%, Newmont +5.4% and Coeur +7.5%.

Dollar strength

The dollar is headed for its longest winning streak 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/-dollar-storm-greenback-on-track-for-longest-winning-run-since-2024?cmpid=112726_marketsdaily&utm_campaign=marketsdaily&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261127&utm_content=11942>
 in two years.

The Bloomberg Dollar Spot Index is up 1.9% in November, on track for a third 
straight month of gains. The greenback has strengthened against all of its G-10 
peers, including the Japanese yen and the Swiss franc.

The sustained move has its roots in the tech-stock slump, combined with strong 
economic growth and high inflation. Some strategists expect the rally to extend 
into 2027 as AI spending drives US growth and capital flows.

“The US economy is booming and inflation is still moving away from the 
target,” said Win Thin of Bank of Nassau 1982. “The Fed needs to hike rates to 
help cool the economy, perhaps in both December and January. That’s dollar 
positive.” —Cole Lipsky
