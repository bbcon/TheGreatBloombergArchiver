**ECB Leadership Vacuum**

Schnabel's early departure creates an immediate vacancy within the ECB Executive Board, intensifying pressure on eurozone governments to act on succession planning with fewer than three months before her exit. With President Lagarde and Chief Economist Lane both scheduled to depart in 2027 — and Lagarde having hinted at an earlier exit — a coordinated package deal on ECB leadership now appears increasingly probable. The institution faces this transition against a backdrop of elevated inflation, a global bond selloff, and unresolved fiscal fragmentation risks across the eurozone.

**United Kingdom**

The 12% drop in UK food export volumes in H1 represents a meaningful structural challenge for British producers, with US tariffs identified as the primary driver. Concurrently, food imports are near record highs, raising questions about supply-chain dependency. The Bank of England's position has grown more hawkish: Governor Bailey's comments that a rate hike is becoming difficult to avoid reflect persistent domestic inflation pressures even as growth remains fragile. Banks are actively exploiting cheap BOE repo financing to run profitable bond trades, a dynamic that bears monitoring from a financial stability perspective.
