**Energy and Economic Competitiveness**
Europe's gas storage outlook has deteriorated materially. If the Hormuz closure persists through end-March — the BloombergNEF base case — storage could fall to 17% full, the lowest in more than a decade, with a cold winter scenario pushing the figure to 10%. Wood Mackenzie estimates that European industry has already reduced gas consumption by roughly one-fifth since 2022, leaving limited further demand-reduction capacity without triggering a recession. Households remain heavily dependent on gas for heating, and a 17% end-of-winter storage level would create, in Wood Mackenzie's assessment, "large upside risk to prices" through 2027.
