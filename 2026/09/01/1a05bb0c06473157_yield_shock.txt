Deep Dive: A Beer Legend Takes on Windshields
Carlos Brito.Photographer: Isabelle Pateer for Bloomberg Businessweek
Like most people, legendary brewing executive Carlos Brito 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-09-01/belron-ceo-carlos-brito-on-car-windshield-repair-industry-s-global-growth?cmpid=090126_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260901&utm_content=8383>
 never gave a lot of thought to windshields. Sure, they shield you from the 
rain — and, well, wind — when you’re driving. But, he thought, “it’s a piece of 
glass, it breaks, you replace it,” so installing them shouldn’t be particularly 
complicated, right?

 * These days, he’ll tell you just how wrong he was. “The windshield has all 
of these magic things it does,” says Brito, CEO of Belron Group, a global chain 
that operates under brand names such as Safelite in the US, Carglass in Europe 
and Autoglass in the UK. “It’s way more than a piece of glass.”
 * Today’s windshields are increasingly alive with cameras, heating filaments 
and special coatings. So, alongside replacing a cracked pane, technicians must 
recalibrate safety features like auto-braking and systems that deploy airbags 
correctly and keep you from accidentally veering out of your lane.
 * And windshields are likely to keep getting bigger. Teslas, for instance, 
have both an oversize windshield and a glass roof coated in heat-resistant 
chemicals designed to reduce the need for air conditioning. For Belron this 
complexity means more chips and cracks, more sophisticated glass, longer jobs — 
and growing sales.
 * Brito knows a lot about windshields for a guy who spent more than three 
decades in the brewing industry. He was the mastermind behind the rollup of 
hundreds of beer brands into what’s now called AB InBev, a colossus that 
controls more than a quarter of the global beer market. Along the way, he 
triggered ire, even lawsuits, for hisruthless takeover tactics 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-09-28/sabmiller-investors-give-go-ahead-for-103-billion-megabrew-deal?cmpid=090126_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260901&utm_content=8383>
