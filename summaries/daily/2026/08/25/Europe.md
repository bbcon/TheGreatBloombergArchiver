**German Recovery Gains Traction Amid Corporate Retrenchment**
Germany's upgraded GDP print and improving business outlook point to a trade-led recovery gaining traction even as the industrial base restructures; Volkswagen's cuts, alongside Chancellor Friedrich Merz's warning that a foiled drone attack at a German airport signals renewed hybrid-threat exposure, underscore the tension between cyclical improvement and structural and security pressures facing the export economy.

**UK: Heat, Housing, and Fiscal Watch**
