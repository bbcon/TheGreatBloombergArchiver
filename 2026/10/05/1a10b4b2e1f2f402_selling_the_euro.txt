The big news in markets today was the euro, which fell to its weakest since 
May 2025 in Asian trade 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/euro-falls-to-17-month-low-on-region-s-fiscal-political-risks?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261005&utm_content=9703>
. The driver was intensifying investor concerns over political and fiscal risks 
in the region.

Fast-money funds in Asia sold the euro for dollars 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/audio/2026-10-05/spain-s-sanchez-calls-snap-election-us-bombers-leave-uk-more?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261005&utm_content=9703>
 in spot trading, according to traders who asked not to be named as they are 
not authorized to speak publicly. That pushed prices to levels that triggered 
additional options-related selling, they said.

The shared currency fell as much as 0.8% to touch $1.1161 during the Asian 
day, with hedge fund selling a feature. Reports thatSpanish government 
officials were preparing 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-05/spanish-pm-calls-snap-election-as-housing-plan-fails-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261005&utm_content=9703>
 for an early election — Prime Minister Pedro Sánchezdid indeed call an 
election for Nov. 29 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/spain-s-sanchez-calls-snap-election-after-housing-plan-fails?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261005&utm_content=9703>
 — increased the prospect of fresh political upheaval in the region. That 
injected more risk into markets already grappling with strains in French and 
other vulnerable sovereign debt.

On Friday, the premium investors charge to hold French bonds over equivalent 
bunds hit levels not seen since 2011. And there are more risks on the horizon. 
Japanese investors hold far more French bonds than benchmark weightings 
recommend, raising thethreat of a new wave of selling 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/french-bonds-at-risk-of-more-japanese-selling-on-outsized-stake?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261005&utm_content=9703>
 that could deepen France’s debt rout.
