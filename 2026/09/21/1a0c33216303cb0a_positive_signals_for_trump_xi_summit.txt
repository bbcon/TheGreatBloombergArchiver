The Trump-Xi show has started in earnest, ahead of the much-anticipated summit 
between the leaders this week. Whiletrade is the perennial bugbear 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/china-buys-more-us-soybeans-just-days-before-trump-xi-summit?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260921&utm_content=9132>
 between the world’s largest economies, it’s artificial intelligence that’s 
grabbing headlines.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent lauded “very successful” talks 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/bessent-hails-very-successful-china-talks-on-ai-threats-trade?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260921&utm_content=9132>
 with his Chinese counterparts on AI, trade and investment Sunday, while China 
described the discussions as positive ahead of this week’s summit.

Bessent, speaking to reporters after hours of talks in New York with China’s 
Vice Premier He Lifeng and other officials, said the two sides agreed toset up 
a dialogue on AI 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-21/us-china-hold-ai-trade-talks-ahead-of-trump-xi-summit-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260921&utm_content=9132>
 aimed at reaching a common understanding on goals and threats. China’s 
official Xinhua news agency called the talks “candid, in-depth and 
constructive,” and confirmed that the officials held a dialogue on AI but gave 
no further details.
