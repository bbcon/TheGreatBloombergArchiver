erased the opposition’s majority on the city council.

 * Closing in: The opposition won 185 of Istanbul’s 319 council seats in 2024, 
but that majority has dwindled to almost nothing. Eleven districts have since 
shifted to AK Party control through trustee appointments, council votes and 
defections rather than new elections.
 * Opposition crackdown: Sixteen of the 26 opposition district mayors elected 
in 2024 have been arrested, along with hundreds of lower-level officials. 
Critics accuse Erdogan of using the justice system to reverse his party’s 
electoral losses; the Justice Ministry has previously said the courts aren’t 
politically influenced by the executive.
 * Why Istanbul matters: City hall controls a $10 billion annual budget and 
has sway over an economy generating roughly $500 billion a year. Erdogan, who 
began his political rise as Istanbul’s mayor, once summed up its importance: 
“Whoever wins Istanbul, wins Turkey.”
