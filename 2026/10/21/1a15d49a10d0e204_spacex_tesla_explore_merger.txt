in riches and fame.

 * The firm’s meteoric ascent was making many executives uneasy inside Tera’s 
headquarters in Istanbul, and some had begun to speak up. Tezmen was brashly 
hyping the firm as the Goldman Sachs of Turkey. And so much cash was pouring in 
from mesmerized retail investors that the firm needed to take on ever-growing 
heaps of leverage to keep generating the spectacular returns.
 * Internal disputes grew so heated that on one afternoon in February, 
Tezmen’s most trusted lieutenant, Emir Munir Sarpyener, got into a shouting 
match with other top executives, according to people familiar.
 * Regulators at the stock exchange knew troubles were brewing, seeing what 
Tera executives feared they would see: a strategy that appeared to revolve 
around creating massive paper gains and could be pumped over and over again.
