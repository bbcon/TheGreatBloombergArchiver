 that will impact more than one-fifth of the workforce, as artificial

Some 1,271 people, or 22% of Canva’s staff, will lose their jobs, change roles

that was posted on the firm’s website. The sweeping changes at the online 
design and publishing platform mirror the broader upheaval across the software 
industry.
