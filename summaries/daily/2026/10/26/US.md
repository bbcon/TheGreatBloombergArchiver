**Monetary Policy**
The FOMC convenes Wednesday and is expected to hold rates unchanged, with Fed Chair Kevin Warsh having signaled no urgency to hike in his final pre-purdah remarks to the IMF. September CPI showed a disquieting broad-based increase that briefly prompted markets to price in a higher probability of an October hike, but successive Fed communications — most notably from New York Fed President John Williams — walked those expectations back. The pattern suggests Warsh, despite his stated preference for abandoning forward guidance, has effectively been pulled back toward market management by the scale of the reaction to his early press conferences. The 10-year yield and market-implied fed funds rate one year forward have moved in close alignment, raising the unresolved question of whether the Fed is driving the bond market or vice versa.

**Markets and Earnings**
The Nasdaq 100 and long-duration Treasuries have both sold off over the past month, compressing the AI-and-bonds trade that defined much of 2026. Alphabet, Microsoft, and Amazon results this week are pivotal: the market is less focused on headline earnings growth than on AI spending trajectories and evidence of monetization. Apollo's Torsten Slok has noted that AI has delivered measurable payoff only within tech itself. Biotech has been a notable exception, rising roughly 5% over the past month as AI-driven drug discovery and a wave of M&A transactions offset the broader tech selloff.
