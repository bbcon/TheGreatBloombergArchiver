In Massena, New York, near the Canadian border, an industrial plant that was 
once one of America’s largest aluminum smelters is attracting a very different 
kind of occupant.

Since 2018 a portion of the site has been used to mine Bitcoin. New York 
Digital Investment Group wants to convert the sprawling complex into a data 
center consuming as much as 635 megawatts of electricity, enough to supply more 
than half a million American homes, according to astory this week 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-11-24/ai-data-centers-are-eating-up-power-us-factories-need?cmpid=BBD112526_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261125&utm_content=11744>
 in Bloomberg Markets by Jacob Lorinc and Shawn Donnan.

The project is a sliver of an infrastructure boom rarely seen in the US since 
the 19th-century railroad expansion. A Columbia Business School economist 
quoted in the story estimates the cost of building data centers, power supplies 
and computing equipment through 2032 at almost $9 trillion.

Yet two of President Donald Trump’s economic priorities are competing for 
electric power: the rapid construction of data centers with foreign components 
that remain largely tariff-free, and the revival of domestic aluminum 
production with the use of levies on foreign imports.

The Democrats’ surprisingly big victories in the midterm elections 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-11-04/midterm-elections-2026-trump-must-now-contend-with-democrats-in-control?cmpid=BBD112526_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261125&utm_content=11744>
 highlight the tension between the economic goals. The vote revealed a broad 
backlash against tariffs because of their impact on consumer prices, yet 
dropping them could further erode the US aluminum industry.

As the story explains, US manufacturers are paying the highest aluminum prices 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/london-aluminum-market-faces-biggest-supply-squeeze-in-decades?cmpid=BBD112526_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261125&utm_content=11744>
 in the world.

Trump has also recently threatened to impose 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/trump-threatens-to-tax-companies-building-data-centers-abroad?cmpid=BBD112526_TRADE&utm_campaign=trade&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261125&utm_content=11744>
 a “tremendous tax” on American companies that build data centers outside the 
US, and dismissed concerns that more data centers threaten to raise electricity 
prices for consumers.
