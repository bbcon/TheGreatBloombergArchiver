<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-05/danube-river-hits-record-lows-and-threatens-romania-hungary-nuclear-plants?cmpid=080526_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260805&utm_content=7366>
A controlled underwater explosion on the Danube riverbed on Aug. 3.
Photographer: Daniel Mihailescu/AFP/Getty Images
With its water level at a record low, the shrunken Danube River is disrupting 
trade and tourist cruises 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-05/danube-river-hits-record-lows-and-threatens-romania-hungary-nuclear-plants?cmpid=080526_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260805&utm_content=7366>
 and making locals along its banks from Bratislava to Belgrade confront the 
impact of climate change.

 * But upriver from Romania’s only nuclear power plant, raising that water 
level by a few inches is now mission critical to maintaining electricity 
supplies.
 * The week started with blasting a rock formation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/romania-blasts-rock-to-redirect-danube-flows-to-nuclear-plant?cmpid=080526_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260805&utm_content=7366>
 on the river with nearly 400 pounds of explosives. That added a much-needed 
few centimeters to avert a full shutdown of the Cernavoda plant.
 * The next step is to sink the loaded barges today in an attempt to raise the 
Danube further and push the water down river toward the nuclear facility.
 * While the Rhine is more critical to Europe as an industrial supply line, 
the Danube is a lifeblood to communities in the 10 countries it either passes 
through or for which it forms a national border. Four capitals — Vienna, 
Bratislava, Budapest and Belgrade — sit on it and the river provides drinking 
water to about 10 million people.
