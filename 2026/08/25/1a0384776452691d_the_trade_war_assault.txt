With munition shortages limiting America’s ability to wage war with Iran, 
President Donald Trump has turned to a trusted weapon that won’t run short: 
tariffs.

Trump is taking the tariff fight 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/trump-s-iran-pivot-from-war-to-sanctions-will-hit-china-or-fail?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 to countries that are hampering the US from achieving its goal of Iran’s 
“economic asphyxiation:” namely China, which is the biggest buyer of Iranian 
oil and serves as a crucial link to the global economy for Tehran.

This shift means the new pressure point is not the Strait of Hormuz but 
Chinese companies. AnyUS campaign 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-15/what-bessent-s-economic-isolation-of-iran-could-look-like?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 that excludes China would be ineffective, but targeting China would likely
trigger retaliation 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/china-defends-cooperation-with-iran-warns-against-disrupting-it-mt8dnid2?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 from Beijing — and potentially more pain for the global economy.

China is already facing rising tariff pressure independently of Iran. The US 
is set to impose a 7.5%overcapacity levy 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/us-eyes-china-overcapacity-tariffs-of-7-5-before-xi-trump-talks?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 on Chinese goods before a planned summit between Trump and President Xi 
Jinping next month, restoring total duties to 20%. The European Union’s 
existing and proposedtrade measures 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/eu-s-curbs-threaten-27-of-chinese-exports-to-bloc-goldman-says?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 against China could cover about 27% of its annual exports to the bloc, 
according to Goldman Sachs.

Trump’s threats may be bluster. He previously said 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-01/us-iran-talks-hit-snag-as-oman-says-next-round-is-postponed?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 the US would impose secondary sanctions on any nation or company buying 
Iranian oil and never followed through, most likely because he would need to 
primarily target China. Yet the shortage of good options over the Iran conflict 
is increasing the risk of a trade war between world’s two largest economies — 
and adding to theaccumulating pressures 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-15/asian-stock-rebound-masks-rising-risks-from-ai-ipo-hangover-smart-money-exodus?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 on Asian investors.

By switching to tariffs, Trump is no longer constrained by capacity (even if 
he islimited by 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/trump-s-latest-10-tariffs-declared-unlawful-by-us-trade-court?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 US court rulings). That amplifies the risk oflasting damage 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-13/iran-war-will-dubai-s-expat-hub-face-a-hong-kong-style-exodus?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 to global trade channels.

The cryptocurrency climbed above $80,000 for the first time since mid-May after 
rallying 23% last week — the most in about three years.

Bessent’s bond purchases are boosting the investment case 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/bitcoin-reaches-three-month-high-of-80-000-as-momentum-returns?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 for Bitcoin by reviving the dollardebasement trade 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/dollar-usd-outlook-what-is-the-debasement-trade-and-why-does-it-matter?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260825&utm_content=8145>
 alongwith gold
