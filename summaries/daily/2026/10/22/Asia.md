• The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas raised its key rate by 50 basis points to 5.50%, a surprise move seen by only one of 25 economists surveyed, in response to resurgent inflation and a peso at a record low.

**China: Markets and AI**
Chinese mainland indices have become some of the world's worst performers, with the bear market reflecting a combination of structurally weak domestic consumption and investor skepticism about the profitability of domestic AI companies. The contrast with funding activity is notable: DeepSeek raised approximately $15 billion in its latest round — one of China's largest-ever — with CATL and Tencent among anchor investors, signaling that private capital retains conviction in AI even as public markets do not. Huawei separately secured a $2.25 billion bank loan to fund chip-related AI projects. Alibaba's shares fell sharply after Chinese regulators summoned e-commerce operators over product-quality standards.

**Southeast Asia**
The Philippines central bank's outsized rate hike reflects the dual pressure confronting emerging Asian economies: imported inflation from elevated global energy prices and currency weakness amplifying that pass-through. The peso at a record low compounds the challenge. Indonesia faces a separate institutional risk, with President Prabowo defying MSCI's threat to downgrade the country from emerging-market to standalone status — a move that triggered equity outflows last week and remains unresolved.
