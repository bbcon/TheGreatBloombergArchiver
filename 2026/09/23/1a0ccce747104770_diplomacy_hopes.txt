Deep Dive: Cornell Warns of ‘Jarring’ Skill Gap
Photographer: Bing Guam/Bloomberg
US universities are facing challenges that undermine their standing 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/cornell-warns-of-jarring-skill-gap-as-students-fret-over-costs?cmpid=092326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260923&utm_content=9214>
 in American society, including from AI, rising costs and political pressures, 
leading to an “unprecedented” crisis, according to a committee of Cornell 
University faculty.

 * The 238-page report by Cornell’s Committee on the Future of the American 
University comes as universities combat declining trust 
<https://provost.cornell.edu/_files/initiatives/future-of-the-american-university-full-report.pdf>
 and the Trump administration seeks to punish some elite schools over what it 
sees as political bias in hiring and recruitment.
 * A faculty committee at Yale University earlier this year asked the Ivy 
League institution to considerscaling back legacy admissions 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-15/yale-committee-backs-curbing-legacy-admissions-laptops-in-class?cmpid=092326_morningemea&utm_campaign=morningemea&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260923&utm_content=9214>
, implement clearer grading standards and limit laptops in classrooms, as part 
of a slate of recommendations.
 * Cornell committee members said they solicited feedback from university 
stakeholders. A key theme in those discussions was growing skepticism about the 
value of the American college experience, along with concerns about the 
readiness of graduates entering the workforce.
 * According to the report, recent college grads are lacking the “ability to 
handle uncertainty and solve problems that do not have clear answers, a 
readiness to absorb critical feedback and recover from setbacks, and working in 
teams that require compromise with others who do not share their perspective or 
assumptions.”
