Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto isn’t letting up on his campaign to root 
out corruption.

In a nearly two-hour State of the Nation speech before parliament, Prabowo 
lashed out at 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/indonesia-s-prabowo-opens-new-fronts-in-war-on-state-corruption?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7736>
 bureaucrats, state firms, police and the militaryover graft 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/gold-bars-pile-of-us-singapore-dollars-mark-fall-of-top-indonesia-graft-hunter?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7736>
 and waste.

“I cannot yet say that all of the nation’s problems have been resolved or that 
the promises I made have been fulfilled. Not yet,” he said. “I’m not saying 
it’s done — we’re just getting started.”

Prabowo singled out the country’s “confusing” bureaucracy and self-serving 
officials as a top problem. He also threatened to investigate the leaders of 
state-owned enterprises — going back 30 years — saying the companies had been 
operating without accountability. He floated an ad hoc court for such 
investigations and suggested amnesty could be granted for those “who repent and 
recognize their wrongdoing.”

His comments come as the country’s financial markets have steadied 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/indonesian-bonds-may-have-seen-worst-of-selling-analysts-say?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7736>
 after tumbling earlier in the yearon concern over 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/-sell-indonesia-sweeps-trading-desks-as-prabowo-tightens-grip?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7736>
 capital outflows and Prabowo’s policymaking and spending. The government is 
targetinga fiscal shortfall 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/prabowo-pledges-lower-budget-deficit-strong-growth-in-2027?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=260814&utm_content=7736>
 of 2.4% of gross domestic product next year, lower than the expected 2.85% 
this year, Prabowo said Friday.

In an indication of the intensity of his administration’s push, he said some 
of his ministers had fainted or been hospitalized under the workload.

“I promised them that, for the remainder of my term,” he said, “I would give 
them more time to rest.”
