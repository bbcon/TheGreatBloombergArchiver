**Energy Flows and the Iran War**
Oil's retreat toward $87 reflects Gulf producers routing around the Hormuz blockade rather than any resolution of the conflict; Iran's military says it also reached a revenue-sharing arrangement with Oman over the waterway. Washington is nonetheless escalating pressure, with the Justice Department preparing to revive dormant "prize court" maritime law to seize and sell Iranian tankers and cargo.

**Nepal and Climate Risk**
Scientists attribute the flood to a large ice-and-rock avalanche detaching from a glacier, generating seismic waves equivalent to a 5.2-magnitude earthquake and blocking a river before it surged roughly 170 kilometers downstream. The UN says Himalayan glaciers melted 65% faster from 2010-2020 than the prior decade, with flood risk expected to roughly triple by century's end.
