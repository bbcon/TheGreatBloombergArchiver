International, according to documents reviewed by Bloomberg News.

Some of the servers were originally sold to Aperia Cloud Services, an official 
Nvidia Cloud Partner headquartered in Singapore. But instead of staying in the 
island nation, the hardware wound up in the hands of a Chinese company Alice 
founded, at a sprawling AI facility in Shanghai. That project has several ties 
to Alice, including that Megaspeed — which she founded in 2023 — used a 
rendering of the data center in a 2024 investor presentation. Megaspeed is 
itself alsosuspected of semiconductor diversion

Microsoft Outage Gums Up Markets, Forces Traders to Use Phones

Microsoft Outlook went dark on Rita Elkazzi just as a volatile week in markets 
was coming to an end. As swings in AI stocks and bonds kept traders on edge, 
the UBS execution-sales head for Australia and New Zealand was forced onto 
phones and alternative channels to communicate. Elkazzi’s experience is echoed 
by many after Microsoft suffered one of its most severe disruptions in recent 
memory. The tech giant is still struggling to restore access to some of its 
flagship software hours after an outage began, affecting its Outlook, Teams and 
Copilot systems. The difficulties have hindered workflows on trading desks, 
delayed corporate disclosure and even affected email access for Japan’s 
Ministry of Finance.

Nvidia shares plunged more than 14% before dip buyers stepped in, cutting the 
loss to 4.3% by Thursday’s close. It is due to report third-quarter earnings on 
Tuesday.

Meanwhile, just when global bond investors were starting to believe the worst 
of this year’s selloff was over, surging Treasury yields are making them think 
again. The forces roiling the world’s biggest debt market show little sign of 
fading, as stubborn inflation and hawkish Federal Reserve rhetoric have traders 
bracing for more aggressive interest-rate hikes, while growing debt supply adds 
to the pressure. The selloff persisted even as turmoil around Nvidia rattled 
stocks and risk sentiment,undercutting hopes that Treasuries had reclaimed 
their traditional haven role

a three-year low.
