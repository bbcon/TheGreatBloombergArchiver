There’s a lot going on in India right now.

Over the weekend, the Reserve Bank of India unveiled some of Asia’s most 
stringent curbs 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-10-12/rbi-tightens-rupee-defense-as-pricey-crude-foreign-outflows-bite-usd-inr?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
 after the currency approached the record low near 97 per dollar. While the 
moves offered the rupee some reprieve Monday, sustaining the recovery may be 
harder as elevated oil prices and capital outflows weigh on the currency.

Still, India is defining its tolerance for rupee weakness 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/inr-usd-india-draws-line-in-sand-for-rupee-at-97-as-rbi-steps-up-defense?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
 with the central bank’s strongest currency defense in more than a decade
reinforcing 97 as the limit 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/inr-usd-india-draws-line-in-sand-for-rupee-at-97-as-rbi-steps-up-defense?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
. The rupee surged 1.2%,its biggest jump since April 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/indian-rupee-strengthens-in-offshore-market-after-rbi-measures?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
, to 95.5963 per dollar. Our reporterbreaks it down for us in this video 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-12/rupee-jumps-on-rbi-measures-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
.

Meanwhile, New Delhi is bracing for a third straight day of tensions 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/india-s-capital-braces-for-more-protests-after-mass-detentions?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
, with opposition parties on Monday planning a march to the Supreme Court and a 
youth-led movementpressing ahead with protests 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-11/india-s-cjp-vows-more-protests-after-mass-detentions-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
 over a contentious revision of voter rolls. That follows a weekend of mass 
detentions and a heavy security presence in Delhi.

Beyond the immediate headlines, there’s a steady drumbeat of change that’s 
lured Wall Street to ramp up bets on India 
<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-12/why-wall-street-bosses-are-betting-on-india-video?cmpid=eveapac&utm_campaign=eveapac&utm_medium=email&utm_source=newsletter&utm_term=261012&utm_content=9972>
