**Middle East Escalation Clouds Oil Outlook**
Lebanon's deadliest day of fighting in months — following an Israeli strike that killed a senior Hezbollah commander — has added pressure to stalled US-Iran talks, even as Iran and Oman reportedly edge closer to a deal on managing the Strait of Hormuz. Despite mounting vessel attacks, covert oil flows out of the Persian Gulf continue at full tilt, keeping crude contained; Brent nonetheless rose 1.2% on the day.

**Commodity Tightness and Trade Enforcement**
Copper markets show acute near-term tightness, with the LME spot-to-three-month spread at its widest since 2021, reflecting electrification- and AI-linked demand outpacing supply. Washington's new tariff regime on solar components — effective December 4 — targets circumvention exemplified by the 20,000-mile China-Kenya-Indonesia route, though it remains uncertain whether the measures will revive competitive US solar manufacturing after a decade of policy whipsaws.
