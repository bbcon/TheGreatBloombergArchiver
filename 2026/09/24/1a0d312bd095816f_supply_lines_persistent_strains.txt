US companies reported robust earnings in the past few weeks, outpacing 
analysts’ expectations by a near-record amount. The quarter would have been 
even stronger if it wasn’t for the supply constraints seeping through the 
economy.

Half of all public companies mentioned shortages in some capacity, according 
to theOrange Book <>, a report on the major themes discussed in 482 company 
earnings calls through this month from Bloomberg Economics and Bloomberg 
Intelligence.

A key part of this is a lack of specialized labor, especially truck drivers, 
with stricter federal government enforcement of commercial driver’s licenses. 
That’s in turn pushing up freight rates and also helping accelerate the move 
from truck to rail. Increased fuel costs amid tensions in the Middle East are 
also driving prices, companies reported.

days through Sept. 20, an increase of 9% from a year ago, according to data 
released by the Ministry of Transport.
